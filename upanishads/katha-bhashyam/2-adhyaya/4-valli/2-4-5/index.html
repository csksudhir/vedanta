    Kaṭha Upaniṣad 2.1.5

    ಮಂತ್ರ

    ಯ ಇಮಂ ಮಧ್ವದಂ ವೇದ ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ ಜೀವಮಂತಿಕಾತ್ । ಈಶಾನಂ ಭೂತಭವ್ಯಸ್ಯ ನ ತತೋ ವಿಜುಗುಪ್ಸತೇ । ಏತದ್ವೈ ತತ್ ॥ ೫ ॥
    He who knows this Self as the enjoyer of the fruits of actions, as the living principle dwelling near (in the body), and as the Lord of the past and the future, does not seek to protect himself from anything thereafter. Verily, this is That.

    ಮಂತ್ರ ಪದಾರ್ಥ

    ಯಃ ಇಮಂ ವೇದ

    Who knows this

    • ಯಃ → who
    • ಇಮಂ → this
    • ವೇದ → knows

    ಮಧ್ವದಂ

    The enjoyer of the fruits of actions

    • ಮಧು → sweetness, pleasant result
    • ಅದಂ → eater, enjoyer
    • ಮಧ್ವದಂ → enjoyer of the fruits (of karma)

    ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ

    As the Self

    • ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ → the Self

    ಜೀವಂ ಅಂತಿಕಾತ್

    As the living principle dwelling near

    • ಜೀವಂ → the living being, life-principle
    • ಅಂತಿಕಾತ್ → near, dwelling within the body

    ಈಶಾನಂ ಭೂತಭವ್ಯಸ್ಯ

    The Lord of the past and the future

    • ಈಶಾನಂ → ruler, lord
    • ಭೂತಸ್ಯ → of the past
    • ಭವ್ಯಸ್ಯ → of the future

    ನ ತತಃ ವಿಜುಗುಪ್ಸತೇ

    Thereafter he does not seek protection

    • ನ → not
    • ತತಃ → thereafter
    • ವಿಜುಗುಪ್ಸತೇ → seeks to protect himself, shrinks away in fear

    ಏತತ್ ವೈ ತತ್

    Verily, this is That

    • ಏತತ್ → this
    • ವೈ → indeed
    • ತತ್ → That (Brahman taught earlier)

    ಭಾಷ್ಯ

    ಕಿಂಚ, ಯಃ ಕಶ್ಚಿತ್ ಇಮಂ ಮಧ್ವದಂ ಕರ್ಮಫಲಭುಜಂ ಜೀವಂ ಪ್ರಾಣಾದಿಕಲಾಪಸ್ಯ ಧಾರಯಿತಾರಮಾತ್ಮಾನಂ ವೇದ ವಿಜಾನಾತಿ ಅಂತಿಕಾತ್ ಅಂತಿಕೇ ಸಮೀಪೇ ಈಶಾನಮ್ ಈಶಿತಾರಂ ಭೂತಭವ್ಯಸ್ಯ ಕಾಲತ್ರಯಸ್ಯ, ತತಃ ತದ್ವಿಜ್ಞಾನಾದೂರ್ಧ್ವಮಾತ್ಮಾನಂ ನ ವಿಜುಗುಪ್ಸತೇ ನ ಗೋಪಾಯಿತುಮಿಚ್ಛತಿ, ಅಭಯಪ್ರಾಪ್ತತ್ವಾತ್ । ಯಾವದ್ಧಿ ಭಯಮಧ್ಯಸ್ಥೋಽನಿತ್ಯಮಾತ್ಮಾನಂ ಮನ್ಯತೇ ತಾವದ್ಗೋಪಾಯಿತುಮಿಚ್ಛತ್ಯಾತ್ಮಾನಮ್ । ಯದಾ ತು ನಿತ್ಯಮದ್ವೈತಮಾತ್ಮಾನಂ ವಿಜಾನಾತಿ, ತದಾ ಕಃ ಕಿಂ ಕುತೋ ವಾ ಗೋಪಾಯಿತುಮಿಚ್ಛೇತ್ । ಏತದ್ವೈ ತದಿತಿ ಪೂರ್ವವತ್ ॥
    Further, whoever knows this Self as the enjoyer of the fruits of actions, as the living principle, as the sustainer of the aggregate of prāṇas and senses, as dwelling nearby in the body, and as the ruler of the past, present, and future — after gaining this knowledge, such a person no longer seeks to protect himself, for he has attained fearlessness. So long as a person remains subject to fear and regards the Self as impermanent, he seeks to protect himself. But when he realizes the Self as eternal and non-dual, then who would protect whom, from what, and for what reason? “Verily, this is That” — as explained before.

    ಭಾಷ್ಯ ಪದಾರ್ಥ

    ಕಿಂಚ

    Further

    • ಕಿಂಚ → moreover, further

    ಯಃ ಕಶ್ಚಿತ್ ಇಮಂ ಮಧ್ವದಂ

    Whoever knows this enjoyer of the fruits of action

    • ಯಃ ಕಶ್ಚಿತ್ → whoever
    • ಇಮಂ → this
    • ಮಧ್ವದಂ → enjoyer of the fruits of karma

    ಕರ್ಮಫಲಭುಜಂ ಜೀವಂ

    The jīva who experiences the fruits of actions

    • ಕರ್ಮ → action
    • ಫಲ → fruit, result
    • ಭುಜಂ → enjoyer
    • ಜೀವಂ → living being

    ಪ್ರಾಣಾದಿಕಲಾಪಸ್ಯ ಧಾರಯಿತಾರಂ

    The sustainer of the aggregate of prāṇas and senses

    • ಪ್ರಾಣಾದಿ → prāṇa and the rest
    • ಕಲಾಪಸ್ಯ → aggregate, collection
    • ಧಾರಯಿತಾರಂ → supporter, sustainer

    ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ ವೇದ ವಿಜಾನಾತಿ

    Knows and realizes as the Self

    • ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ → the Self
    • ವೇದ → knows
    • ವಿಜಾನಾತಿ → realizes

    ಅಂತಿಕಾತ್ ಅಂತಿಕೇ ಸಮೀಪೇ

    As dwelling nearby, within the body

    • ಅಂತಿಕಾತ್ → near
    • ಅಂತಿಕೇ → in proximity
    • ಸಮೀಪೇ → nearby

    ಈಶಾನಂ ಈಶಿತಾರಂ

    As the ruler and controller

    • ಈಶಾನಂ → ruler
    • ಈಶಿತಾರಂ → controller

    ಭೂತಭವ್ಯಸ್ಯ ಕಾಲತ್ರಯಸ್ಯ

    Of the three divisions of time

    • ಭೂತ → past
    • ಭವ್ಯ → future
    • ಕಾಲತ್ರಯಸ್ಯ → of the threefold time (past, present, future)

    ತತಃ ತದ್ವಿಜ್ಞಾನಾತ್ ಊರ್ಧ್ವಂ

    After realizing That

    • ತತಃ → thereafter
    • ತತ್-ವಿಜ್ಞಾನಾತ್ → from the knowledge of That
    • ಊರ್ಧ್ವಂ → afterwards

    ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ ನ ವಿಜುಗುಪ್ಸತೇ

    He does not seek to protect himself

    • ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ → himself
    • ನ → not
    • ವಿಜುಗುಪ್ಸತೇ → seeks to protect, guard

    ನ ಗೋಪಾಯಿತುಂ ಇಚ್ಛತಿ

    Nor does he desire preservation

    • ನ → not
    • ಗೋಪಾಯಿತುಂ → to protect, preserve
    • ಇಚ್ಛತಿ → desires

    ಅಭಯಪ್ರಾಪ್ತತ್ವಾತ್

    Because he has attained fearlessness

    • ಅಭಯ → fearlessness
    • ಪ್ರಾಪ್ತತ್ವಾತ್ → because of having attained

    ಯಾವತ್ ಹಿ ಭಯಮಧ್ಯಸ್ಥಃ

    So long as one remains in fear

    • ಯಾವತ್ → so long as
    • ಹಿ → indeed
    • ಭಯ-ಮಧ್ಯಸ್ಥಃ → situated amidst fear

    ಅನಿತ್ಯಂ ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ ಮನ್ಯತೇ

    He considers the Self to be impermanent

    • ಅನಿತ್ಯಂ → impermanent
    • ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ → the Self
    • ಮನ್ಯತೇ → thinks

    ತಾವತ್ ಗೋಪಾಯಿತುಂ ಇಚ್ಛತಿ ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ

    So long he seeks to protect himself

    • ತಾವತ್ → so long
    • ಗೋಪಾಯಿತುಂ → to protect
    • ಇಚ್ಛತಿ → desires
    • ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ → himself

    ಯದಾ ತು ನಿತ್ಯಂ ಅದ್ವೈತಂ ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ ವಿಜಾನಾತಿ

    But when he realizes the Self as eternal and non-dual

    • ಯದಾ → when
    • ತು → however
    • ನಿತ್ಯಂ → eternal
    • ಅದ್ವೈತಂ → non-dual
    • ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ → the Self
    • ವಿಜಾನಾತಿ → realizes

    ತದಾ ಕಃ ಕಿಂ ಕುತೋ ವಾ ಗೋಪಾಯಿತುಂ ಇಚ್ಛೇತ್

    Then who would protect what, and from whom?

    • ತದಾ → then
    • ಕಃ → who
    • ಕಿಂ → what
    • ಕುತಃ → from whom, from what
    • ವಾ → indeed
    • ಗೋಪಾಯಿತುಂ → to protect
    • ಇಚ್ಛೇತ್ → would desire

    ಏತದ್ವೈ ತತ್

    Verily, this is That

    • ಏತತ್ → this
    • ವೈ → indeed
    • ತತ್ → That (Brahman)

    ಇತಿ ಪೂರ್ವವತ್

    As explained before

    • ಇತಿ → thus
    • ಪೂರ್ವವತ್ → as before
