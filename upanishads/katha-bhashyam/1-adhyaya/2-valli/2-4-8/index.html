    Kaṭha Upaniṣad 2.1.8

    ಮಂತ್ರ

    ಅರಣ್ಯೋರ್ನಿರ್ಹಿತೋ ಜಾತವೇದಾ ಗರ್ಭ ಇವ ಸುಭೃತೋ ಗರ್ಭಿಣೀಭಿಃ । ದಿವೇ ದಿವ ಈಡ್ಯೋ ಜಾಗೃವದ್ಭಿರ್ಹವಿಷ್ಮದ್ಭಿರ್ಮನುಷ್ಯೇಭಿರಗ್ನಿಃ । ಏತದ್ವೈ ತತ್ ॥ ೮ ॥
    Agni, the all-knowing fire, lies hidden in the two araṇi sticks, carefully preserved like an embryo by pregnant women. Day after day, he is worshipped by vigilant men who offer oblations. Verily, this is That.

    ಮಂತ್ರ ಪದಾರ್ಥ

    ಅರಣ್ಯೋಃ ನಿರ್ಹಿತಃ ಜಾತವೇದಾಃ

    The all-knowing Fire hidden in the two araṇis

    • ಅರಣ್ಯೋಃ → in the two araṇi sticks
    • ನಿರ್ಹಿತಃ → hidden, placed within
    • ಜಾತವೇದಾಃ → Agni, the knower of all beings

    ಗರ್ಭಃ ಇವ ಸುಭೃತಃ

    Well protected like an embryo

    • ಗರ್ಭಃ → embryo, fetus
    • ಇವ → like
    • ಸುಭೃತಃ → carefully protected, well preserved

    ಗರ್ಭಿಣೀಭಿಃ

    By pregnant women

    • ಗರ್ಭಿಣೀಭಿಃ → by pregnant women

    ದಿವೇ ದಿವೇ

    Day after day

    • ದಿವೇ ದಿವೇ → every day

    ಈಡ್ಯಃ

    Worthy of worship

    • ಈಡ್ಯಃ → worthy of praise and worship

    ಜಾಗೃವದ್ಭಿಃ

    By vigilant persons

    • ಜಾಗೃವದ್ಭಿಃ → by those who are awake, alert

    ಹವಿಷ್ಮದ್ಭಿಃ ಮನುಷ್ಯೇಭಿಃ

    By men possessing sacrificial offerings

    • ಹವಿಷ್ಮದ್ಭಿಃ → possessing oblations
    • ಮನುಷ್ಯೇಭಿಃ → by human beings

    ಅಗ್ನಿಃ

    Agni

    • ಅಗ್ನಿಃ → Fire

    ಏತತ್ ವೈ ತತ್

    Verily, this is That

    • ಏತತ್ → this
    • ವೈ → indeed
    • ತತ್ → That (Brahman)

    ಭಾಷ್ಯ

    ಕಿಂಚ, ಯಃ ಅಧಿಯಜ್ಞಮ್ ಉತ್ತರಾಧರಾರಣ್ಯೋರ್ನಿಹಿತಃ ಸ್ಥಿತಃ ಜಾತವೇದಾ ಅಗ್ನಿಃ ಪುನಃ ಸರ್ವಹವಿಷಾಂ ಭೋಕ್ತಾ ಅಧ್ಯಾತ್ಮಂ ಚ ಯೋಗಿಭಿಃ, ಗರ್ಭ ಇವ ಗರ್ಭಿಣೀಭಿಃ ಅಂತರ್ವತ್ನೀಭಿರಗರ್ಹಿತಾನ್ನಭೋಜನಾದಿನಾ ಯಥಾ ಗರ್ಭಃ ಸುಭೃತಃ ಸುಷ್ಠು ಸಮ್ಯಗ್ಭೃತೋ ಲೋಕೇ, ಇತ್ಥಮೇವ ಋತ್ವಿಗ್ಭಿರ್ಯೋಗಿಭಿಶ್ಚ ಸುಭೃತ ಇತ್ಯೇತತ್ । ಕಿಂಚ, ದಿವೇ ದಿವೇ ಅಹನ್ಯಹನಿ ಈಡ್ಯಃ ಸ್ತುತ್ಯೋ ವಂದ್ಯಶ್ಚ ಕರ್ಮಿಭಿರ್ಯೋಗಿಭಿಶ್ಚಾಧ್ವರೇ ಹೃದಯೇ ಚ ಜಾಗೃವದ್ಭಿಃ ಜಾಗರಣಶೀಲೈಃ ಅಪ್ರಮತ್ತೈರಿತ್ಯೇತತ್ । ಹವಿಷ್ಮದ್ಭಿಃ ಆಜ್ಯಾದಿಮದ್ಭಿಃ ಧ್ಯಾನಭಾವನಾವದ್ಭಿಶ್ಚ ಮನುಷ್ಯೇಭಿಃ ಮನುಷ್ಯೈಃ ಅಗ್ನಿಃ ; ಏತದ್ವೈ ತತ್ ತದೇವ ಪ್ರಕೃತಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ ॥
    Further, that Agni, known as Jātavedas, who in the sacrificial context is hidden and established in the upper and lower araṇi sticks, and who is the enjoyer of all sacrificial offerings, is also realized inwardly by yogins. Just as an embryo is carefully nourished and protected by pregnant women through proper food and conduct, so too this Agni is carefully sustained by priests through ritual and by yogins through contemplation. Moreover, day after day he is worthy of praise and worship by ritualists in the sacrifice and by yogins in the heart. He is worshipped by vigilant and attentive persons who are free from negligence. He is served by people possessing offerings such as ghee and other sacrificial materials, and also by those endowed with meditation and contemplation. Verily, this Agni is that very Brahman which is the subject under discussion.

    ಭಾಷ್ಯ ಪದಾರ್ಥ

    ಕಿಂಚ

    Further

    • ಕಿಂಚ → moreover, further

    ಯಃ ಅಧಿಯಜ್ಞಮ್

    In the context of sacrifice

    • ಯಃ → who
    • ಅಧಿಯಜ್ಞಮ್ → with reference to sacrifice

    ಉತ್ತರಾಧರ ಅರಣ್ಯೋಃ ನಿಹಿತಃ ಸ್ಥಿತಃ

    Hidden and established in the upper and lower araṇis

    • ಉತ್ತರ → upper
    • ಅಧರ → lower
    • ಅರಣ್ಯೋಃ → in the two araṇi sticks
    • ನಿಹಿತಃ → placed, hidden
    • ಸ್ಥಿತಃ → abiding

    ಜಾತವೇದಾಃ ಅಗ್ನಿಃ

    Agni, the all-knowing fire

    • ಜಾತವೇದಾಃ → Jātavedas
    • ಅಗ್ನಿಃ → Agni

    ಸರ್ವಹವಿಷಾಂ ಭೋಕ್ತಾ

    The enjoyer of all oblations

    • ಸರ್ವ → all
    • ಹವಿಷಾಂ → of offerings
    • ಭೋಕ್ತಾ → enjoyer

    ಅಧ್ಯಾತ್ಮಂ ಚ ಯೋಗಿಭಿಃ

    And inwardly by yogins

    • ಅಧ್ಯಾತ್ಮಂ → inwardly, in the individual self
    • ಚ → and
    • ಯೋಗಿಭಿಃ → by yogins

    ಗರ್ಭ ಇವ ಗರ್ಭಿಣೀಭಿಃ

    Like an embryo by pregnant women

    • ಗರ್ಭಃ → embryo
    • ಇವ → like
    • ಗರ್ಭಿಣೀಭಿಃ → by pregnant women

    ಅಂತರ್ವತ್ನೀಭಿಃ

    By women carrying a child

    • ಅಂತರ್ವತ್ನೀಭಿಃ → pregnant women

    ಅಗರ್ಹಿತ ಅನ್ನ ಭೋಜನ ಆದಿನಾ

    Through proper food and conduct

    • ಅಗರ್ಹಿತ → blameless, proper
    • ಅನ್ನ → food
    • ಭೋಜನ → eating
    • ಆದಿನಾ → and the like

    ಯಥಾ ಗರ್ಭಃ ಸುಭೃತಃ

    Just as an embryo is well nourished

    • ಯಥಾ → just as
    • ಗರ್ಭಃ → embryo
    • ಸುಭೃತಃ → well sustained

    ಸುಷ್ಠು ಸಮ್ಯಕ್ ಭೃತಃ

    Carefully maintained

    • ಸುಷ್ಠು → well
    • ಸಮ್ಯಕ್ → properly
    • ಭೃತಃ → nourished

    ಇತ್ಥಮ್ ಏವ ಋತ್ವಿಗ್ಭಿಃ ಯೋಗಿಭಿಃ ಚ ಸುಭೃತಃ

    Likewise sustained by priests and yogins

    • ಇತ್ಥಮ್ ಏವ → in the same way
    • ಋತ್ವಿಗ್ಭಿಃ → by priests
    • ಯೋಗಿಭಿಃ → by yogins
    • ಚ → and
    • ಸುಭೃತಃ → carefully maintained

    ದಿವೇ ದಿವೇ ಅಹನಿ ಅಹನಿ

    Day after day

    • ದಿವೇ ದಿವೇ → day after day
    • ಅಹನಿ ಅಹನಿ → every day

    ಈಡ್ಯಃ ಸ್ತುತ್ಯಃ ವಂದ್ಯಃ ಚ

    Worthy of praise and worship

    • ಈಡ್ಯಃ → worthy of praise
    • ಸ್ತುತ್ಯಃ → praiseworthy
    • ವಂದ್ಯಃ → worthy of worship
    • ಚ → and

    ಕರ್ಮಿಭಿಃ ಯೋಗಿಭಿಃ ಚ

    By ritualists and yogins

    • ಕರ್ಮಿಭಿಃ → by ritual performers
    • ಯೋಗಿಭಿಃ → by yogins
    • ಚ → and

    ಅಧ್ವರೇ ಹೃದಯೇ ಚ

    In the sacrifice and in the heart

    • ಅಧ್ವರೇ → in the sacrifice
    • ಹೃದಯೇ → in the heart
    • ಚ → and

    ಜಾಗೃವದ್ಭಿಃ

    By vigilant persons

    • ಜಾಗೃವದ್ಭಿಃ → by the wakeful

    ಜಾಗರಣಶೀಲೈಃ ಅಪ್ರಮತ್ತೈಃ

    By alert and careful people

    • ಜಾಗರಣಶೀಲೈಃ → habitually alert
    • ಅಪ್ರಮತ್ತೈಃ → free from negligence

    ಹವಿಷ್ಮದ್ಭಿಃ ಆಜ್ಯಾದಿಮದ್ಭಿಃ

    By those possessing offerings such as ghee

    • ಹವಿಷ್ಮದ್ಭಿಃ → possessing sacrificial offerings
    • ಆಜ್ಯಾದಿಮದ್ಭಿಃ → possessing ghee and similar materials

    ಧ್ಯಾನಭಾವನಾವದ್ಭಿಃ ಚ

    And by those endowed with meditation

    • ಧ್ಯಾನ → meditation
    • ಭಾವನಾ → contemplation
    • ವದ್ಭಿಃ → possessing
    • ಚ → and

    ಮನುಷ್ಯೇಭಿಃ ಮನುಷ್ಯೈಃ

    By human beings

    • ಮನುಷ್ಯೇಭಿಃ → by human beings
    • ಮನುಷ್ಯೈಃ → by people

    ಅಗ್ನಿಃ

    Agni

    • ಅಗ್ನಿಃ → Agni

    ಏತದ್ವೈ ತತ್

    Verily, this is That

    • ಏತತ್ → this
    • ವೈ → indeed
    • ತತ್ → That

    ತದೇವ ಪ್ರಕೃತಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ

    That very Brahman under discussion

    • ತತ್ ಏವ → that very
    • ಪ್ರಕೃತಂ → presently being discussed
    • ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ → Brahman
