    ಪ್ರಶ್ನೋಪನಿಷತ್ — ದ್ವಿತೀಯ ಪ್ರಶ್ನ, ಮಂತ್ರ ೧

    Praśna Upaniṣad — Second Question, Verse 1

    Source of Ṭīkā: Ānandāśrama Sanskrit Series No. 8 (Ānandagiri on Śaṅkara Bhāṣya)

    ಮೂಲ (Mūla)

    ಅಥ ಹೈನಂ ಭಾರ್ಗವೋ ವೈದರ್ಭಿಃ ಪಪ್ರಚ್ಛ ಭಗವನ್ ಕತ್ಯೇವ ದೇವಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಂ ವಿಧಾರಯಂತೇ ।

    ಕತರ ಏತತ್ ಪ್ರಕಾಶಯಂತೇ ।

    ಕಃ ಪುನಃ ಏಷಾಂ ವರಿಷ್ಠ ಇತಿ ॥ ೧ ॥

    Overall Translation

    Then Bhārgava Vaidarbhi asked him (Pippalāda): "Revered Sir, how many gods indeed sustain the creature (body)?

    Which of them illuminate this?

    And who again is the foremost among them?"

    Word-to-Word

    ಅಥ — then / next · ಹ — indeed · ಏನಂ — him (Pippalāda) · ಭಾರ್ಗವಃ — Bhārgava (of the Bhṛgu lineage) · ವೈದರ್ಭಿಃ — Vaidarbhi (born in Vidarbha) · ಪಪ್ರಚ್ಛ — asked · ಭಗವನ್ — O Revered Sir · ಕತ್ಯೇವ — how many indeed · ದೇವಾಃ — gods / divine powers · ಪ್ರಜಾಂ — the creature / body · ವಿಧಾರಯಂತೇ — sustain / uphold ।

    ಕತರೇ — which of them · ಏತತ್ — this · ಪ್ರಕಾಶಯಂತೇ — illuminate / make manifest ।

    ಕಃ — who · ಪುನಃ — moreover / again · ಏಷಾಂ — among them · ವರಿಷ್ಠಃ — the foremost / the greatest · ಇತಿ — thus ॥

    ಭಾಷ್ಯ (Bhāṣya)

    ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ಅತ್ತಾ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ಇತಿ ಉಕ್ತಮ್ ।

    ತಸ್ಯ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿತ್ವಂ ಅತ್ತೃತ್ವಂ ಚ ಅಸ್ಮಿನ್ ಶರೀರೇ ಅವಧಾರಯಿತವ್ಯಂ ಇತಿ ಅಯಂ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಃ ಆರಭ್ಯತೇ ।

    ಅಥ ಅನಂತರಂ ಹ ಕಿಲ ಏನಂ ಭಾರ್ಗವಃ ವೈದರ್ಭಿಃ ಪಪ್ರಚ್ಛ — ಹೇ ಭಗವನ್ ಕತ್ಯೇವ ದೇವಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಂ ಶರೀರಲಕ್ಷಣಾಂ ವಿಧಾರಯಂತೇ ವಿಶೇಷೇಣ ಧಾರಯಂತೇ ।

    ಕತರೇ ಬುದ್ಧೀಂದ್ರಿಯ-ಕರ್ಮೇಂದ್ರಿಯ-ವಿಭಕ್ತಾನಾಂ ಏತತ್ ಪ್ರಕಾಶನಂ ಸ್ವಮಾಹಾತ್ಮ್ಯ-ಪ್ರಖ್ಯಾಪನಂ ಪ್ರಕಾಶಯಂತೇ ।

    ಕಃ ಅಸೌ ಪುನಃ ಏಷಾಂ ವರಿಷ್ಠಃ ಪ್ರಧಾನಃ ಕಾರ್ಯಕರಣ-ಲಕ್ಷಣಾನಾಂ ಇತಿ ॥

    Overall Translation

    It was said (in the first Praśna) that Prāṇa is the eater and Prajāpati. That very Prajāpati-nature and eater-nature of it is to be ascertained / established in this body — this is why this question begins.

    Then, next, Bhārgava Vaidarbhi indeed asked him: "O Revered Sir, how many gods indeed sustain — uphold in a specific manner — the creature that is characterised by the body?

    Which of them — among those divided into cognitive faculties (buddhīndriyas) and action faculties (karmendriyas) — illuminate this, meaning proclaim their own greatness?

    Who, moreover, is the foremost, the primary, among them — these that are characterised as effects and instruments?"

    Word-to-Word

    ಪ್ರಾಣಃ — Prāṇa · ಅತ್ತಾ — the eater · ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ — Prajāpati · ಇತಿ ಉಕ್ತಂ — thus was said (in the first Praśna) ।

    ತಸ್ಯ — of that (Prāṇa) · ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿತ್ವಂ — Prajāpati-nature · ಅತ್ತೃತ್ವಂ ಚ — and eater-nature · ಅಸ್ಮಿನ್ ಶರೀರೇ — in this body · ಅವಧಾರಯಿತವ್ಯಂ — is to be ascertained / established · ಇತಿ — thus · ಅಯಂ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಃ — this question · ಆರಭ್ಯತೇ — begins ।

    ಅಥ ಅನಂತರಂ — then / next · ಹ ಕಿಲ — indeed · ಏನಂ — him · ಭಾರ್ಗವಃ ವೈದರ್ಭಿಃ — Bhārgava Vaidarbhi · ಪಪ್ರಚ್ಛ — asked · ಹೇ ಭಗವನ್ — O Revered Sir · ಕತ್ಯೇವ — how many indeed · ದೇವಾಃ — gods / divine powers · ಪ್ರಜಾಂ — the creature · ಶರೀರಲಕ್ಷಣಾಂ — characterised by the body · ವಿಧಾರಯಂತೇ — sustain · ವಿಶೇಷೇಣ ಧಾರಯಂತೇ — uphold in a specific manner ।

    ಕತರೇ — which of them · ಬುದ್ಧೀಂದ್ರಿಯ-ಕರ್ಮೇಂದ್ರಿಯ-ವಿಭಕ್ತಾನಾಂ — among those divided into cognitive and action faculties · ಏತತ್ — this · ಪ್ರಕಾಶನಂ — illumination · ಸ್ವಮಾಹಾತ್ಮ್ಯ-ಪ್ರಖ್ಯಾಪನಂ — proclamation of their own greatness · ಪ್ರಕಾಶಯಂತೇ — illuminate / make manifest ।

    ಕಃ ಅಸೌ ಪುನಃ — who moreover · ಏಷಾಂ — among them · ವರಿಷ್ಠಃ — the foremost · ಪ್ರಧಾನಃ — the primary · ಕಾರ್ಯಕರಣ-ಲಕ್ಷಣಾನಾಂ — of those characterised as effects and instruments · ಇತಿ — thus ॥

    Anvaya (Prose Order of the Bhāṣya)

    The opening of the Bhāṣya requires anvaya — it connects the second Praśna to the first before launching the question itself.

    Sentence — The connection to the first Praśna and the purpose of this question

    As written:

    ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ಅತ್ತಾ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ಇತಿ ಉಕ್ತಮ್ ।

    ತಸ್ಯ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿತ್ವಂ ಅತ್ತೃತ್ವಂ ಚ ಅಸ್ಮಿನ್ ಶರೀರೇ ಅವಧಾರಯಿತವ್ಯಂ ಇತಿ ಅಯಂ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಃ ಆರಭ್ಯತೇ ।

    Anvaya (resolved prose order):

    [ಪ್ರಥಮಪ್ರಶ್ನೇ] ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ಅತ್ತಾ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ಇತಿ ಉಕ್ತಂ [ಆಸೀತ್] ।

    [ಕಿಂ ತು] ತಸ್ಯ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿತ್ವಂ ಅತ್ತೃತ್ವಂ ಚ ಅಸ್ಮಿನ್ ಶರೀರೇ [ಕಥಂ ವರ್ತತೇ ಇತಿ] ಅವಧಾರಯಿತವ್ಯಂ — ಇತ್ಯ-ಅರ್ಥಂ ಅಯಂ [ದ್ವಿತೀಯಃ] ಪ್ರಶ್ನಃ ಆರಭ್ಯತೇ ।

    English: In the first Praśna it was said that Prāṇa is the eater and Prajāpati. But how that Prajāpati-nature and eater-nature of Prāṇa operates in this body — that is to be ascertained. For this purpose this (second) question begins.

    Note: Śaṅkara explicitly connects the second Praśna to the first by citing its conclusion. Ānandagiri confirms this structural link and expands it — the second Praśna establishes Prāṇa's qualities (foremost-ness, eater-nature, Prajāpati- nature) within the body; the third Praśna then prescribes its upāsanā (meditation). These three Praśnas form a unit.

    ಟೀಕಾ (Ṭīkā)

    ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ಅತ್ತಾ ಇತಿ ।

    ಅವಧಾರಯಿತವ್ಯಂ ಇತಿ ।

    ಅತ್ತಾ ವಿಶ್ವಸ್ಯ ಸತ್ಪತಿಃ ಇತಿ ಅತ್ತೃತ್ವಸ್ಯ ಏಷಃ ಅಗ್ನಿಃ ತಪತಿ ಇತಿ ಆರಭ್ಯ ಅರಾ ಇವ ರಥನಾಭೌ ಪ್ರಾಣೇ ಸರ್ವಂ ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠಿತಂ ।

    ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ಚರಸಿ ಗರ್ಭೇ ತ್ವಮೇವ ಪ್ರತಿಜಾಯಸೇ ಇತಿ ಆದಿನಾ ಚ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿತ್ವಸ್ಯ ಉತ್ತರತ್ρ ವಕ್ಷ್ಯಮಾಣತ್ವಾತ್ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ।

    ಇದಂ ಉಪಲಕ್ಷಣಂ ಪೂರ್ವಂ ಗತಿಶ್ರವಣೇನ ವಿರಕ್ತಸ್ಯ ಅಪಿ ಚಿತ್ತ-ಏಕಾಗ್ರ್ಯಂ ವಿನಾ ವಕ್ಷ್ಯಮಾಣ-ಆತ್ಮಜ್ಞಾನ-ಅಸಿದ್ಧೇಃ ತದರ್ಥೇ ಮಂದಾನಾಂ ಫಲವಿಶೇಷಾರ್ಥಂ ಚ ಪ್ರಾಣೋಪಾಸನಾರ್ಥಂ ಪ್ರಶ್ನದ್ವಯ-ಆರಂಭಃ ।

    ತತ್ρ ಅಪಿ ಶ್ರೇಷ್ಠತ್ವ-ಅತ್ತೃತ್ವ-ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿತ್ವ-ಆದಿ-ಗುಣ-ನಿರ್ಧಾರಣಾರ್ಥಂ ದ್ವಿತೀಯಃ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಃ ।

    ತದ್-ಉತ್ಪತ್ತಿ-ಆದಿ-ನಿರ್ಧಾರಣ-ಪೂರ್ವಕಂ ತದ್-ಉಪಾಸನ-ವಿಧಾನ-ಅರ್ಥಂ ತೃತೀಯಃ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಃ ಇತಿ ಅಪಿ ದ್ರಷ್ಟವ್ಯಂ ।

    ಪ್ರಜಾಶಬ್ದೇನ ಶರೀರಂ ಏವ ಗೃಹ್ಯತೇ ನ ಜೀವಃ ತಸ್ಯ ಪ್ರಾಣಧಾರಕತ್ವೇನ ತದ್ಧಾರ್ಯತ್ವ-ಅಭಾವಾತ್ ಇತ್ಯಾಹ – ಶರೀರ ಇತಿ ।

    ವಿಭಕ್ತಾನಾಂ ಇತಿ ನಿರ್ಧಾರಣೇ ಷಷ್ಠೀ ।

    ಸ್ವಮಾಹಾತ್ಮ್ಯ-ಖ್ಯಾಪನಂ ಇತಿ ।

    ಅವಕಾಶದಾನ-ಆದಿಕಂ ಆಕಾಶ-ಆದೀನಾಂ ಮಾಹಾತ್ಮ್ಯಂ ತಸ್ಯ ಖ್ಯಾಪನಂ ತಸ್ಯ ಲೋಕಾನ್ ಪ್ರತಿ ಪ್ರಕಟನಂ ತತ್ ಪ್ರಕಾಶಯಂತೇ ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ।

    ಪಾಕಂ ಪಚತಿ ಇತಿವತ್ ।

    ಅವಕಾಶದಾನ-ಆದಿಕಂ ಸ್ವಸ್ವಕಾರ್ಯಂ ಸರ್ವಲೋಕ-ಪ್ರಕಟನಂ ಯಥಾ ತಥಾ ಕತರೇ ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ॥ ೧ ॥

    Overall Translation

    Ānandagiri opens by connecting the second Praśna to the first — with the words "prāṇo'ttā" (Prāṇa is the eater). He then explains "avādhārayitavyam" (is to be ascertained): the eater-nature was established in the first Praśna through the statement "the eater, the lord of all that exists"; and the Prajāpati-nature will be established in this Praśna through the passage beginning "this fire heats" and ending "all is established in Prāṇa like spokes in a hub" — and also through "O Prajāpati, you move in the womb, you are born again" and so on.

    Ānandagiri then provides the structural purpose of the second and third Praśnas as a unit: this (the mention of "avādhārayitavyam") is an upalakaṣaṇa (indicative) pointing to the larger purpose. Even for one who has become dispassionate through hearing about the two paths (in the first Praśna), without single-pointedness of mind (cittaikāgrya) the knowledge of the Self to be stated (in the subsequent Praśnas) cannot be attained. Therefore, for those of lesser capacity, and for the purpose of a special fruit, and for the purpose of the upāsanā (meditation) of Prāṇa — the two questions (second and third Praśnas) are begun.

    Within that: the second Praśna is for the purpose of determining the qualities of Prāṇa — its supremacy, its eater-nature, its Prajāpati-nature, and so on. The third Praśna is for the purpose of prescribing its upāsanā — preceded by the determination of its origin and the like. This too should be understood.

    On the word "prajā" (creature): Ānandagiri states that by the word "prajā" only the body is meant — not the jīva (individual soul). This is because the jīva is itself the upholder of the prāṇas and therefore cannot be what is upheld (dhārya). This is stated with the word "śarīra" (body).

    On "vibhaktānām": the genitive (ṣaṣṭhī) here is in the sense of determination (nirdhāraṇa) — "which among those divided."

    On "svamāhātmya-khyāpanam" (proclamation of their own greatness): Ānandagiri explains — the greatness of ākāśa and the like consists of things such as giving space (avakāśadāna). The proclamation of that greatness is its manifestation to the world — they "illuminate" or "make manifest" — they perform this, is the meaning. It is like the expression "he cooks the cooking" (pākaṃ pacati) — an emphatic cognate construction. The meaning is: which of them make manifest their own respective functions — such as giving space and the like — to all the worlds?

    Word-to-Word

    ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ಅತ್ತಾ ಇತಿ — on the words "prāṇo'ttā (Prāṇa is the eater)" ।

    ಅವಧಾರಯಿತವ್ಯಂ ಇತಿ — on the word "avādhārayitavyam (is to be ascertained)" ।

    ಅತ್ತಾ ವಿಶ್ವಸ್ಯ ಸತ್ಪತಿಃ ಇತಿ — "the eater, the lord of all that exists" (Śruti citation for eater-nature) · ಅತ್ತೃತ್ವಸ್ಯ — of the eater-nature · ಏಷಃ ಅಗ್ನಿಃ ತಪತಿ ಇತಿ ಆರಭ್ಯ — beginning from "this fire heats" · ಅರಾ ಇವ ರಥನಾಭೌ ಪ್ರಾಣೇ ಸರ್ವಂ ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠಿತಂ — "all is established in Prāṇa like spokes in the hub of a wheel" (Śruti citation) ।

    ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ಚರಸಿ ಗರ್ಭೇ — "O Prajāpati, you move in the womb" · ತ್ವಮೇವ ಪ್ರತಿಜಾಯಸೇ — "you yourself are born again" (Śruti citation for Prajāpati-nature) · ಇತಿ ಆದಿನಾ ಚ — and by these and the like · ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿತ್ವಸ್ಯ — of the Prajāpati-nature · ಉತ್ತರತ್ρ — further on (in this Praśna) · ವಕ್ಷ್ಯಮಾಣತ್ವಾತ್ — because it will be stated · ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

    ಇದಂ — this · ಉಪಲಕ್ಷಣಂ — is an indicative / also stands for · ಪೂರ್ವಂ — previously · ಗತಿಶ್ರವಣೇನ — through hearing about the paths (in the first Praśna) · ವಿರಕ್ತಸ್ಯ ಅಪಿ — even of one who has become dispassionate · ಚಿತ್ತ-ಏಕಾಗ್ರ್ಯಂ ವಿನಾ — without single-pointedness of mind · ವಕ್ಷ್ಯಮಾಣ-ಆತ್ಮಜ್ಞಾನ-ಅಸಿದ್ಧೇಃ — because the Self-knowledge to be stated cannot be attained · ತದರ್ಥೇ — for that purpose · ಮಂದಾನಾಂ — for those of lesser capacity · ಫಲವಿಶೇಷಾರ್ಥಂ ಚ — and for the purpose of a special fruit · ಪ್ರಾಣೋಪಾಸನಾರ್ಥಂ — for the purpose of the upāsanā of Prāṇa · ಪ್ರಶ್ನದ್ವಯ-ಆರಂಭಃ — the beginning of the two questions (Praśnas 2 and 3) ।

    ತತ್ρ ಅಪಿ — and within that · ಶ್ರೇಷ್ಠತ್ವ-ಅತ್ತೃತ್ವ-ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿತ್ವ-ಆದಿ-ಗುಣ-ನಿರ್ಧಾರಣಾರ್ಥಂ — for the purpose of determining the qualities of supremacy, eater-nature, Prajāpati-nature and so on · ದ್ವಿತೀಯಃ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಃ — the second Praśna ।

    ತದ್-ಉತ್ಪತ್ತಿ-ಆದಿ-ನಿರ್ಧಾರಣ-ಪೂರ್ವಕಂ — preceded by the determination of its origin and the like · ತದ್-ಉಪಾಸನ-ವಿಧಾನ-ಅರ್ಥಂ — for the purpose of prescribing its upāsanā · ತೃತೀಯಃ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಃ — the third Praśna · ಇತಿ ಅಪಿ ದ್ರಷ್ಟವ್ಯಂ — this too should be understood ।

    ಪ್ರಜಾಶಬ್ದೇನ — by the word "prajā (creature)" · ಶರೀರಂ ಏವ ಗೃಹ್ಯತೇ — only the body is meant · ನ ಜೀವಃ — not the jīva · ತಸ್ಯ — because the jīva · ಪ್ರಾಣಧಾರಕತ್ವೇನ — being itself the upholder of the prāṇas · ತದ್ಧಾರ್ಯತ್ವ-ಅಭಾವಾತ್ — because of the absence of the quality of being upheld (by the gods) · ಇತ್ಯಾಹ — states · ಶರೀರ ಇತಿ — with the word "śarīra (body)" ।

    ವಿಭಕ್ತಾನಾಂ ಇತಿ — on the word "vibhaktānām (among those divided)" · ನಿರ್ಧಾರಣೇ ಷಷ್ಠೀ — the genitive is in the sense of determination ।

    ಸ್ವಮಾಹಾತ್ಮ್ಯ-ಖ್ಯಾಪನಂ ಇತಿ — on the phrase "svamāhātmya-khyāpanam (proclamation of their own greatness)" ।

    ಅವಕಾಶದಾನ-ಆದಿಕಂ — giving space and the like · ಆಕಾಶ-ಆದೀನಾಂ — of ākāśa and the like · ಮಾಹಾತ್ಮ್ಯಂ — the greatness · ತಸ್ಯ ಖ್ಯಾಪನಂ — the proclamation of that greatness · ತಸ್ಯ — of it · ಲೋಕಾನ್ ಪ್ರತಿ ಪ್ರಕಟನಂ — manifestation to the world · ತತ್ ಪ್ರಕಾಶಯಂತೇ — they illuminate / make manifest · ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ — they perform · ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

    ಪಾಕಂ ಪಚತಿ ಇತಿವತ್ — like the expression "he cooks the cooking" (cognate accusative construction) ।

    Summary of Mūla, Bhāṣya, and Ṭīkā

    Text Author Function
    ಮೂಲ (Mūla) Upaniṣad (Atharva-veda tradition) Bhārgava Vaidarbhi poses three questions — how many gods sustain the body, which illuminate it, and who is foremost among them
    ಭಾṣya (Bhāṣya) Śaṅkarācārya (8th c. CE) Connects the second Praśna to the first (Prāṇa = eater = Prajāpati); interprets "prajā" as body not jīva; glosses "vidhārayante" as specific upholding; explains "prakāśayante" as proclamation of greatness; "variṣṭha" as foremost of effects-and-instruments
    ಟೀಕಾ (Ṭīkā) Ānandagiri (13th c. CE) Provides the structural framework for Praśnas 2 and 3 as a unit for prāṇopāsanā; distinguishes second Praśna (determining Prāṇa's qualities) from third (prescribing its upāsanā); explains prajā = body not jīva; glosses prakāśayante as cognate construction; identifies nidhāraṇa-ṣaṣṭhī

    Core Philosophical Thread

    1. The second Praśna begins where the first ended — Prāṇa was established as the eater and Prajāpati in the first Praśna; now those qualities are to be ascertained within the body specifically.
    2. Three questions in one verse — how many sustain, which illuminate, who is foremost. These three will be answered progressively through the second Praśna, culminating in the identification of Prāṇa as the foremost.
    3. Praśnas 2 and 3 form a unit — Ānandagiri's structural observation is the most important contribution of this Ṭīkā: the second Praśna determines Prāṇa's qualities (foremost, eater, Prajāpati); the third prescribes its upāsanā. Both serve those who need cittaikāgrya (single-pointedness) before Self-knowledge.
    4. Prajā = body, not jīva — Ānandagiri's precision here is important. The jīva upholds the prāṇas and cannot itself be upheld by the gods. Only the body is upheld (dhārya). This prevents a misreading of the verse as asking about the support of the individual soul.
    5. Svamāhātmya-khyāpana as cognate construction — the expression "they illuminate their own greatness" is like "he cooks the cooking" — the object is implicit in the verb. Ānandagiri's grammatical note makes the meaning precise: each faculty manifests its own specific function to all the world.
