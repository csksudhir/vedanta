    ಪ್ರಶ್ನೋಪನಿಷತ್ — ಪ್ರಥಮ ಪ್ರಶ್ನ, ಮಂತ್ರ ೧೧

    Praśna Upaniṣad — First Question, Verse 11

    Source of Ṭīkā: Ānandāśrama Sanskrit Series No. 8 (Ānandagiri on Śaṅkara Bhāṣya)

    ಮೂಲ (Mūla)

    ಪಂಚಪಾದಂ ಪಿತರಂ ದ್ವಾದಶಾಕೃತಿಂ ದಿವ ಆಹುಃ ಪರೇ ಅರ್ಧೇ ಪುರೀಷಿಣಮ್ ।

    ಅಥೇಮೇ ಅನ್ಯ ಉ ಪರೇ ವಿಚಕ್ಷಣಂ ಸಪ್ತಚಕ್ರೇ ಷಡರ ಆಹುರರ್ಪಿತಮಿತಿ ॥ ೧೧ ॥

    Overall Translation

    Some knowers of Time call him (the Year / Prajāpati) five-footed, the Father, twelve-formed, full of water (pūrīṣin) — placed in the higher half, beyond the sky.

    And then these others, equally learned, call him the all-discerning one — placed (as the entire world) in the wheel of seven horses, having six spokes.

    Word-to-Word

    ಪಂಚಪಾದಂ — five-footed (having five seasons as feet) · ಪಿತರಂ — the Father (progenitor of all) · ದ್ವಾದಶಾಕೃತಿಂ — twelve-formed (having twelve months as forms/limbs) · ದಿವಃ — from / beyond the sky · ಪರೇ — in the higher · ಅರ್ಧೇ — half / region · ಪುರೀಷಿಣಂ — full of water / nourishment · ಆಹುಃ — they call / say ।

    ಅಥ — and now / moreover · ಇಮೇ — these · ಅನ್ಯೇ — others · ಉ — also / but (particle) · ಪರೇ — other (equally learned) knowers · ವಿಚಕ್ಷಣಂ — all-discerning / omniscient · ಸಪ್ತಚಕ್ರೇ — in the wheel of seven (horses / spokes) · ಷಡರೇ — having six spokes (six seasons) · ಆಹುಃ — they say / describe · ಅರ್ಪಿತಂ — placed / fixed (as spokes in a hub) · ಇತಿ — thus ॥

    ಭಾಷ್ಯ (Bhāṣya)

    ಪಂಚಪಾದಂ ಪಂಚ ಋತವಃ ಪಾದಾ ಇವಾಸ್ಯ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಾತ್ಮನ ಆದಿತ್ಯಸ್ಯ, ತೈರ್ಹ್ಯಸೌ ಪಾದೈರಿವ ಋತುಭಿರಾವರ್ತತೇ ।

    ಹೇಮಂತಶಿಶಿರಾವೇಕೀಕೃತ್ಯ ಇಯಂ ಕಲ್ಪನಾ ।

    ಪಿತರಂ ಸರ್ವಸ್ಯ ಜನಯಿತೃತ್ವಾತ್ಪಿತೃತ್ವಂ ತಸ್ಯ ; ದ್ವಾದಶಾಕೃತಿಂ ದ್ವಾದಶ ಮಾಸಾ ಆಕೃತಯೋಽವಯವಾ ಆಕರಣಂ ವಾ ಅವಯವಿಕರಣಮಸ್ಯ ದ್ವಾದಶಮಾಸೈಃ ತಂ ದ್ವಾದಶಾಕೃತಿಮ್ ।

    ದಿವಃ ದ್ಯುಲೋಕಾತ್ ಪರೇ ಊರ್ಧ್ವೇ ಅರ್ಧೇ ಸ್ಥಾನೇ ತೃತೀಯಸ್ಯಾಂ ದಿವೀತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ; ಪುರೀಷಿಣಂ ಪುರೀಷವಂತಮ್ ಉದಕವಂತಮ್ ಆಹುಃ ಕಾಲವಿದಃ ।

    ಅಥ ತಮೇವಾನ್ಯೇ ಇಮೇ ಉ ಪರೇ ಕಾಲವಿದಃ ವಿಚಕ್ಷಣಂ ನಿಪುಣಂ ಸರ್ವಜ್ಞಂ ಸಪ್ತಚಕ್ರೇ ಸಪ್ತಹಯರೂಪೇ ಚಕ್ರೇ ಸಂತತಗತಿಮತಿ ಕಾಲಾತ್ಮನಿ ಷಡರೇ ಷಡೃತುಮತಿ ಆಹುಃ ಸರ್ವಮಿದಂ ಜಗತ್ಕಥಯಂತಿ — ಅರ್ಪಿತಮ್ ಅರಾ ಇವ ರಥನಾಭೌ ನಿವಿಷ್ಟಮಿತಿ ।

    ಯದಿ ಪಂಚಪಾದೋ ದ್ವಾದಶಾಕೃತಿರ್ಯದಿ ವಾ ಸಪ್ತಚಕ್ರಃ ಷಡರಃ ಸರ್ವಥಾಪಿ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಃ ಕಾಲಾತ್ಮಾ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಶ್ಚಂದ್ರಾದಿತ್ಯಲಕ್ಷಣೋ ಜಗತಃ ಕಾರಣಮ್ ॥

    Overall Translation

    Five-footed: the five seasons are like the five feet of the Year-natured Sun (Āditya); for by them — by the seasons, as if by feet — he moves / revolves. This reckoning is made by treating Hemanta (early winter) and Śiśira (late winter) as one.

    The Father: his fatherhood comes from being the generator of all. Twelve-formed: the twelve months are his forms / limbs — or alternatively, the twelve months are the instruments by which he is constituted as a whole. Thus he is twelve-formed.

    Beyond the sky: he is in the higher, upward region — in the third heaven (beyond the sky-world). Full of water (pūrīṣiṇ): full of nourishment, full of water. Thus the knowers of Time describe him.

    And now these other equally learned knowers of Time describe that same one as all-discerning, omniscient — placed in the wheel of seven horses (seven-spoked), which has continuous motion and is of the nature of Time — in the six-spoked (six-seasoned) one. They say all this world is placed in him — fixed like spokes in the hub of a chariot-wheel.

    Whether five-footed and twelve-formed, or seven-wheeled and six-spoked — in every way the Year, whose nature is Time, is Prajāpati characterised by Moon and Sun, and is the cause of the world.

    Anvaya (Prose Order of the Bhāṣya)

    This verse has one sentence requiring explicit anvaya treatment, flagged by Ānandagiri, and one compound requiring grammatical disambiguation.

    Sentence 1 — The second school of knowers (the "saptacakra" view)

    As written:

    ಅಥ ತಮೇವ ಅನ್ಯೇ ಇಮೇ ಉ ಪರೇ ಕಾಲವಿದಃ ವಿಚಕ್ಷಣಂ ನಿಪುಣಂ ಸರ್ವಜ್ಞಂ ಸಪ್ತಚಕ್ರೇ ಸಪ್ತಹಯರೂಪೇ ಚಕ್ರೇ ಸಂತತಗತಿಮತಿ ಕಾಲಾತ್ಮನಿ ಷಡರೇ ಷಡೃತುಮತಿ ಆಹುಃ ಸರ್ವಮಿದಂ ಜಗತ್ಕಥಯಂತಿ — ಅರ್ಪಿತಮ್ ।

    Anvaya (resolved prose order):

    ಅಥ [ಪರೇ ಕಾಲವಿದಃ] ಇಮೇ ಅನ್ಯೇ ಉ ಪರೇ [ಏವ] — [ತೇ] ತಂ ಏವ [ಸಂವತ್ಸರಂ] ವಿಚಕ್ಷಣಂ [ಇತಿ] ಆಹುಃ ।

    [ಕಿಂ ಚ ?] ಸರ್ವಂ ಇದಂ ಜಗತ್ ಸಪ್ತಚಕ್ರೇ ಷಡರೇ [ಕಾಲಾತ್ಮನಿ] ಅರಾ ಇವ ರಥನಾಭೌ ಅರ್ಪಿತಂ [ಇತಿ] ಕಥಯಂತಿ ।

    English: And now these other equally learned knowers of Time — they call that very same (Year) all-discerning. And moreover they say that all this world is fixed in the seven-wheeled, six-spoked (Year / Time), like spokes in the hub of a wheel.

    Note: The particle "u" (ಉ) is glossed by Ānandagiri as equivalent to "tu" — marking a contrast or addition between the two schools of knowers. The two verbs ಆಹುಃ and ಕಥಯಂತಿ are near-synonyms here — Ānandagiri treats them as a hendiadys expressing the one act of description. The locatives ಸಪ್ತಚಕ್ರೇ and ಷಡರೇ are both predicates of ಅರ್ಪಿತಂ, not separate finite clauses.

    Compound disambiguation — ದ್ವಾದಶಾಕೃತಿ

    Śaṅkara offers two grammatical analyses of this compound:

    Analysis 1 (samānādhikaraṇa bahuvrīhi): The twelve months are his forms / limbs (ākṛtayaḥ = avayavāḥ) — he who has twelve months as his forms.

    Analysis 2 (vyādhikaraṇa bahuvrīhi): The twelve months are the instruments of his constitution (ākaraṇam = avayavī-karaṇam) — he who is constituted by / through twelve months as his limbs. In both analyses the referent and the final meaning are the same: the Year whose body is made of twelve months.

    ಟೀಕಾ (Ṭīkā)

    ಇಯಂ ಕಲ್ಪನಾ ಇತಿ ।

    ಪಂಚಧಾ ಕರ್ಮಸಾಧ್ಯಚಂದ್ರವೈಲಕ್ಷಣ್ಯಕಲ್ಪನಾ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ।

    ಜನಯಿತೃತ್ವಾದಿತಿ ।

    ಸಂವತ್ಸರಾತ್ಮಕಕಾಲಸ್ಯ ಸರ್ವಜನಕತ್ವಾದಿತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ।

    ಸಮಾನಾಧಿಕರಣಬಹುವ್ರೀಹಿತಯಾ ವ್ಯಾಖ್ಯಾಯ ವ್ಯಾಧಿಕರಣಬಹುವ್ರೀಹಿರ್ವೇತ್ಯಾಹ – ಆಕರಣಂ ವೇತಿ ।

    ಅವಯವಿಕರಣಮಿತಿ ।

    ಪಕ್ಷದ್ವಯೇಽಪಿ ಗುಣಭೇದೇನ ಕಲ್ಪನಾಭೇದೇನ ಚ ಭೇದಃ ನ ಧರ್ಮಿಭೇದಃ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ॥ ೧೧ ॥

    Overall Translation

    On the phrase "this reckoning" (iyaṃ kalpanā): Ānandagiri explains this means the five-fold reckoning — the convention that distinguishes the Sun (Year) from the action-produced Moon by casting the seasons as its five feet.

    On "because of being the generator" (janayitṛtvāt): Ānandagiri clarifies that the meaning is — because the Year-natured Time is the progenitor of all (sarvajanakatva).

    On the compound "dvādaśākṛti" — Śaṅkara first analyses it as a samānādhikaraṇa bahuvrīhi (appositional compound), then offers a vyādhikaraṇa bahuvrīhi (non-appositional compound) as an alternative — with the words "ākaraṇaṃ vā" (or alternatively, the instrument of constitution). On the second analysis, "ākaraṇam" means "avayavī-karaṇam" — being constituted through its limbs. In both analyses, the meaning is the same.

    On "dyulokāt" (from the sky-world): Ānandagiri explains this means from the sky-world that is of the nature of the ākāśa (space) — i.e., the antarikṣa (mid-region). Otherwise, if it meant from the svarga (heaven), then "beyond it" would make the Sun the fourth (world), which would contradict the statement "in the third heaven" — hence an anvaya-fault would result.

    On "udakavantam" (full of water): Ānandagiri explains this rests on the Smṛti statement — "from the Sun rain is born" — hence the Sun is the source of water and is called water-full.

    On the syntactic connection of "anye" (others): the word "anye" (in the second half) connects with the verb "āhuḥ" from the first half of the verse. The particle "u" is glossed as a nipāta equivalent in meaning to "tu" (but / moreover). The syntactic connection (anvaya) is: "the others (pāre), however, call that same one all-discerning (vicakṣaṇam)."

    To the question "what do they call it?" — Ānandagiri answers: with the words "saptacakra." The meaning is: in that all-discerning one of the nature of the seven-wheeled etc., all this world is said to be fixed (arpitam).

    On the question of what exactly differs between the two views — Ānandagiri answers with "yadi" (whether). In the first view (pūrvamata), the Year as Time is described through the Sun-nature — by the convention of the seasons as feet and the months as limbs. In the second view, Hemanta and Śiśira are separated out, the six seasons are treated as spokes (aras), the Year is treated as a wheel by virtue of its quality of revolution (parivartana), and that same Year is described with Time as primary and with the quality of being the support of all (sarvāśrayatva). In both views the difference is only in the quality (guṇa) and in the convention (kalpanā) — there is no difference in the subject (dharmin) itself.

    ಇಯಂ ಕಲ್ಪನಾ ಇತಿ — on the phrase "this reckoning / convention" ।

    ಜನಯಿತೃತ್ವಾತ್ ಇತಿ — on the phrase "because of being the generator" ।

    ಸಂವತ್ಸರಾತ್ಮಕ-ಕಾಲಸ್ಯ — of the Year-natured Time · ಸರ್ವಜನಕತ್ವಾತ್ — because of being the progenitor of all · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

    ಸಮಾನಾಧಿಕರಣ-ಬಹುವ್ರೀಹಿ-ತಯಾ — as a samānādhikaraṇa bahuvrīhi (appositional compound) · ವ್ಯಾಖ್ಯಾಯ — having explained / analysed · ವ್ಯಾಧಿಕರಣ-ಬಹುವ್ರೀಹಿಃ ವಾ — or alternatively a vyādhikaraṇa bahuvrīhi (non-appositional compound) · ಇತಿ ಆಹ — thus states · ಆಕರಣಂ ವಾ ಇತಿ — with the words "or the instrument of constitution" ।

    ಅವಯವಿಕರಣಂ ಇತಿ — on the word "avayavī-karaṇam" ।

    ಅವಯವಿತ್ವೇನ ಕರಣಂ — being constituted through its parts / by functioning as a limb-whole · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

    ಪಕ್ಷದ್ವಯೇ ಅಪಿ — in both analyses / both alternatives · ಏಕಃ ಏವ ಅರ್ಥಃ — the meaning is one and the same ।

    ದ್ಯುಲೋಕಾತ್ ಇತಿ — on the word "dyulokāt (from the sky-world)" ।

    ಆಕಾಶರೂಪಾತ್ — from that which is of the nature of ākāśa (space) · ಅಂತರಿಕ್ಷಲೋಕಾತ್ — from the mid-region (antarikṣa) · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

    ಅನ್ಯಥಾ — otherwise · ಸ್ವರ್ಗಲೋಕಾತ್ ಪರಸ್ಯ — of what is beyond the svarga (heaven) · ಚತುರ್ಥತ್ವೇನ — as the fourth (world) · ತೃತೀಯಸ್ಯಾಂ ಇತಿ — the statement "in the third (heaven)" · ಅನನ್ವಯ-ಆಪತ್ತೇಃ — because of the resulting anvaya-fault (syntactic inconsistency) ।

    ಉದಕವಂತಂ ಇತಿ — on the word "udakavantam (full of water)" ।

    ಆದಿತ್ಯಾತ್ ಜಾಯತೇ ವೃಷ್ಟಿಃ — from the Sun rain is born · ಇತಿ ಸ್ಮೃತೇಃ — because of this Smṛti statement · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

    ಅನ್ಯ ಇತಿ ಅಸ್ಯ — of the word "anye (others)" · ಪೂರ್ವಾರ್ಧಗತೇನ — occurring in the first half (of the verse) · ಆಹುಃ ಇತಿ ಅನೇನ — with the verb "āhuḥ (they say)" · ಸಂಬಂಧಃ — is the syntactic connection ।

    ಉ ಇತಿ — the particle "u" · ತುಶಬ್ದ-ಸಮಾನಾರ್ಥಃ — equivalent in meaning to "tu (but / moreover)" · ನಿಪಾತಃ — is an indeclinable particle ।

    ಪರೇ ತು — the others however · ತಂ ಏವ — that very same (one) · ವಿಚಕ್ಷಣಂ — as all-discerning · ಆಹುಃ — call / say · ಇತಿ ಅನ್ವಯಃ — thus is the syntactic connection ।

    ಕಿಂ ಆಹುಃ — what do they say? · ಇತ್ಯತಃ ಆಹ — to this (Ānandagiri) answers · ಸಪ್ತಚಕ್ರ ಇತಿ — with the word "saptacakra (seven-wheeled)" ।

    ತಸ್ಮಿನ್ — in that · ವಿಚಕ್ಷಣೇ — all-discerning one · ಸಪ್ತಚಕ್ರ-ಆದ್ಯಾತ್ಮಕೇ — of the nature of the seven-wheeled etc. · ಸರ್ವಂ ಇದಂ ಜಗತ್ — all this world · ಅರ್ಪಿತಂ — is placed / fixed · ಇತಿ ಆಹುಃ — they say · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

    ಮತದ್ವಯೇ ಅಪಿ — even in both views / schools · ಕೀದೃಶಃ — what kind of · ಅರ್ಥಭೇದಃ — difference in meaning · ಇತ್ಯತಃ ಆಹ — to this (Ānandagiri) answers · ಯದಿ ಇತಿ — with the word "yadi (whether)" ।

    ಪೂರ್ವಮತೇ — in the first view · ಋತೂನಾಂ — of the seasons · ಪಾದತ್ವ-ಕಲ್ಪನಯಾ — through the convention of treating them as feet · ಮಾಸಾನಾಂ — of the months · ಅವಯವತ್ವ-ಕಲ್ಪನಯಾ — through the convention of treating them as limbs · ಆದಿತ್ಯಾತ್ಮನಾ — through the Sun-nature · ಸಂವತ್ಸರಃ — the Year · ಕಾಲಃ ಏವ — Time itself · ಉಕ್ತಃ — is described ।

    ದ್ವಿತೀಯೇ ತು — in the second view however · ಹೇಮಂತಶಿಶಿರೌ — Hemanta and Śiśira (the two winter seasons) · ಪೃಥಕ್ಕೃತ್ಯ — having separated out · ಷಣ್ಣಾಂ ಋತೂನಾಂ — of the six seasons · ಅರತ್ವ-ಕಲ್ಪನಯಾ — through the convention of treating them as spokes · ಸಂವತ್ಸರಸ್ಯ — of the Year · ಪರಿವರ್ತನ-ಗುಣ-ಯೋಗೇನ — through the quality of revolution / rotation · ಚಕ್ರತ್ವ-ಕಲ್ಪನಯಾ — through the convention of treating it as a wheel · ಕಾಲ-ಪ್ರಾಧಾನ್ಯೇನ — with Time as primary · ಸರ್ವಾಶ್ರಯತ್ವೇನ ಚ — and with the quality of being the support of all · ಸಃ ಏವ — that very same (Year) · ಉಕ್ತಃ — is described ।

    ಪಕ್ಷದ್ವಯೇ ಅಪಿ — in both views too · ಗುಣಭೇದೇನ — by difference in attribute / quality · ಕಲ್ಪನಾಭೇದೇನ ಚ — and by difference in convention · ಭೇದಃ — the difference (between them) · ನ ಧರ್ಮಿಭೇದಃ — is not a difference in the subject (dharmin) itself · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ॥

    Summary of Mūla, Bhāṣya, and Ṭīkā

    Text Author Function
    ಮೂಲ (Mūla) Upaniṣad (Atharva-veda tradition) Verse (śloka / mantra) cited by Śaṅkara as Vedic authority for the Year as Prajāpati; presents two schools of Time-knowers — one describing the Year as five-footed and twelve-formed, the other as seven-wheeled and six-spoked
    ಭಾಷ್ಯ (Bhāṣya) Śaṅkarācārya (8th c. CE) Glosses each epithet of the verse; resolves the apparent contradiction between the two schools by showing both describe the same Year-as-Prajāpati; closes with the summary statement that in any case the Year is the cause of the world
    ಟೀಕಾ (Ṭīkā) Ānandagiri (13th c. CE) Disambiguates the grammar of dvādaśākṛti (two bahuvrīhi analyses); resolves the locative "dyulokāt" as antarikṣa not svarga (to avoid an anvaya-fault); glosses "u" as equivalent to "tu"; shows the two schools differ only in convention and quality, not in their subject

    Core Philosophical Thread

    1. The verse is a śloka cited as Vedic authority (mantra) to support the Bhāṣya's analysis — specifically to corroborate that the Saṃvatsara (Year) is Prajāpati and the cause of the world.
    2. Two schools of Time-knowers describe the Year using different conventions — five-fold (pañcapāda / dvādaśākṛti) and six-fold (saptacakra / ṣaḍara) — but both identify the same entity.
    3. The difference between the two conventions is in guṇa (attribute) and kalpanā (convention of description) only — not in the dharmin (the subject being described). This is Ānandagiri's key clarification.
    4. Hemanta and Śiśira are the pivotal difference: the first school merges them into one season (giving five seasons / five feet); the second school separates them (giving six seasons / six spokes).
    5. The closing Bhāṣya statement ties the verse back to the argument of the entire first question: the Year — whether five-footed or seven-wheeled — is characterised by Moon and Sun (Rayi and Prāṇa) and is the cause of the world.
