    Praśna Upaniṣad 1.1

    ಮಂತ್ರ 1.1

    ಮಂತ್ರೋಕ್ತಸ್ಯಾರ್ಥಸ್ಯ ವಿಸ್ತರಾನುವಾದೀದಂ ಬ್ರಾಹ್ಮಣಮಾರಭ್ಯತೇ । ಋಷಿಪ್ರಶ್ನಪ್ರತಿವಾಚನಾಖ್ಯಾಯಿಕಾ ತು ವಿದ್ಯಾಸ್ತುತಯೇ । ಏವಂ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯಸಂವಾಸಾದಿತಪೋಯುಕ್ತೈರ್ಗ್ರಾಹ್ಯಾ, ಪಿಪ್ಪಲಾದವತ್ಸರ್ವಜ್ಞಕಲ್ಪೈರಾಚಾರ್ಯೈಃ ವಕ್ತವ್ಯಾ ಚ, ನ ಯೇನ ಕೇನಚಿದಿತಿ ವಿದ್ಯಾಂ ಸ್ತೌತಿ । ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯಾದಿಸಾಧನಸೂಚನಾಚ್ಚ ತತ್ಕರ್ತವ್ಯತಾ ಸ್ಯಾತ್ —
    This Brāhmaṇa section is now commenced as a detailed exposition of the meaning that was briefly stated in the mantra. The narrative in the form of the sages asking questions and receiving answers is intended for the glorification of this knowledge (Brahma-vidyā). The purpose is to praise the knowledge by showing that it is fit to be received only by those who have undergone austerities such as living in brahmacarya for a year, residing with the teacher, and observing other disciplines. Likewise, it is to be taught only by teachers who, like Pippalāda, are comparable to omniscient persons; it is not something that may be taught by just anyone. Thus the Upaniṣad extols the greatness of this knowledge. Furthermore, by indicating disciplines such as brahmacarya and the like, the text also establishes that these are duties to be undertaken by seekers of knowledge.

    ಮಂತ್ರೋಕ್ತಸ್ಯಾರ್ಥಸ್ಯ ವಿಸ್ತರಾನುವಾದೀ ಇದಂ ಬ್ರಾಹ್ಮಣಮಾರಭ್ಯತೇ

    This Brāhmaṇa portion is begun as a detailed explanation of the meaning expressed in the mantra.

    ಮಂತ್ರೋಕ್ತಸ್ಯ → stated in the mantra ಅರ್ಥಸ್ಯ → of the meaning ವಿಸ್ತರ → detailed ಅನುವಾದೀ → explanatory restatement ಇದಂ → this ಬ್ರಾಹ್ಮಣಂ → Brāhmaṇa section ಆರಭ್ಯತೇ → is commenced ಋಷಿಪ್ರಶ್ನಪ್ರತಿವಾಚನಾಖ್ಯಾಯಿಕಾ ತು ವಿದ್ಯಾಸ್ತುತಯೇ

    The narrative consisting of the sages' questions and answers is for the praise of the knowledge.

    ಋಷಿ → sage ಪ್ರಶ್ನ → question ಪ್ರತಿವಾಚನ → answer ಆಖ್ಯಾಯಿಕಾ → narrative account ತು → indeed ವಿದ್ಯಾ → knowledge ಸ್ತುತಯೇ → for praise ಏವಂ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯಸಂವಾಸಾದಿತಪೋಯುಕ್ತೈರ್ಗ್ರಾಹ್ಯಾ

    Thus this knowledge is to be received by those endowed with austerities such as living for a year in brahmacarya and residing with the teacher.

    ಏವಂ → thus ಸಂವತ್ಸರ → one year ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯ → celibate discipline ಸಂವಾಸ → residence with the teacher ಆದಿ → and other ತಪಃ → austerities ಯುಕ್ತೈಃ → endowed with ಗ್ರಾಹ್ಯಾ → fit to be received ಪಿಪ್ಪಲಾದವತ್ಸರ್ವಜ್ಞಕಲ್ಪೈರಾಚಾರ್ಯೈಃ ವಕ್ತವ್ಯಾ ಚ

    And it is to be taught by teachers who, like Pippalāda, are comparable to omniscient persons.

    ಪಿಪ್ಪಲಾದವತ್ → like Pippalāda ಸರ್ವಜ್ಞಕಲ್ಪೈಃ → comparable to omniscient beings ಆಚಾರ್ಯೈಃ → by teachers ವಕ್ತವ್ಯಾ → should be taught ಚ → and

    ನ ಯೇನ ಕೇನಚಿದಿತಿ ವಿದ್ಯಾಂ ಸ್ತೌತಿ By implying that it should not be taught by just anyone, the text praises the knowledge. - ನ → not - ಯೇನ ಕೇನಚಿತ್ → by any random person - ಇತಿ → thus - ವಿದ್ಯಾಂ → the knowledge - ಸ್ತೌತಿ → praises

    ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯಾದಿಸಾಧನಸೂಚನಾಚ್ಚ ತತ್ಕರ್ತವ್ಯತಾ ಸ್ಯಾತ್ And by indicating disciplines such as brahmacarya, it establishes that these are to be practiced. - ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯಾದಿ → brahmacarya and similar disciplines - ಸಾಧನ → means/spiritual disciplines - ಸೂಚನಾತ್ → by indicating - ಚ → and - ತತ್ → those - ಕರ್ತವ್ಯತಾ → obligatoriness, duty - ಸ್ಯಾತ್ → is established

    ಸುಕೇಶಾ ಚ ಭಾರದ್ವಾಜಃ ಶೈಬ್ಯಶ್ಚ ಸತ್ಯಕಾಮಃ ಸೌರ್ಯಾಯಣೀ ಚ ಗಾರ್ಗ್ಯಃ ಕೌಸಲ್ಯಶ್ಚಾಶ್ವಲಾಯನೋ ಭಾರ್ಗವೋ ವೈದರ್ಭಿಃ ಕಬಂಧೀ ಕಾತ್ಯಾಯನಸ್ತೇ ಹೈತೇ
    ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಪರಾ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮನಿಷ್ಠಾಃ ಪರಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಾನ್ವೇಷಮಾಣಾ ಏಷ ಹ ವೈ ತತ್ಸರ್ವಂ ವಕ್ಷ್ಯತೀತಿ ತೇ ಹ ಸಮಿತ್ಪಾಣಯೋ ಭಗವಂತಂ ಪಿಪ್ಪಲಾದಮುಪಸನ್ನಾಃ ॥ ೧ ॥
    Sukeśa, the son of Bharadvāja; Satyakāma, the son of Śibi; Gārgya of the lineage of Sūryāyaṇa; Kauśalya, the son of Aśvala; Bhārgava of Vidarbha; and Kabandhi, the son of Kātyāyana—these seekers were devoted to Brahman, established in Brahman, and desirous of knowing the Supreme Brahman. Thinking, “Surely this revered teacher will explain all this to us,” they approached the venerable Pippalāda, carrying sacrificial fuel in their hands.

    ಸುಕೇಶಾ ಚ ಭಾರದ್ವಾಜಃ And Sukeśa, the son of Bharadvāja. - ಸುಕೇಶಃ → Sukeśa - ಚ → and - ಭಾರದ್ವಾಜಃ → descendant/son of Bharadvāja

    ಶೈಬ್ಯಃ ಚ ಸತ್ಯಕಾಮಃ And Satyakāma, the descendant of Śibi. - ಶೈಬ್ಯಃ → descendant of Śibi - ಸತ್ಯಕಾಮಃ → Satyakāma

    ಸೌರ್ಯಾಯಣೀ ಚ ಗಾರ್ಗ್ಯಃ And Gārgya of the Sūryāyaṇa lineage. - ಸೌರ್ಯಾಯಣೀ → belonging to Sūryāyaṇa lineage - ಗಾರ್ಗ್ಯಃ → Gārgya

    ಕೌಸಲ್ಯಃ ಚ ಆಶ್ವಲಾಯನಃ And Kauśalya, son of Aśvala. - ಕೌಸಲ್ಯಃ → Kauśalya - ಆಶ್ವಲಾಯನಃ → descendant of Aśvala

    ಭಾರ್ಗವಃ ವೈದರ್ಭಿಃ Bhārgava of Vidarbha. - ಭಾರ್ಗವಃ → descendant of Bhṛgu - ವೈದರ್ಭಿಃ → from Vidarbha

    ಕಬಂಧೀ ಕಾತ್ಯಾಯನಃ Kabandhi, son of Kātyāyana. - ಕಬಂಧೀ → Kabandhi - ಕಾತ್ಯಾಯನಃ → descendant of Kātyāyana

    ತೇ ಹ ಏತೇ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಪರಾಃ These indeed were devoted to Brahman. - ತೇ → they - ಹ → indeed - ಏತೇ → these - ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಪರಾಃ → having Brahman as their supreme goal

    ಬ್ರಹ್ಮನಿಷ್ಠಾಃ Established in Brahman. - ಬ್ರಹ್ಮನಿಷ್ಠಾಃ → steadfast in contemplation of Brahman

    In this context, Śaṅkara generally takes this as being devoted to scriptural pursuit and contemplation leading toward Brahma-jñāna.

    ಪರಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ ಅನ್ವೇಷಮಾಣಾಃ Seeking the Supreme Brahman. - ಪರಂ → supreme - ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ → Brahman - ಅನ್ವೇಷಮಾಣಾಃ → searching for, seeking

    ಏಷಃ ಹ ವೈ ತತ್ ಸರ್ವಂ ವಕ್ಷ್ಯತಿ ಇತಿ Thinking, “Surely he will explain all that.” - ಏಷಃ → this teacher - ಹ ವೈ → indeed - ತತ್ ಸರ್ವಂ → all that (Brahman-related knowledge) - ವಕ್ಷ್ಯತಿ → will explain - ಇತಿ → thus

    ತೇ ಹ ಸಮಿತ್ಪಾಣಯಃ They approached carrying sacrificial fuel in their hands. - ತೇ → they - ಹ → indeed - ಸಮಿತ್ → sacrificial fuel sticks - ಪಾಣಯಃ → in their hands

    Samit-pāṇi is symbolic: humility readiness for discipleship willingness to serve the teacher

    ಭಗವಂತಂ ಪಿಪ್ಪಲಾದಂ ಉಪಸನ್ನಾಃ They approached the revered Pippalāda. - ಭಗವಂತಂ → venerable, revered one - ಪಿಪ್ಪಲಾದಂ → Pippalāda - ಉಪಸನ್ನಾಃ → approached

    ಸುಕೇಶಾ ಚ ನಾಮತಃ, ಭರದ್ವಾಜಸ್ಯಾಪತ್ಯಂ ಭಾರದ್ವಾಜಃ । ಶೈಬ್ಯಶ್ಚ ಶಿಬೇರಪತ್ಯಂ ಶೈಬ್ಯಃ, ಸತ್ಯಕಾಮೋ ನಾಮತಃ । ಸೌರ್ಯಾಯಣೀ ಸೂರ್ಯಸ್ಯಾಪತ್ಯಂ ಸೌರ್ಯಃ, ತಸ್ಯಾಪತ್ಯಂ ಸೌರ್ಯಾಯಣಿಃ ; ಛಾಂದಸಂ ಸೌರ್ಯಾಯಣೀತಿ ; ಗಾರ್ಗ್ಯಃ ಗರ್ಗಗೋತ್ರೋತ್ಪನ್ನಃ । ಕೌಸಲ್ಯಶ್ಚ ನಾಮತಃ, ಅಶ್ವಲಸ್ಯಾಪತ್ಯಮಾಶ್ವಲಾಯನಃ । ಭಾರ್ಗವಃ ಭೃಗೋರ್ಗೋತ್ರಾಪತ್ಯಂ ಭಾರ್ಗವಃ, ವೈದರ್ಭಿಃ ವಿದರ್ಭೇಷು ಭವಃ । ಕಬಂಧೀ ನಾಮತಃ, ಕತ್ಯಸ್ಯಾಪತ್ಯಂ ಕಾತ್ಯಾಯನಃ ; ವಿದ್ಯಮಾನಃ ಪ್ರಪಿತಾಮಹೋ ಯಸ್ಯ ಸಃ ; ಯುವಪ್ರತ್ಯಯಃ । ತೇ ಹ ಏತೇ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಪರಾಃ ಅಪರಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ ಪರತ್ವೇನ ಗತಾಃ, ತದನುಷ್ಠಾನನಿಷ್ಠಾಶ್ಚ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮನಿಷ್ಠಾಃ, ಪರಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ ಅನ್ವೇಷಮಾಣಾಃ ಕಿಂ ತತ್ ಯನ್ನಿತ್ಯಂ ವಿಜ್ಞೇಯಮಿತಿ ತತ್ಪ್ರಾಪ್ತ್ಯರ್ಥಂ ಯಥಾಕಾಮಂ ಯತಿಷ್ಯಾಮ ಇತ್ಯೇವಂ ತದನ್ವೇಷಣಂ ಕುರ್ವಂತಃ, ತದಧಿಗಮಾಯ ಏಷ ಹ ವೈ ತತ್ಸರ್ವಂ ವಕ್ಷ್ಯತೀತಿ ಆಚಾರ್ಯಮುಪಜಗ್ಮುಃ । ಕಥಮ್ ? ತೇ ಹ ಸಮಿತ್ಪಾಣಯಃ ಸಮಿದ್ಭಾರಗೃಹೀತಹಸ್ತಾಃ ಸಂತಃ, ಭಗವಂತಂ ಪೂಜಾವಂತಂ ಪಿಪ್ಪಲಾದಮಾಚಾರ್ಯಮ್ ಉಪಸನ್ನಾಃ ಉಪಜಗ್ಮುಃ ॥
    Sukeśa is the personal name; being the son of Bharadvāja, he is called Bhāradvāja. Likewise, Śaibya means a descendant of Śibi, while Satyakāma is his personal name. Sauryāyaṇi means a descendant of Sūrya; "Sauryāyaṇi" is a Vedic form of expression. Gārgya means one born in the lineage of Garga. Kauśalya is the personal name, and Āśvalāyana means a descendant of Aśvala. Bhārgava means one descended from the lineage of Bhṛgu, and Vaidarbhi means one belonging to the region of Vidarbha. Kabandhi is the personal name, and Kātyāyana means a descendant of Katya; the suffix indicates that the ancestor (Katya) is still remembered in the lineage. These indeed were devoted to Brahman, taking the lower Brahman as their supreme goal and being steadfast in its contemplation and worship; hence they are called Brahmaniṣṭhas. At the same time, they were seekers of the Supreme Brahman, wondering, "What is that eternal Reality which ought to be known?" Desiring to attain That, and resolving, "We shall strive in every way to realize It," they engaged in the search for the Supreme Brahman. Thinking, "Surely this teacher will explain all that to us," they approached him. How did they approach? Carrying bundles of sacrificial fuel in their hands, they approached the revered and venerable teacher Pippalāda.

    ಸುಕೇಶಾ ಚ ನಾಮತಃ "Sukeśa" is the personal name. - ಸುಕೇಶಾ → Sukeśa - ನಾಮತಃ → by name

    ಭರದ್ವಾಜಸ್ಯ ಅಪತ್ಯಂ ಭಾರದ್ವಾಜಃ Being the son (descendant) of Bharadvāja, he is called Bhāradvāja. - ಭರದ್ವಾಜಸ್ಯ → of Bharadvāja - ಅಪತ್ಯಂ → offspring - ಭಾರದ್ವಾಜಃ → descendant of Bharadvāja

    ಶೈಬ್ಯಃ ಚ ಶಿಬೇಃ ಅಪತ್ಯಂ Śaibya means a descendant of Śibi. - ಶೈಬ್ಯಃ → descendant of Śibi - ಶಿಬೇಃ → of Śibi - ಅಪತ್ಯಂ → offspring

    ಸತ್ಯಕಾಮಃ ನಾಮತಃ Satyakāma is the personal name. - ಸತ್ಯಕಾಮಃ → Satyakāma - ನಾಮತಃ → by name

    ಸೌರ್ಯಾಯಣೀ ಸೂರ್ಯಸ್ಯ ಅಪತ್ಯಂ Sauryāyaṇi means a descendant of Sūrya. - ಸೌರ್ಯಾಯಣೀ → Sauryāyaṇi - ಸೂರ್ಯಸ್ಯ → of Sūrya - ಅಪತ್ಯಂ → offspring

    ಛಾಂದಸಂ ಸೌರ್ಯಾಯಣೀತಿ "Sauryāyaṇi" is the Vedic (metrical) form. - ಛಾಂದಸಂ → Vedic usage - ಸೌರ್ಯಾಯಣೀತಿ → the form "Sauryāyaṇi"

    ಗಾರ್ಗ್ಯಃ ಗರ್ಗಗೋತ್ರೋತ್ಪನ್ನಃ Gārgya is one born in the lineage of Garga. - ಗಾರ್ಗ್ಯಃ → Gārgya - ಗರ್ಗ → Garga - ಗೋತ್ರ → lineage - ಉತ್ಪನ್ನಃ → born

    ಕೌಸಲ್ಯಃ ಚ ನಾಮತಃ Kauśalya is the personal name. - ಕೌಸಲ್ಯಃ → Kauśalya - ನಾಮತಃ → by name

    ಅಶ್ವಲಸ್ಯ ಅಪತ್ಯಂ ಆಶ್ವಲಾಯನಃ Āśvalāyana means descendant of Aśvala. - ಅಶ್ವಲಸ್ಯ → of Aśvala - ಅಪತ್ಯಂ → offspring - ಆಶ್ವಲಾಯನಃ → descendant of Aśvala

    ಭಾರ್ಗವಃ ಭೃಗೋಃ ಗೋತ್ರಾಪತ್ಯಂ Bhārgava means one descended from Bhṛgu's lineage. - ಭಾರ್ಗವಃ → Bhārgava - ಭೃಗೋಃ → of Bhṛgu - ಗೋತ್ರಾಪತ್ಯಂ → descendant of the lineage

    ವೈದರ್ಭಿಃ ವಿದರ್ಭೇಷು ಭವಃ Vaidarbhi means belonging to Vidarbha. - ವೈದರ್ಭಿಃ → Vaidarbhi - ವಿದರ್ಭೇಷು → in Vidarbha - ಭವಃ → born/residing

    ಕಬಂಧೀ ನಾಮತಃ Kabandhi is the personal name. - ಕಬಂಧೀ → Kabandhi - ನಾಮತಃ → by name

    ಕತ್ಯಸ್ಯ ಅಪತ್ಯಂ ಕಾತ್ಯಾಯನಃ Kātyāyana means descendant of Katya. - ಕತ್ಯಸ್ಯ → of Katya - ಅಪತ್ಯಂ → offspring - ಕಾತ್ಯಾಯನಃ → descendant of Katya

    ತೇ ಹ ಏತೇ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಪರಾಃ These indeed were devoted to Brahman. - ತೇ → they - ಹ → indeed - ಏತೇ → these - ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಪರಾಃ → devoted to Brahman

    ಅಪರಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ ಪರತ್ವೇನ ಗತಾಃ They had taken the lower Brahman as their immediate object of worship. - ಅಪರಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ → lower Brahman (saguṇa Brahman) - ಪರತ್ವೇನ → as supreme - ಗತಾಃ → accepted

    This is an important Śaṅkara gloss. Here "ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಪರಾಃ" is interpreted as seekers devoted to meditation upon saguṇa Brahman.

    ತದನುಷ್ಠಾನನಿಷ್ಠಾಃ ಚ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮನಿಷ್ಠಾಃ And they were steadfast in its practice. - ತತ್ → that - ಅನುಷ್ಠಾನ → practice/worship - ನಿಷ್ಠಾಃ → steadfast - ಬ್ರಹ್ಮನಿಷ್ಠಾಃ → devoted to Brahman

    ಪರಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ ಅನ್ವೇಷಮಾಣಾಃ Seeking the Supreme Brahman. - ಪರಂ → supreme - ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ → Brahman - ಅನ್ವೇಷಮಾಣಾಃ → searching for

    Notice the distinction:

    • ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಪರಾಃ → devoted to lower Brahman
    • ಪರಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ ಅನ್ವೇಷಮಾಣಾಃ → seeking knowledge of Nirguṇa Brahman

    ಕಿಂ ತತ್ ಯತ್ ನಿತ್ಯಂ ವಿಜ್ಞೇಯಂ ಇತಿ "What is that eternal Reality that ought to be known?" - ಕಿಂ ತತ್ → what is that? - ಯತ್ → which - ನಿತ್ಯಂ → eternal - ವಿಜ್ಞೇಯಂ → to be known - ಇತಿ → thus

    Śaṅkara presents the inner enquiry motivating them. ತತ್ಪ್ರಾಪ್ತ್ಯರ್ಥಂ ಯಥಾಕಾಮಂ ಯತಿಷ್ಯಾಮಃ

    "We shall strive in every way to attain That."

    • ತತ್ → that
    • ಪ್ರಾಪ್ತಿ → attainment
    • ಅರ್ಥಂ → for the sake of
    • ಯಥಾಕಾಮಂ → by every possible effort
    • ಯತಿಷ್ಯಾಮಃ → we shall strive

    ಏಷ ಹ ವೈ ತತ್ ಸರ್ವಂ ವಕ್ಷ್ಯತಿ ಇತಿ "Surely he will explain all this." - ಏಷಃ → this teacher - ಹ ವೈ → indeed - ತತ್ ಸರ್ವಂ → all that - ವಕ್ಷ್ಯತಿ → will explain

    ಆಚಾರ್ಯಂ ಉಪಜಗ್ಮುಃ They approached the teacher. - ಆಚಾರ್ಯಂ → teacher - ಉಪಜಗ್ಮುಃ → approached

    ಸಮಿತ್ಪಾಣಯಃ Carrying fuel sticks in their hands. - ಸಮಿತ್ → sacrificial fuel - ಪಾಣಯಃ → in their hands

    This signifies: humility discipleship readiness for service

    ಸಮಿದ್ಭಾರಗೃಹೀತಹಸ್ತಾಃ ಸಂತಃ Having their hands occupied with bundles of fuel. - ಸಮಿದ್ಭಾರ → bundle of fuel sticks - ಗೃಹೀತ → held - ಹಸ್ತಾಃ → hands - ಸಂತಃ → being
