    Praśna Upaniṣad 1.2

    ಮಂತ್ರ

    ತಾನ್ಹ ಸ ಋಷಿರುವಾಚ ಭೂಯ ಏವ ತಪಸಾ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯೇಣ ಶ್ರದ್ಧಯಾ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಂ ಸಂವತ್ಸ್ಯಥ ಯಥಾಕಾಮಂ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಾನ್ಪೃಚ್ಛತ ಯದಿ ವಿಜ್ಞಾಸ್ಯಾಮಃ ಸರ್ವಂ ಹ ವೋ ವಕ್ಷ್ಯಾಮ ಇತಿ ॥ ೨ ॥
    To them, the sage said: "Live here for one more year, practicing austerity, brahmacarya, and faith. Then ask questions according to your desire. If we know the answers, we shall explain everything to you."

    ತಾನ್ ಹ — to them indeed

    • ತಾನ್ → to them
    • ಹ → indeed

    ಸ ಋಷಿಃ ಉವಾಚ — the sage said

    • ಸಃ → he
    • ಋಷಿಃ → the sage
    • ಉವಾಚ → said

    ಭೂಯ ಏವ — for some more time; further

    • ಭೂಯಃ → further, additional
    • ಏವ → indeed

    ತಪಸಾ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯೇಣ ಶ್ರದ್ಧಯಾ — through austerity, brahmacarya, and faith

    • ತಪಸಾ → through austerity
    • ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯೇಣ → through brahmacarya
    • ಶ್ರದ್ಧಯಾ → through faith

    ಸಂವತ್ಸರಂ ಸಂವತ್ಸ್ಯಥ — you shall stay for a year

    • ಸಂವತ್ಸರಂ → for one year
    • ಸಂವತ್ಸ್ಯಥ → you shall stay, dwell

    ಯಥಾಕಾಮಂ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಾನ್ ಪೃಚ್ಛತ — ask questions according to your desire

    • ಯಥಾಕಾಮಂ → according to your wish
    • ಪ್ರಶ್ನಾನ್ → questions
    • ಪೃಚ್ಛತ → ask

    ಯದಿ ವಿಜ್ಞಾಸ್ಯಾಮಃ — if we know

    • ಯದಿ → if
    • ವಿಜ್ಞಾಸ್ಯಾಮಃ → we know

    ಸರ್ವಂ ಹ ವಃ ವಕ್ಷ್ಯಾಮಃ — we shall indeed tell you everything

    • ಸರ್ವಂ → everything
    • ಹ → indeed
    • ವಃ → to you
    • ವಕ್ಷ್ಯಾಮಃ → we shall tell

    ಇತಿ — thus

    • ಇತಿ → thus

    ಭಾಷ್ಯ

    ತಾನ್ ಏವಮುಪಗತಾನ್ ಸಃ ಹ ಕಿಲ ಋಷಿಃ ಉವಾಚ ಭೂಯಃ ಪುನರೇವ — ಯದ್ಯಪಿ ಯೂಯಂ ಪೂರ್ವಂ ತಪಸ್ವಿನ ಏವ, ತಥಾಪೀಹ ತಪಸಾ ಇಂದ್ರಿಯಸಂಯಮೇನ ವಿಶೇಷತೋ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯೇಣ ಶ್ರದ್ಧಯಾ ಚ ಆಸ್ತಿಕ್ಯಬುದ್ಧ್ಯಾ ಆದರವಂತಃ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಂ ಕಾಲಂ ಸಂವತ್ಸ್ಯಥ ಸಮ್ಯಗ್ಗುರುಶುಶ್ರೂಷಾಪರಾಃ ಸಂತೋ ವತ್ಸ್ಯಥ । ತತಃ ಯಥಾಕಾಮಂ ಯೋ ಯಸ್ಯ ಕಾಮಸ್ತಮನತಿಕ್ರಮ್ಯ ಯದ್ವಿಷಯೇ ಯಸ್ಯ ಜಿಜ್ಞಾಸಾ ತದ್ವಿಷಯಾನ್ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಾನ್ ಪೃಚ್ಛತ । ಯದಿ ತದ್ಯುಷ್ಮತ್ಪೃಷ್ಟಂ ವಿಜ್ಞಾಸ್ಯಾಮಃ । ಅನುದ್ಧತತ್ವಪ್ರದರ್ಶನಾರ್ಥೋ ಯದಿ - ಶಬ್ದೋ ನಾಜ್ಞಾನಸಂಶಯಾರ್ಥಃ ಪ್ರಶ್ನನಿರ್ಣಯಾದವಸೀಯತೇ ಸರ್ವಂ ಹ ವೋ ವಃ ಪೃಷ್ಟಾರ್ಥಂ ವಕ್ಷ್ಯಾಮ ಇತಿ ॥
    To those who had approached him in that manner, the sage indeed said again: "Although you are already practitioners of austerity, nevertheless, remain here for a year with discipline — practicing austerity in the form of control of the senses, especially brahmacarya, and faith, that is, a trusting attitude toward the authority of the scriptures and the teacher. Live here with reverence and with wholehearted dedication to serving the teacher. Thereafter, ask questions according to your respective desires. Whatever subject each of you wishes to know, and whatever inquiry arises in each one's mind, ask questions concerning that very subject. If we know the answer to what you ask, we shall explain it to you." The word 'if' (yadi) is used only to indicate the sage's humility and freedom from arrogance; it is not intended to imply ignorance or doubt. This is evident from the fact that all the questions are subsequently answered and resolved. Thus he says: "We shall indeed explain to you everything concerning the matters that you ask."

    ತಾನ್ ಏವಮ್ ಉಪಗತಾನ್ ಸಃ ಹ ಕಿಲ ಋಷಿಃ ಉವಾಚ

    The sage indeed said to those who had approached him in this manner.

    • ತಾನ್ → those (students)
    • ಏವಮ್ → thus, in this manner
    • ಉಪಗತಾನ್ → those who had approached
    • ಸಃ → he
    • ಹ ಕಿಲ → indeed
    • ಋಷಿಃ → the sage
    • ಉವಾಚ → said

    ಭೂಯಃ ಪುನರೇವ

    Further, once again.

    • ಭೂಯಃ → further, additionally
    • ಪುನಃ → again
    • ಏವ → indeed

    ಯದ್ಯಪಿ ಯೂಯಂ ಪೂರ್ವಂ ತಪಸ್ವಿನ ಏವ

    Although you are already practitioners of austerity,

    • ಯದ್ಯಪಿ → although
    • ಯೂಯಂ → you
    • ಪೂರ್ವಂ → previously, already
    • ತಪಸ್ವಿನಃ → practitioners of austerity
    • ಏವ → indeed

    ತಥಾಪಿ ಇಹ ತಪಸಾ ಇಂದ್ರಿಯಸಂಯಮೇನ

    Even so, here through tapas, namely control of the senses,

    • ತಥಾಪಿ → nevertheless
    • ಇಹ → here
    • ತಪಸಾ → through austerity
    • ಇಂದ್ರಿಯ-ಸಂಯಮೇನ → through restraint of the senses
    • ಇಂದ್ರಿಯ → senses
    • ಸಂಯಮ → control, restraint
    • ಏನ → by, through

    Śaṅkara explicitly glosses tapas here as indriya-saṃyama (sense-control).

    ವಿಶೇಷತೋ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯೇಣ

    Especially through brahmacarya.

    • ವಿಶೇಷತಃ → especially, particularly
    • ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯೇಣ → through brahmacarya

    ಶ್ರದ್ಧಯಾ ಚ ಆಸ್ತಿಕ್ಯಬುದ್ಧ್ಯಾ

    And through faith, that is, a trusting attitude toward the scriptures and teacher.

    • ಶ್ರದ್ಧಯಾ → through faith
    • ಚ → and
    • ಆಸ್ತಿಕ್ಯ-ಬುದ್ಧ್ಯಾ → through an attitude of trust in the authority of the scriptures
    • ಆಸ್ತಿಕ್ಯ → faith in the validity of the Veda
    • ಬುದ್ಧಿ → understanding, attitude

    ಆದರವಂತಃ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಂ ಕಾಲಂ ಸಂವತ್ಸ್ಯಥ

    You shall live here for a year with reverence.

    • ಆದರ-ವಂತಃ → possessed of reverence
    • ಆದರ → respect, reverence
    • ವಂತಃ → possessing
    • ಸಂವತ್ಸರಂ ಕಾಲಂ → for the period of one year
    • ಸಂವತ್ಸ್ಯಥ → you shall stay

    ಸಮ್ಯಕ್ ಗುರುಶುಶ್ರೂಷಾಪರಾಃ ಸಂತಃ ವತ್ಸ್ಯಥ

    You shall live devoted to proper service of the teacher.

    • ಸಮ್ಯಕ್ → properly
    • ಗುರು-ಶುಶ್ರೂಷಾ-ಪರಾಃ → devoted to service of the teacher
    • ಗುರು → teacher
    • ಶುಶ್ರೂಷಾ → service, attendance
    • ಪರಾಃ → dedicated to
    • ಸಂತಃ → being
    • ವತ್ಸ್ಯಥ → you shall live

    ತತಃ ಯಥಾಕಾಮಂ

    Thereafter, according to your desire,

    • ತತಃ → then, thereafter
    • ಯಥಾ-ಕಾಮಂ → according to your wish

    ಯಃ ಯಸ್ಯ ಕಾಮಃ ತಂ ಅನತಿಕ್ರಮ್ಯ

    Without going beyond what each one desires to know.

    • ಯಃ → whichever
    • ಯಸ್ಯ → of whom
    • ಕಾಮಃ → desire
    • ತಂ → that
    • ಅನತಿಕ್ರಮ್ಯ → not transgressing, not exceeding

    ಯದ್ವಿಷಯೇ ಯಸ್ಯ ಜಿಜ್ಞಾಸಾ

    Regarding whatever subject each one has an inquiry,

    • ಯದ್-ವಿಷಯೇ → regarding whichever topic
    • ಯಸ್ಯ → of whom
    • ಜಿಜ್ಞಾಸಾ → desire to know, inquiry

    ತದ್ವಿಷಯಾನ್ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಾನ್ ಪೃಚ್ಛತ

    Ask questions concerning those subjects.

    • ತದ್-ವಿಷಯಾನ್ → concerning those subjects
    • ಪ್ರಶ್ನಾನ್ → questions
    • ಪೃಚ್ಛತ → ask

    ಯದಿ ತತ್ ಯುಷ್ಮತ್ ಪೃಷ್ಟಂ ವಿಜ್ಞಾಸ್ಯಾಮಃ

    If we know that which is asked by you,

    • ಯದಿ → if
    • ತತ್ → that
    • ಯುಷ್ಮತ್-ಪೃಷ್ಟಂ → asked by you
    • ಯುಷ್ಮತ್ → by you
    • ಪೃಷ್ಟಂ → asked
    • ವಿಜ್ಞಾಸ್ಯಾಮಃ → we know

    ಅನುದ್ಧತತ್ವಪ್ರದರ್ಶನಾರ್ಥಃ ಯದಿ-ಶಬ್ದಃ

    The word "if" is used to indicate absence of arrogance.

    • ಅನುದ್ಧತತ್ವ-ಪ್ರದರ್ಶನಾರ್ಥಃ → for demonstrating humility
    • ಅನುದ್ಧತತ್ವ → non-arrogance, humility
    • ಪ್ರದರ್ಶನ → showing
    • ಅರ್ಥಃ → purpose
    • ಯದಿ-ಶಬ್ದಃ → the word "if"

    This is an important observation of Śaṅkara.

    ನ ಅಜ್ಞಾನಸಂಶಯಾರ್ಥಃ

    It is not intended to suggest ignorance or doubt.

    • ನ → not
    • ಅಜ್ಞಾನ → ignorance
    • ಸಂಶಯ → doubt
    • ಅರ್ಥಃ → meaning, purpose
    • ಪ್ರಶ್ನನಿರ್ಣಯಾತ್ ಅವಸೀಯತೇ

    This is understood from the subsequent resolution of the questions.

    • ಪ್ರಶ್ನ-ನಿರ್ಣಯಾತ್ → from the determination of the questions
    • ಪ್ರಶ್ನ → questions
    • ನಿರ್ಣಯ → ascertainment, resolution
    • ಅವಸೀಯತೇ → is understood, is concluded

    ಸರ್ವಂ ಹ ವಃ ಪೃಷ್ಟಾರ್ಥಂ ವಕ್ಷ್ಯಾಮಃ ಇತಿ

    "We shall indeed explain to you everything that you ask."

    • ಸರ್ವಂ → everything
    • ಹ → indeed
    • ವಃ → to you
    • ಪೃಷ್ಟ-ಅರ್ಥಂ → the matter asked about
    • ಪೃಷ್ಟ → asked
    • ಅರ್ಥಂ → subject matter
    • ವಕ್ಷ್ಯಾಮಃ → we shall explain
    • ಇತಿ → thus
