    Praśna Upaniṣad 1.4

    ಮಂತ್ರ

    ತಸ್ಮೈ ಸ ಹೋವಾಚ ಪ್ರಜಾಕಾಮೋ ವೈ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ಸ ತಪೋಽತಪ್ಯತ ಸ ತಪಸ್ತಪ್ತ್ವಾ ಸ ಮಿಥುನಮುತ್ಪಾದಯತೇ ರಯಿಂ ಚ ಪ್ರಾಣಂ ಚೇತ್ಯೇತೌ ಮೇ ಬಹುಧಾ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಕರಿಷ್ಯತ ಇತಿ ॥ ೪ ॥
    To him, the sage replied: "Prajāpati, desiring progeny, performed austerity. Having practiced austerity, he produced a pair — Rayi and Prāṇa — thinking, 'These two will create many kinds of beings for me.'"

    ತಸ್ಮೈ ಸಃ ಹ ಉವಾಚ

    To him indeed he said

    • ತಸ್ಮೈ → to him
    • ಸಃ → he
    • ಹ → indeed
    • ಉವಾಚ → said

    ಪ್ರಜಾಕಾಮಃ ವೈ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ

    Prajāpati indeed desired progeny

    • ಪ್ರಜಾ-ಕಾಮಃ → desirous of progeny
    • ಪ್ರಜಾ → offspring, beings
    • ಕಾಮಃ → desire
    • ವೈ → indeed
    • ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ → Prajāpati, the creator

    ಸಃ ತಪಃ ಅತಪ್ಯತ

    He practiced austerity

    • ಸಃ → he
    • ತಪಃ → austerity
    • ಅತಪ್ಯತ → practiced austerity

    ಸಃ ತಪಃ ತಪ್ತ್ವಾ

    Having performed austerity

    • ಸಃ → he
    • ತಪಃ → austerity
    • ತಪ್ತ್ವಾ → having practiced, having performed

    ಸಃ ಮಿಥುನಂ ಉತ್ಪಾದಯತೇ

    He produced a pair

    • ಸಃ → he
    • ಮಿಥುನಂ → pair, couple
    • ಉತ್ಪಾದಯತೇ → produced, created

    ರಯಿಂ ಚ ಪ್ರಾಣಂ ಚ

    Rayi and Prāṇa

    • ರಯಿಂ → Rayi
    • ಚ → and
    • ಪ್ರಾಣಂ → Prāṇa
    • ಚ → and

    ಇತಿ ಏತೌ ಮೇ ಬಹುಧಾ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಕರಿಷ್ಯತಃ

    "These two will create many kinds of beings for me"

    • ಇತಿ → thus
    • ಏತೌ → these two
    • ಮೇ → for me
    • ಬಹುಧಾ → in many ways, manifoldly
    • ಪ್ರಜಾಃ → beings, offspring
    • ಕರಿಷ್ಯತಃ → will create, will produce

    ಇತಿ

    Thus

    • ಇತಿ → thus

    ಭಾಷ್ಯ

    ತಸ್ಮೈ ಏವಂ ಪೃಷ್ಟವತೇ ಸ ಹ ಉವಾಚ ತದಪಾಕರಣಾಯಾಹ — ಪ್ರಜಾಕಾಮಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಆತ್ಮನಃ ಸಿಸೃಕ್ಷುಃ ವೈ, ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ಸರ್ವಾತ್ಮಾ ಸನ್ ಜಗತ್ಸ್ರಕ್ಷ್ಯಾಮೀತ್ಯೇವಂ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನವಾನ್ಯಥೋಕ್ತಕಾರೀ ತದ್ಭಾವಭಾವಿತಃ ಕಲ್ಪಾದೌ ನಿರ್ವೃತ್ತೋ ಹಿರಣ್ಯಗರ್ಭಃ, ಸೃಜ್ಯಮಾನಾನಾಂ ಪ್ರಜಾನಾಂ ಸ್ಥಾವರಜಂಗಮಾನಾಂ ಪತಿಃ ಸನ್ , ಜನ್ಮಾಂತರಭಾವಿತಂ ಜ್ಞಾನಂ ಶ್ರುತಿಪ್ರಕಾಶಿತಾರ್ಥವಿಷಯಂ ತಪಃ, ಅನ್ವಾಲೋಚಯತ್ ಅತಪ್ಯತ । ಅಥ ತು ಸಃ ಏವಂ ತಪಃ ತಪ್ತ್ವಾ ಶ್ರೌತಂ ಜ್ಞಾನಮನ್ವಾಲೋಚ್ಯ, ಸೃಷ್ಟಿಸಾಧನಭೂತಂ ಮಿಥುನಮ್ ಉತ್ಪಾದಯತೇ ಮಿಥುನಂ ದ್ವಂದ್ವಮುತ್ಪಾದಿತವಾನ್ ರಯಿಂ ಚ ಸೋಮಮನ್ನಂ ಪ್ರಾಣಂ ಚ ಅಗ್ನಿಮತ್ತಾರಮ್ ಇತ್ಯೇತೌ ಅಗ್ನೀಷೋಮೌ ಅತ್ರನ್ನಭೂತೌ ಮೇ ಮಮ ಬಹುಧಾ ಅನೇಕಧಾ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಕರಿಷ್ಯತಃ ಇತಿ ಏವಂ ಸಂಚಿಂತ್ಯ ಅಂಡೋತ್ಪತ್ತಿಕ್ರಮೇಣ ಸೂರ್ಯಾಚಂದ್ರಮಸಾವಕಲ್ಪಯತ್ ॥
    To him who had asked in this manner, Pippalāda replied in order to remove his doubt. Prajāpati, desiring to create beings from himself, being the Self of all and endowed with the knowledge, "I shall create the universe," and acting in accordance with that knowledge, manifested as Hiraṇyagarbha at the beginning of a cycle of creation. As the lord of all beings that were to be created, both stationary and moving, he contemplated upon tapas, which here means knowledge concerning the truths revealed by the scriptures and carried forward from previous births. Having thus practiced that tapas, and having reflected upon the scriptural knowledge, he created a pair which would serve as the means of creation. He produced the duality of Rayi and Prāṇa — Rayi being the Moon, food; and Prāṇa being Fire, the eater of food. Thinking, "These two, Agni and Soma, food and eater, will generate for me beings in many forms," he projected the Sun and Moon through the process of cosmic manifestation beginning from the cosmic egg.

    ತಸ್ಮೈ ಏವಂ ಪೃಷ್ಟವತೇ

    To him who had asked in this manner

    • ತಸ್ಮೈ → to him
    • ಏವಂ → thus, in this manner
    • ಪೃಷ್ಟವತೇ → to the one who had questioned

    ಸಃ ಹ ಉವಾಚ

    He indeed said

    • ಸಃ → he
    • ಹ → indeed
    • ಉವಾಚ → said

    ತದಪಾಕರಣಾಯ

    For removing that doubt

    • ತತ್ → that
    • ಅಪಾಕರಣಾಯ → for removal, for clearing away

    ಪ್ರಜಾಕಾಮಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಆತ್ಮನಃ ಸಿಸೃಕ್ಷುಃ

    Desiring to create beings from himself

    • ಪ್ರಜಾ-ಕಾಮಃ → desirous of creating beings
    • ಪ್ರಜಾಃ → beings
    • ಆತ್ಮನಃ → from himself
    • ಸಿಸೃಕ್ಷುಃ → desirous of creating

    ವೈ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ

    Indeed Prajāpati

    • ವೈ → indeed
    • ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ → Prajāpati

    ಸರ್ವಾತ್ಮಾ ಸನ್

    Being the Self of all

    • ಸರ್ವ → all
    • ಆತ್ಮಾ → Self
    • ಸನ್ → being

    ಜಗತ್ ಸ್ರಕ್ಷ್ಯಾಮಿ ಇತಿ

    "I shall create the universe"

    • ಜಗತ್ → universe
    • ಸ್ರಕ್ಷ್ಯಾಮಿ → I shall create
    • ಇತಿ → thus

    ಏವಂ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನವಾನ್

    Possessed of this knowledge

    • ಏವಂ → thus
    • ವಿಜ್ಞಾನವಾನ್ → endowed with knowledge

    ಯಥೋಕ್ತಕಾರೀ

    Acting in accordance with that knowledge

    • ಯಥಾ → according to
    • ಉಕ್ತ → what was known or understood
    • ಕಾರೀ → one who acts

    ತದ್ಭಾವಭಾವಿತಃ

    Endowed with that contemplation

    • ತತ್ → that
    • ಭಾವ → contemplation, idea
    • ಭಾವಿತಃ → imbued with, pervaded by

    ಕಲ್ಪಾದೌ ನಿರ್ವೃತ್ತಃ ಹಿರಣ್ಯಗರ್ಭಃ

    Manifested as Hiraṇyagarbha at the beginning of creation

    • ಕಲ್ಪ-ಆದೌ → at the beginning of a kalpa
    • ನಿರ್ವೃತ್ತಃ → manifested, came forth
    • ಹಿರಣ್ಯಗರ್ಭಃ → Hiraṇyagarbha

    ಸೃಜ್ಯಮಾನಾನಾಂ ಪ್ರಜಾನಾಂ ಸ್ಥಾವರಜಂಗಮಾನಾಂ ಪತಿಃ ಸನ್

    Being the lord of all beings to be created, moving and unmoving

    • ಸೃಜ್ಯಮಾನಾನಾಂ → of those being created
    • ಪ್ರಜಾನಾಂ → beings
    • ಸ್ಥಾವರ → stationary
    • ಜಂಗಮಾನಾಂ → moving
    • ಪತಿಃ → lord
    • ಸನ್ → being

    ಜನ್ಮಾಂತರಭಾವಿತಂ ಜ್ಞಾನಂ

    Knowledge carried over from previous births

    • ಜನ್ಮಾಂತರ → previous births
    • ಭಾವಿತಂ → cultivated, carried forward
    • ಜ್ಞಾನಂ → knowledge

    ಶ್ರುತಿಪ್ರಕಾಶಿತಾರ್ಥವಿಷಯಂ ತಪಃ

    Tapas consisting of knowledge of truths revealed by scripture

    • ಶ್ರುತಿ → scripture
    • ಪ್ರಕಾಶಿತ → revealed
    • ಅರ್ಥ → truth, meaning
    • ವಿಷಯಂ → concerning
    • ತಪಃ → tapas

    ಅನ್ವಾಲೋಚಯತ್ ಅತಪ್ಯತ

    He reflected upon; he practiced tapas

    • ಅನ್ವಾಲೋಚಯತ್ → contemplated
    • ಅತಪ್ಯತ → practiced tapas

    ಅಥ ತು ಸಃ ಏವಂ ತಪಃ ತಪ್ತ್ವಾ

    Then, having thus performed tapas

    • ಅಥ ತು → then indeed
    • ಸಃ → he
    • ಏವಂ → thus
    • ತಪಃ → tapas
    • ತಪ್ತ್ವಾ → having practiced

    ಶ್ರೌತಂ ಜ್ಞಾನಂ ಅನ್ವಾಲೋಚ್ಯ

    Having reflected upon scriptural knowledge

    • ಶ್ರೌತಂ → scriptural
    • ಜ್ಞಾನಂ → knowledge
    • ಅನ್ವಾಲೋಚ್ಯ → having contemplated

    ಸೃಷ್ಟಿಸಾಧನಭೂತಂ ಮಿಥುನಮ್ ಉತ್ಪಾದಯತೇ

    He produced a pair that would become the means of creation

    • ಸೃಷ್ಟಿ → creation
    • ಸಾಧನಭೂತಂ → serving as the means
    • ಮಿಥುನಂ → pair
    • ಉತ್ಪಾದಯತೇ → produced

    ಮಿಥುನಂ ದ್ವಂದ್ವಮ್ ಉತ್ಪಾದಿತವಾನ್

    He created a duality

    • ಮಿಥುನಂ → pair
    • ದ್ವಂದ್ವಂ → duality
    • ಉತ್ಪಾದಿತವಾನ್ → created

    ರಯಿಂ ಚ ಸೋಮಮ್ ಅನ್ನಂ

    Rayi, namely the Moon, which is food

    • ರಯಿಂ → Rayi
    • ಚ → and
    • ಸೋಮಂ → Moon
    • ಅನ್ನಂ → food

    ಪ್ರಾಣಂ ಚ ಅಗ್ನಿಮ್ ಅತ್ತಾರಮ್

    Prāṇa, namely Fire, the eater

    • ಪ್ರಾಣಂ → Prāṇa
    • ಚ → and
    • ಅಗ್ನಿಂ → Fire

    ಇತಿ ಏತೌ ಅಗ್ನೀಷೋಮೌ

    Thus these two are Agni and Soma

    • ಇತಿ → thus
    • ಏತೌ → these two
    • ಅಗ್ನೀಷೋಮೌ → Agni and Soma

    ಅನ್ನಭೂತೌ

    Food and eater

    • ಅನ್ನ → food
    • ಭೂತೌ → being

    ಮೇ ಮಮ ಬಹುಧಾ ಅನೇಕಧಾ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಕರಿಷ್ಯತಃ

    Will create for me beings of many kinds

    • ಏವಂ → in this manner
    • ಸಂಚಿಂತ್ಯ → having reflected
