# ಪ್ರಶ್ನೋಪನಿಷತ್ — ಪ್ರಥಮ ಪ್ರಶ್ನ, ಮಂತ್ರ ೯
# Praśna Upaniṣad — First Question, Verse 9

> **Source of Ṭīkā:** Ānandāśrama Sanskrit Series No. 8 (Ānandagiri on Śaṅkara Bhāṣya)

---

## ಮೂಲ (Mūla)

> ಸಂವತ್ಸರೋ ವೈ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಸ್ತಸ್ಯಾಯನೇ ದಕ್ಷಿಣಂ ಚೋತ್ತರಂ ಚ ।
>
> ತದ್ಯೇ ಹ ವೈ ತದಿಷ್ಟಾಪೂರ್ತೇ ಕೃತಮಿತ್ಯುಪಾಸತೇ ತೇ ಚಾಂದ್ರಮಸಮೇವ ಲೋಕಮಭಿಜಯಂತೇ ।
>
> ತ ಏವ ಪುನರಾವರ್ತಂತೇ ತಸ್ಮಾದೇತ ಋಷಯಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಕಾಮಾ ದಕ್ಷಿಣಂ ಪ್ರತಿಪದ್ಯಂತೇ ।
>
> ಏಷ ಹ ವೈ ರಯಿರ್ಯಃ ಪಿತೃಯಾಣಃ ॥ ೯ ॥

### Overall Translation

The Year (Saṃvatsara) is indeed Prajāpati (the Lord of creatures). His two paths
are the Southern and the Northern. Those who worship iṣṭa (sacrificial rites) and
pūrta (charitable works) as the (sufficient) accomplishment — they win only the
lunar world. They indeed return again (to this world). Therefore those seers who
desire progeny resort to the Southern path. This very Pitṛyāṇa is Rayi (the
material/nourishing principle).

### Word-to-Word

ತತ್ — that · ಯೇ — those who · ಹ ವೈ — truly / indeed · ತತ್ — that (qualifying the action) · ಇಷ್ಟಾಪೂರ್ತೇ — sacrificial rites and charitable works · ಕೃತಂ — accomplished / done · ಇತಿ — thus (thinking) · ಉಪಾಸತೇ — they worship · ತೇ — they · ಚಾಂದ್ರಮಸಂ ಏವ — only the lunar · ಲೋಕಂ — world / realm · ಅಭಿಜಯಂತೇ — win / attain ।

ತೇ ಏವ — they indeed · ಪುನಃ — again · ಆವರ್ತಂತೇ — return · ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ — therefore · ಏತೇ — these · ಋಷಯಃ — seers / sages · ಪ್ರಜಾಕಾಮಾಃ — desirous of progeny · ದಕ್ಷಿಣಂ — the Southern (path) · ಪ್ರತಿಪದ್ಯಂತೇ — resort to / enter upon ।

ಏಷಃ — this · ಹ ವೈ — verily / indeed · ರಯಿಃ — Rayi (the material / nourishing principle) · ಯಃ — which · ಪಿತೃಯಾಣಃ — the path of the ancestors ॥

## ಭಾಷ್ಯ (Bhāṣya)

> ಯಶ್ಚಾಸೌ ಚಂದ್ರಮಾ ಮೂರ್ತಿರನ್ನಮಮೂರ್ತಿಶ್ಚ ಪ್ರಾಣೋಽತ್ತಾದಿತ್ಯಸ್ತದೇತದೇಕಂ ಮಿಥುನಂ ಸರ್ವಂ ಕಥಂ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಕರಿಷ್ಯತ ಇತಿ, ಉಚ್ಯತೇ — ತದೇವ ಕಾಲಃ ಸಂವತ್ಸರೋ ವೈ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ, ತನ್ನಿರ್ವರ್ತ್ಯತ್ವಾತ್ಸಂವತ್ಸರಸ್ಯ ।
>
> ಚಂದ್ರಾದಿತ್ಯನಿರ್ವರ್ತ್ಯತಿಥ್ಯಹೋರಾತ್ರಸಮುದಾಯೋ ಹಿ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಃ ತದನನ್ಯತ್ವಾದ್ರಯಿಪ್ರಾಣೈತನ್ಮಿಥುನಾತ್ಮಕ ಏವೇತ್ಯುಚ್ಯತೇ ।
>
> ತತ್ಕಥಮ್ ? ತಸ್ಯ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಸ್ಯ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತೇಃ ಅಯನೇ ಮಾರ್ಗೌ ದ್ವೌ ದಕ್ಷಿಣಂ ಚೋತ್ತರಂ ಚ ।
>
> ಪ್ರಸಿದ್ಧೇ ಹ್ಯಯನೇ ಷಣ್ಮಾಸಲಕ್ಷಣೇ, ಯಾಭ್ಯಾಂ ದಕ್ಷಿಣೇನೋತ್ತರೇಣ ಚ ಯಾತಿ ಸವಿತಾ ಕೇವಲಕರ್ಮಿಣಾಂ ಜ್ಞಾನಸಂಯುಕ್ತಕರ್ಮವತಾಂ ಚ ಲೋಕಾನ್ವಿದಧತ್ ।
>
> ಕಥಮ್ ? ತತ್ ತತ್ರ ಚ ಬ್ರಾಹ್ಮಣಾದಿಷು ಯೇ ಹ ವೈ ಋಷಯಃ ತದುಪಾಸತ ಇತಿ ।
>
> ಕ್ರಿಯಾವಿಶೇಷಣೋ ದ್ವಿತೀಯಸ್ತಚ್ಛಬ್ದಃ ।
>
> ಇಷ್ಟಂ ಚ ಪೂರ್ತಂ ಚ ಇಷ್ಟಾಪೂರ್ತೇ ಇತ್ಯಾದಿ ಕೃತಮೇವೋಪಾಸತೇ ನಾಕೃತಂ ನಿತ್ಯಮ್, ತೇ ಚಾಂದ್ರಮಸಮೇವ ಚಂದ್ರಮಸಿ ಭವಂ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತೇರ್ಮಿಥುನಾತ್ಮಕಸ್ಯಾಂಶಂ ರಯಿಮನ್ನಭೂತಂ ಲೋಕಮ್ ಅಭಿಜಯಂತೇ ಕೃತರೂಪತ್ವಾಚ್ಚಾಂದ್ರಮಸಸ್ಯ ।
>
> ತೇ ಏವ ಚ ಕೃತಕ್ಷಯಾತ್ ಪುನರಾವರ್ತಂತೇ ಇಮಂ ಲೋಕಂ ಹೀನತರಂ ವಾ ವಿಶಂತೀತಿ ಹ್ಯುಕ್ತಮ್ ।
>
> ಯಸ್ಮಾದೇವಂ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಮನ್ನಾತ್ಮಕಂ ಫಲತ್ವೇನಾಭಿನಿರ್ವರ್ತಯಂತಿ ಚಂದ್ರಮಿಷ್ಟಾಪೂರ್ತಕರ್ಮಣಾ ಪ್ರಜಾಕಾಮಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾರ್ಥಿನಃ ಏತೇ ಋಷಯಃ ಸ್ವರ್ಗದ್ರಷ್ಟಾರಃ ಗೃಹಸ್ಥಾಃ, ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ಸ್ವಕೃತಮೇವ ದಕ್ಷಿಣಂ ದಕ್ಷಿಣಾಯನೋಪಲಕ್ಷಿತಂ ಚಂದ್ರಂ ಪ್ರತಿಪದ್ಯಂತೇ ।
>
> ಏಷ ಹ ವೈ ರಯಿಃ ಅನ್ನಮ್, ಯಃ ಪಿತೃಯಾಣಃ ಪಿತೃಯಾಣೋಪಲಕ್ಷಿತಶ್ಚಂದ್ರಃ ॥

### Overall Translation

**[Opening question — pūrvapakṣa:]** The Moon is what has form (mūrti); food is
matter; the formless (amūrti) is Prāṇa; and the Sun is the eater — how can this
one pair generate all progeny?

**[Answer:]** It is said thus: That very Kāla (Time) is the Saṃvatsara, and the
Saṃvatsara is indeed Prajāpati — because everything is produced out of it. For
the Year is nothing but the aggregate of tithis (lunar days), days, and nights,
produced by Moon and Sun together. Since the Year is non-different from the dyad
of Rayi and Prāṇa, it is said to be of the very nature of that pair
(mithunātmaka).

How so? Prajāpati the Year has two paths — the Southern and the Northern. These
are the well-known solstices (ayanas), each characterised by six months, by which
the Sun (Savitṛ) travels southward and northward, distributing worlds
respectively to those who perform only ritual action (kevalakarmins) and to those
who combine knowledge with action (jñānasaṃyuktakarmavants).

How exactly? Among Brāhmaṇas and others — those seers who worship the iṣṭāpūrta
as accomplished. The second occurrence of the word "tat" here serves as an
adjective qualifying the action. Iṣṭa is sacrificial rite; pūrta is charitable
work (and so on); they worship only what has been done (kṛtam) — not what is
eternal and unconditioned (nitya). They win only the cāndramasa (lunar) world —
the Rayi portion, the food-natured, material aspect of Prajāpati's dyadic nature,
which exists in the Moon — because the lunar world has the character of the
accomplished (kṛtarūpa).

They indeed return again when their accumulated merit is exhausted — entering this
world or a still lower one, as has been stated. Because those seers — householders
and perceivers of heaven — desire progeny and produce Prajāpati in his food-form
as their fruit through sacrificial and charitable rites, they resort to the
Southern path signified by the Southern solstice, which is nothing but the result
of their own deeds. This Pitṛyāṇa is verily Rayi — food/matter. The Moon,
characterised by the Pitṛyāṇa path, is that Rayi.

### Anvaya (Prose Order of the Bhāṣya)

The Bhāṣya contains four sentences whose word-order is sufficiently compressed or
inverted that the syntactic structure needs to be laid out explicitly. These are
the four that Ānandagiri himself flags with "ಇತ್ಯನ್ವಯಃ."

---

**Sentence 1 — The pūrvapakṣa (opening question)**

*As written:*
> ಯಃ ಚಾಸೌ ಚಂದ್ರಮಾ ಮೂರ್ತಿಃ ಅನ್ನಂ ಅಮೂರ್ತಿಃ ಚ ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ಅತ್ತಾ ಆದಿತ್ಯಃ —
> ತತ್ ಏತತ್ ಏಕಂ ಮಿಥುನಂ ಸರ್ವಂ — ಕಥಂ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಕರಿಷ್ಯತಿ ।

*Anvaya (resolved prose order):*
> ಯಃ ಚಂದ್ರಮಾ ಅಸೌ [ಸಃ] ಮೂರ್ತಿಃ [ರಯಿಃ] ; ಅನ್ನಂ [ಅಪಿ ರಯಿಃ ಏವ] ।
>
> ಯಃ ಚ ಪ್ರಾಣಃ [ಸಃ] ಅಮೂರ್ತಿಃ ; ಆದಿತ್ಯಃ [ಅಪಿ ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ಏವ,] ಅತ್ತಾ [ಚ] ।
>
> ತತ್ ಏತತ್ [ರಯಿಪ್ರಾಣರೂಪಂ] ಏಕಂ ಮಿಥುನಂ ಸರ್ವಂ ಸರ್ವಾತ್ಮಕಂ [ಅಸ್ತಿ] ।
>
> [ಏತತ್ ಮಿಥುನಂ] ಕಥಂ [ಸರ್ವಾಃ] ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಕರಿಷ್ಯತಿ [ಇತಿ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಃ] ?

*English:* That Moon is the formed one (Rayi); food too is Rayi. That Prāṇa is the
formless one; the Sun too is Prāṇa, and is the eater. That one pair — of the
nature of Rayi and Prāṇa — is all, is everything. [Question:] How will this pair
generate all progeny?

---

**Sentence 2 — The Year produces through tithis and ahorātras**

*As written:*
> ಚಂದ್ರಾದಿತ್ಯನಿರ್ವರ್ತ್ಯ-ತಿಥ್ಯಹೋರಾತ್ರ-ಸಮುದಾಯಃ ಹಿ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಃ ।
>
> ತದನನ್ಯತ್ವಾತ್ ರಯಿಪ್ರಾಣ-ಏತನ್ಮಿಥುನಾತ್ಮಕ ಏವ [ಸಂವತ್ಸರಃ] ಇತಿ ಉಚ್ಯತೇ ।

*Anvaya (resolved prose order):*
> ಸಂವತ್ಸರಃ ಹಿ [ಕಃ ?] ಚಂದ್ರನಿರ್ವರ್ತ್ಯ-ತಿಥಿ-ಆದಿತ್ಯನಿರ್ವರ್ತ್ಯ-ಅಹೋರಾತ್ರ-ಸಮುದಾಯಃ [ಏವ] ।
>
> [ಸಂವತ್ಸರಃ] ರಯಿಪ್ರಾಣ-ಮಿಥುನಾತ್ [ಅನನ್ಯಃ] — ತದ್-ಅನನ್ಯತ್ವಾತ್ —
> [ಅತಃ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಃ] ಮಿಥುನಾತ್ಮಕ ಏವ ಇತಿ ಉಚ್ಯತೇ ।

*English:* For what is the Year? It is the aggregate of the tithis produced by the
Moon and the ahorātras (days-and-nights) produced by the Sun. Since the Year is
non-different from the Rayi-Prāṇa pair — because of that non-difference — the
Year is said to be of the very nature of that pair.

*Note:* The compound ಚಂದ್ರಾದಿತ್ಯನಿರ್ವರ್ತ್ಯ-ತಿಥ್ಯಹೋರಾತ್ರ-ಸಮುದಾಯಃ hides the
internal division that Ānandagiri makes explicit: tithis are produced by the Moon,
ahorātras by the Sun — not both by both.

**Sentence 3 — The Sun's two movements and the worlds allotted (the most complex sentence)**

*As written:*
> ಪ್ರಸಿದ್ಧೇ ಹಿ ಅಯನೇ ಷಣ್ಮಾಸ-ಲಕ್ಷಣೇ, ಯಾಭ್ಯಾಂ ದಕ್ಷಿಣೇನ ಉತ್ತರೇಣ ಚ
> ಯಾತಿ ಸವಿತಾ ಕೇವಲಕರ್ಮಿಣಾಂ ಜ್ಞಾನಸಂಯುಕ್ತಕರ್ಮವತಾಂ ಚ ಲೋಕಾನ್ ವಿದಧತ್ ।

*Anvaya (resolved prose order — two separate syntactic streams):*

> **[Stream 1 — Southern path:]**
> ಸವಿತಾ [ಚಂದ್ರಃ ಅಪಿ — ಉಪಲಕ್ಷಣಾತ್] ದಕ್ಷಿಣೇನ [ಅಯನೇನ] ಯಾತಿ
> ಕೇವಲಕರ್ಮಿಣಾಂ ಲೋಕಾನ್ ವಿದಧತ್ ।

> **[Stream 2 — Northern path:]**
> [ಸ ಏವ ಸವಿತಾ] ಉತ್ತರೇಣ [ಅಯನೇನ] ಯಾತಿ
> ಜ್ಞಾನಸಂಯುಕ್ತಕರ್ಮವತಾಂ ಲೋಕಾನ್ ವಿದಧತ್ ।

*English:*
Stream 1: The Sun (and the Moon too, by upalakaṣaṇa) travels by the Southern
solstice-path while distributing worlds to those who perform only ritual action.

Stream 2: That same Sun travels by the Northern solstice-path while distributing
worlds to those who combine knowledge with action.

*Note:* The original text runs both streams together in one participial construction
(ವಿದಧತ್ governing both objects). Ānandagiri's anvaya splits them explicitly and
also adds that ಸವಿತಾ is an upalakaṣaṇa — it denotes the Moon as well, since the
Moon too moves along these paths generating tithis.

---

**Sentence 4 — The final statement on householder-seers and the Southern path**

*As written:*
> ಯಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಏವಂ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಂ ಅನ್ನಾತ್ಮಕಂ ಫಲತ್ವೇನ ಅಭಿನಿರ್ವರ್ತಯಂತಿ
> ಚಂದ್ರಂ ಇಷ್ಟಾಪೂರ್ತ-ಕರ್ಮಣಾ ಪ್ರಜಾಕಾಮಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾರ್ಥಿನಃ ಏತೇ ಋಷಯಃ
> ಸ್ವರ್ಗದ್ರಷ್ಟಾರಃ ಗೃಹಸ್ಥಾಃ, ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಸ್ವಕೃತಂ ಏವ ದಕ್ಷಿಣಂ
> ದಕ್ಷಿಣಾಯನ-ಉಪಲಕ್ಷಿತಂ ಚಂದ್ರಂ ಪ್ರತಿಪದ್ಯಂತೇ ।

*Anvaya (resolved prose order):*
> ಏತೇ ಋಷಯಃ — ಪ್ರಜಾಕಾಮಾಃ, ಪ್ರಜಾರ್ಥಿನಃ, ಸ್ವರ್ಗದ್ರಷ್ಟಾರಃ, ಗೃಹಸ್ಥಾಃ —
> ಇಷ್ಟಾಪೂರ್ತ-ಕರ್ಮಣಾ [ಮಾಧ್ಯಮೇನ] ಅನ್ನಾತ್ಮಕಂ [ಚ] ಚಂದ್ರರೂಪಂ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಂ
> ಫಲತ್ವೇನ ಅಭಿನಿರ್ವರ್ತಯಂತಿ [ಯಸ್ಮಾತ್] —
> ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ [ತೇ] ಸ್ವಕೃತಂ ಏವ [ಯತ್] ದಕ್ಷಿಣಾಯನ-ಉಪಲಕ್ಷಿತಂ ದಕ್ಷಿಣಂ [ಚಂದ್ರಂ]
> ಪ್ರತಿಪದ್ಯಂತೇ ।

*English:* Because these seers — desirous of progeny, seekers of offspring,
perceivers of heaven, householders — bring about Prajāpati in his food-natured
Moon-form as their fruit through sacrificial and charitable action, therefore they
resort to nothing but their own deed, which is the Southern path — the Moon
indicated by the Southern solstice.

*Note:* The accumulation of four appositive epithets for ಋಷಯಃ and the placement of
ಯಸ್ಮಾತ್…ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ across the sentence boundary makes this the densest construction
in the Bhāṣya on this verse. The anvaya clarifies that ಫಲತ್ವೇನ ಅಭಿನಿರ್ವರ್ತಯಂತಿ
and ಪ್ರತಿಪದ್ಯಂತೇ are the two main verbs, and that ಚಂದ್ರಂ is to be supplied in
the concluding clause from context.

### Overall Translation

Ānandagiri opens by locating this Bhāṣya passage within its context: Prajāpati was
said to produce a pair (mithuna), and Śaṅkara now brings that pair to conclusion
with the words "yaścāsau" (that which is the Moon). The syntax (anvaya) is: "That
Moon which has form, and that Prāṇa which is formless — these two together are one
pair that is everything (sarvātmaka)." It was said that "these two will produce
many progeny for me" — and now the question "how?" arises.

Ānandagiri explains that Śaṅkara's answer shows how Rayi and Prāṇa, through the
medium of the Saṃvatsara (Year) and related structures, become creators of
progeny. That very pair (mithuna) is the Saṃvatsara — Time itself — and that
Saṃvatsara is Prajāpati, because the Saṃvatsara is produced out of (nirvartyate)
the pair that is of the nature of Prajāpati. Ānandagiri then unpacks the sub-point
(tannirvartyatvāt): tithis (lunar days) are produced by the Moon; days and nights
(ahorātras) are produced by the Sun — this is the division. Yet even though Time
is produced by them, how can Time be identical to them? Ānandagiri answers by
citing the principle of non-difference between cause and effect (kāryakāraṇayoḥ
abhedāt) — hence "non-difference from that" (tadananyatvāt).

Ānandagiri then clarifies that it is not only through tithis etc. that the Year is
produced by Moon and Sun, but also through the two ayanas (solstices) — and this
is the purpose of the sub-question "tat katham?" (how so?). The real question is:
from what other reason does the Year come to be produced by Moon and Sun? The
correct syntactic connection (anvaya) of Śaṅkara's sentence is: the Sun travels
by the Southern path while distributing worlds for those who perform only ritual
action; it travels by the Northern path while distributing worlds for those who
combine knowledge with action. And the word "Savitṛ" (Sun) here is an
upalakaṣaṇa (indicative term) — it also implies the Moon. The Southern solstice
begins with Jyeṣṭha (month), and the Northern with Mārgaśīrṣa — this is the
accepted Vedic teaching. Since the two solstices are established because of the
Sun (and Moon) moving along those Southern and Northern paths for the purpose of
distributing worlds to the performers of action, the two ayanas are produced by
them, and through the ayanas the Year too is produced by them.

To the further question "how are Moon and Sun distributors of worlds?", Ānandagiri
explains: because the worlds are attained through the Southern and Northern ayanas
produced by Moon and Sun, and because the worlds thus attained are themselves of
the nature of Moon and Sun — therefore those two are the distributors of worlds.
This is resolved by Śaṅkara's statement beginning "tad ye ha vā…" — which
Ānandagiri glosses as "tat tatra."

On the definition of iṣṭa and pūrta, Ānandagiri quotes the traditional
two-verse definition:

**Iṣṭa** is: Agnihotra, austerity (tapas), truth (satya), observance of the
Vedas, hospitality (ātithya), and Vaiśvadeva rite.

**Pūrta** is: the digging of step-wells, wells, and tanks; construction of
temples; gift of food; and planting of gardens.

On the word "kṛtam" (accomplished) — Ānandagiri explains that Śaṅkara's "iti"
after "kṛtam" draws in and covers "iṣṭāpūrte" as well as "pūrta," and the word
"ādi" (etc.) stands for "datta" (what is given/donated). The meaning is: they
worship and carry out only what is accomplished/conditioned — only kārya (the
produced, finite). This qualifier (viśeṣaṇa) is stated as the reason for their
return. Finally, Ānandagiri explains "kṛtarūpatvāt": because the lunar world
itself is born from conditioned acts like iṣṭi etc., it too has the character of
the kṛta (the accomplished, the finite) and is therefore impermanent — hence
rebirth. The Vedic statement "imaṃ lokam" is cited as the scriptural proof
(māna) for this return.

ತತ್ — this · ಉಪಪಾದಯತಿ — (Śaṅkara) establishes / proves · ಚಂದ್ರ ಇತಿ — with the word "Moon" ।

ಚಂದ್ರನಿರ್ವರ್ತ್ಯಾಃ — produced by the Moon · ತಿಥಯಃ — the tithis (lunar days) · ಆದಿತ್ಯನಿರ್ವರ್ತ್ಯಾನಿ — produced by the Sun · ಅಹೋರಾತ್ರಾಣಿ — the days-and-nights · ಇತಿ ವಿಭಾಗಃ — thus is the division / distinction ।

ತನ್ನಿರ್ವರ್ತ್ಯತ್ವೇ ಅಪಿ — even though produced by them · ಕಾಲಸ್ಯ — of Time · ಕಥಂ — how · ತದಾತ್ಮಕತಾ — identity with them / being of their nature · ಇತಿ ಆಶಂಕ್ಯ — anticipating this doubt · ಕಾರ್ಯಕಾರಣಯೋಃ ಅಭೇದಾತ್ — because of the non-difference of cause and effect · ಇತಿ ಆಹ — thus (Śaṅkara) states · ತದನನ್ಯತ್ವಾತ್ ಇತಿ — with the words "because of non-difference from that" ।

ನ ಕೇವಲಂ — not only · ತಿಥ್ಯಾದಿ-ದ್ವಾರಾ — through the medium of tithis etc. · ಚಂದ್ರಾದಿ-ನಿರ್ವರ್ತ್ಯತ್ವಂ — being produced by Moon etc. · ಸಂವತ್ಸರಸ್ಯ — of the Year · ಕಿಂ ತು — but also · ಅಯನದ್ವಯ-ದ್ವಾರಾ ಅಪಿ — through the medium of the two solstices as well · ಇತಿ ವಕ್ತುಂ — in order to say this · ತಸ್ಯ ಅಯನೇ ಇತಿ ಆದಿ ವಾಕ್ಯಂ — the sentence beginning "his two paths" · ತತ್-ಪ್ರಶ್ನ-ಪೂರ್ವಕಂ — preceded by that question · ವ್ಯಾಚಷ್ಟೇ — (Śaṅkara) explains · ತತ್ ಕಥಂ ಇತಿ — with the words "how so?" ।

ಚಂದ್ರಾದಿತ್ಯ-ನಿರ್ವರ್ತ್ಯತ್ವಂ — being produced by Moon and Sun · ಕುತಃ — from what · ಹೇತ್ವಂತರಾತ್ — from another / additional reason · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

ಕೇವಲಕರ್ಮಿಣಾಂ — of those who perform only ritual action · ಲೋಕಾನ್ — worlds · ವಿದಧತ್ — while distributing · ದಕ್ಷಿಣೇನ ಯಾತಿ — travels by the Southern (path) ।

ಜ್ಞಾನಯುಕ್ತಕರ್ಮವತಾಂ — of those who combine knowledge with action · ಲೋಕಾನ್ ವಿದಧತ್ — while distributing worlds · ಉತ್ತರೇಣ ಯಾತಿ — travels by the Northern · ಇತಿ ಅನ್ವಯಃ — thus is the syntactic connection ।

ಸವಿತಾ ಇತಿ — the word "Savitṛ (Sun)" · ಉಪಲಕ್ಷಣಂ — is an indicative term (standing for a wider class) · ಚಂದ್ರಸ್ಯ ಅಪಿ — for the Moon as well ।

ಜ್ಯೇಷ್ಠ-ಆದಿಃ — beginning with Jyeṣṭha (month) · ದಕ್ಷಿಣಾಯನಂ — the Southern solstice · ಮಾರ್ಗಶೀರ್ಷ-ಆದಿಃ — beginning with Mārgaśīrṣa (month) · ಉತ್ತರಾಯಣಂ — the Northern solstice · ಇತಿ ಶ್ರುತಿಷು ಪ್ರಸಿದ್ಧೇಃ — as established in the Vedic texts · ತತಃ ಚ — and therefore · ಕರ್ಮಿಣಾಂ ಲೋಕಾನ್ ವಿಧಾತುಂ — in order to distribute worlds to the performers of action · ತಯೋಃ — of those two (Moon and Sun) · ದಕ್ಷಿಣ-ಉತ್ತರಾಭ್ಯಾಂ ಮಾರ್ಗಾಭ್ಯಾಂ — by the Southern and Northern paths · ಗಮನಾತ್ — because of the movement / travel · ತನ್ನಿಮಿತ್ತತ್ವಾತ್ — because they are the cause of it · ಅಯನದ್ವಯ-ಪ್ರಸಿದ್ಧೇಃ — because the two solstices are well established · ತನ್ನಿರ್ವರ್ತ್ಯತ್ವಂ — being produced by them · ತಯೋಃ ಅಯನಯೋಃ — of those two solstices · ಇತಿ — thus · ತದ್-ದ್ವಾರಾ — through them (the solstices) · ಸಂವತ್ಸರಸ್ಯ ಅಪಿ — of the Year as well · ತನ್ನಿರ್ವರ್ತ್ಯತ್ವಂ — being produced by them · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

ಚಂದ್ರಾದಿತ್ಯಯೋಃ — of Moon and Sun · ಕಥಂ — how · ಲೋಕವಿಧಾಯಕತ್ವಂ — the quality of being distributors of worlds · ಇತಿ ಪೃಚ್ಛತಿ — thus (Ānandagiri) asks · ಕಥಂ ಇತಿ — with the word "how?" ।

ಚಂದ್ರಾದಿತ್ಯ-ನಿರ್ವರ್ತ್ಯ-ದಕ್ಷಿಣ-ಉತ್ತರ-ಅಯನ-ದ್ವಾರಾ — through the Southern and Northern solstices produced by Moon and Sun · ಲೋಕಪ್ರಾಪ್ತೇಃ — because of the attainment of worlds · ಪ್ರಾಪ್ಯಸ್ಯ ಲೋಕಸ್ಯ ಅಪಿ — and because the world to be attained also · ಚಂದ್ರಾದಿತ್ಯ-ಆತ್ಮಕತ್ವಾತ್ — because of being of the nature of Moon and Sun · ತಯೋಃ — of those two · ತದ್-ವಿಧಾಯಕತ್ವಂ — their quality of being distributors of those worlds · ಇತಿ — thus · ತದ್-ಯೇ ಹ ವಾ ಇತ್ಯಾದಿ-ವಾಕ್ಯೇನ — by the sentence beginning "tad ye ha vā" · ಪರಿಹರತಿ — (Śaṅkara) resolves / answers · ತತ್ ತತ್ರ ಇತಿ — with the words "tat tatra" ।

ಇಷ್ಟಂ ಚ ಇತಿ — and with the words "iṣṭa and" ।

ಅಗ್ನಿಹೋತ್ರಂ — the Agnihotra rite (daily fire offering) · ತಪಃ — austerity · ಸತ್ಯಂ — truth · ವೇದಾನಾಂ ಅನುಪಾಲನಂ — observance of the Vedas · ಆತಿಥ್ಯಂ — hospitality · ವೈಶ್ವದೇವಃ — the Vaiśvadeva rite (offering to all gods) · ಚ — and · ಇಷ್ಟಂ ಇತಿ ಅಭಿಧೀಯತೇ — is called Iṣṭa ॥

ವಾಪೀ-ಕೂಪ-ತಡಾಗ-ಆದಿ — step-wells, wells, tanks, etc. · ದೇವತಾಯತನಾನಿ — temples / shrines · ಅನ್ನಪ್ರದಾನಂ — gift of food · ಆರಾಮಃ — garden / park · ಚ — and · ಪೂರ್ತಂ ಇತಿ ಅಭಿಧೀಯತೇ — is called Pūrta ॥

ಇತಿ ತಯೋಃ ಭೇದಃ — thus is the distinction between the two ।

ಕೃತಂ ಇತಿ ಉಪಾಸತೇ ಇತಿ — the phrase "they worship (it as) accomplished" · ಕೃತ-ಶಬ್ದ-ಉಪರಿತನ-ಇತಿ-ಶಬ್ದಂ — the word "iti" placed after the word "kṛta" · ಇಷ್ಟಾಪೂರ್ತೇ ಇತಿ — the compound "iṣṭāpūrte" · ಪೂರ್ತ-ಶಬ್ದ-ಉಪರಿ — over the word "pūrta" · ಆಕೃಷ್ಯ — drawing / pulling (the iti) · ಆದಿ-ಶಬ್ದ-ಪರ್ಯಾಯತಯಾ — as a synonym for the word "ādi" (etc.) · ವ್ಯಾಚಷ್ಟೇ — (Śaṅkara) explains · ಇತ್ಯಾದಿ ಇತಿ — with the word "and so on" ।

ದತ್ತಂ — what is given / donated · ಆದಿ-ಶಬ್ದ-ಅರ್ಥಃ — is the meaning of the word "ādi" ।

ಕೃತಂ ಏವ ಉಪಾಸತೇ — they worship only what is accomplished / conditioned · ಕಾರ್ಯಂ ಏವ ಅನುತಿಷ್ಠಂತಿ — they carry out only what is produced / finite · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

ಇದಂ ಚ ವಿಶೇಷಣಂ — and this qualifier / adjective · ಪುನರಾವೃತ್ತೌ — for the return / rebirth · ಹೇತುತಯಾ — as the reason · ಉಕ್ತಂ — is stated ।

ಕೃತರೂಪ-ಇಷ್ಟ್ಯಾದಿ-ಜನ್ಯತ್ವಾತ್ — because (the Moon) is born from conditioned acts like iṣṭi etc. · ಚಂದ್ರಸ್ಯ ಅಪಿ — even of the Moon · ಕೃತತ್ವೇನ — because of having the nature of the accomplished / conditioned · ಅನಿತ್ಯತ್ವಾತ್ — because of impermanence · ಪುನರಾವೃತ್ತಿಃ — there is return / rebirth · ಇತಿ ಆಹ — thus (Śaṅkara) states · ಕೃತರೂಪತ್ವಾತ್ ಇತಿ — with the words "because of having the character of the accomplished" ।

ಪುನರಾವೃತ್ತೌ — for (establishing) the return / rebirth · ಮಂತ್ರ-ವಾಕ್ಯಂ — the Vedic statement / scriptural sentence · ಪ್ರಮಾಣಯತಿ — (Śaṅkara) cites as proof / establishes with scriptural authority · ಇಮಂ ಲೋಕಂ ಇತಿ — with the words "this world" ॥

## Summary of Mūla, Bhāṣya, and Ṭīkā

| Text | Author | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **ಮೂಲ** (Mūla) | Upaniṣad (Atharva-veda tradition) | Root teaching on the two paths and Prajāpati as the Year |
| **ಭಾಷ್ಯ** (Bhāṣya) | Śaṅkarācārya (8th c. CE) | Philosophical commentary resolving the question of how the Rayi-Prāṇa pair creates all progeny, via Time, solstices, and the two paths |
| **ಟೀಕಾ** (Ṭīkā) | Ānandagiri (13th c. CE, student of Śaṅkara's lineage) | Grammatical and logical gloss on the Bhāṣya — resolving anvaya (syntax), defining iṣṭa/pūrta with traditional verses, and identifying upalakaṣaṇa usage |

### Core Philosophical Thread

1. **Rayi and Prāṇa** (Moon/matter and Sun/life-force) are the primordial pair.
2. **The Year (Saṃvatsara)** is non-different from this pair — it is produced by them through tithis, ahorātras, and the two ayanas, and cause-effect non-difference makes it identical with them.
3. **The two paths** after death follow from this structure: the Southern (Pitṛyāṇa) leads to the lunar world — a finite, conditioned realm of Rayi — and return; the Northern (Devayāna) leads beyond.
4. **Iṣṭa-pūrta** worshippers, treating conditioned deeds as the final end, attain only the conditioned lunar world and cycle back.
5. **Ānandagiri's contribution** is to sharpen the logical spine: the identity of Time with the Rayi-Prāṇa pair rests on the Vedāntic principle of kāryakāraṇa-abheda (non-difference of effect from cause), and "Savitṛ" in the Bhāṣya functions as an upalakaṣaṇa that includes the Moon as well.
