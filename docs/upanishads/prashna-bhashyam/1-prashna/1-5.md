# Praśna Upaniṣad 1.5

## ಮಂತ್ರ

<div class="mantra">
ಆದಿತ್ಯೋ ಹ ವೈ ಪ್ರಾಣೋ ರಯಿರೇವ ಚಂದ್ರಮಾ ರಯಿರ್ವಾ ಏತತ್ಸರ್ವಂ ಯನ್ಮೂರ್ತಂ ಚಾಮೂರ್ತಂ ಚ ತಸ್ಮಾನ್ಮೂರ್ತಿರೇವ ರಯಿಃ ॥ ೫ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
The Sun indeed is Prāṇa, and the Moon indeed is Rayi. Verily, all this—whatever is formed and whatever is formless—is Rayi. Therefore, form alone is Rayi.
</div>

### ಮಂತ್ರ ಪದಾರ್ಥ

ಆದಿತ್ಯಃ ಹ ವೈ ಪ್ರಾಣಃ

- ಆದಿತ್ಯಃ → the Sun
- ಹ → indeed
- ವೈ → verily, indeed
- ಪ್ರಾಣಃ → Prāṇa, the life principle

ರಯಿಃ ಏವ ಚಂದ್ರಮಾಃ

The Moon indeed is Rayi

- ರಯಿಃ → Rayi
- ಏವ → indeed
- ಚಂದ್ರಮಾಃ → the Moon

ರಯಿಃ ವಾ ಏತತ್ ಸರ್ವಂ

Indeed, all this is Rayi

- ರಯಿಃ → Rayi
- ವಾ → indeed, verily
- ಏತತ್ → this
- ಸರ್ವಂ → all

ಯತ್ ಮೂರ್ತಂ ಚ ಅಮೂರ್ತಂ ಚ

Whatever is formed and whatever is formless

- ಯತ್ → whatever
- ಮೂರ್ತಂ → formed, having shape
- ಚ → and
- ಅಮೂರ್ತಂ → formless
- ಚ → and

ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಮೂರ್ತಿಃ ಏವ ರಯಿಃ

Therefore form alone is Rayi

- ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ → therefore
- ಮೂರ್ತಿಃ → form, the formed
- ಏವ → indeed, alone
- ರಯಿಃ → Rayi

## ಭಾಷ್ಯ

<div class="bhasya">
ತತ್ರ ಆದಿತ್ಯಃ ಹ ವೈ ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ಅತ್ತಾ ಅಗ್ನಿಃ । ರಯಿರೇವ ಚಂದ್ರಮಾಃ । ರಯಿರೇವಾನ್ನಂ ಸೋಮ ಏವ । ತದೇತದೇಕಮತ್ತಾ ಅಗ್ನಿಶ್ಚಾನ್ನಂ ಚ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿರೇಕಂ ತು ಮಿಥುನಮ್ ; ಗುಣಪ್ರಧಾನಕೃತೋ ಭೇದಃ । ಕಥಮ್ ? ರಯಿರ್ವೈ ಅನ್ನಮೇವ ಏತತ್ ಸರ್ವಮ್ । ಕಿಂ ತತ್ ? ಯತ್ ಮೂರ್ತಂ ಚ ಸ್ಥೂಲಂ ಚ ಅಮೂರ್ತಂ ಚ ಸೂಕ್ಷ್ಮಂ ಚ । ಮೂರ್ತಾಮೂರ್ತೇ ಅತ್ತ್ರನ್ನರೂಪೇ ಅಪಿ ರಯಿರೇವ । ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಪ್ರವಿಭಕ್ತಾದಮೂರ್ತಾತ್ ಯದನ್ಯನ್ಮೂರ್ತರೂಪಂ ಮೂರ್ತಿಃ, ಸೈವ ರಯಿಃ ಅನ್ನಮ್ ಅಮೂರ್ತೇನ ಅತ್ತ್ರಾ ಅದ್ಯಮಾನತ್ವಾತ್ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
Among the two, the Sun is indeed Prāṇa, the eater, Fire. The Moon alone is Rayi. Rayi itself is food, that is, Soma.

Thus, the eater, Fire, and the food together constitute a single Prajāpati appearing as a pair. The distinction between them is made only on account of differences in attributes and predominance.

How so? Indeed, all this is nothing but Rayi, that is, food. What is that all? Whatever is gross and formed, and whatever is subtle and formless. Both the formed and the formless, though constituting the eater and the eaten, are still Rayi alone.

Therefore, among these, the formed aspect, distinguished from the formless, is specifically called Rayi or food, because it is consumed by the formless eater.
</div>

### ಭಾಷ್ಯ ಪದಾರ್ಥ

ತತ್ರ ಆದಿತ್ಯಃ ಹ ವೈ ಪ್ರಾಣಃ

Among them, the Sun indeed is Prāṇa

- ತತ್ರ → among them, in that context
- ಆದಿತ್ಯಃ → the Sun
- ಹ → indeed
- ವೈ → verily
- ಪ್ರಾಣಃ → Prāṇa

ಅತ್ತಾ ಅಗ್ನಿಃ

The eater, namely Fire

- ಅತ್ತಾ → eater, consumer
- ಅಗ್ನಿಃ → Fire

ರಯಿಃ ಏವ ಚಂದ್ರಮಾಃ

The Moon indeed is Rayi

- ರಯಿಃ → Rayi
- ಏವ → indeed
- ಚಂದ್ರಮಾಃ → the Moon

ರಯಿಃ ಏವ ಅನ್ನಂ ಸೋಮಃ ಏವ

Rayi itself is food, namely Soma

- ರಯಿಃ → Rayi
- ಏವ → indeed
- ಅನ್ನಂ → food
- ಸೋಮಃ → Soma, the Moon
- ಏವ → indeed

ತತ್ ಏತತ್ ಏಕಂ ಅತ್ತಾ ಅಗ್ನಿಃ ಚ ಅನ್ನಂ ಚ

The eater, Fire, and the food are one

- ತತ್ ಏತತ್ → thus this
- ಏಕಂ → one
- ಅತ್ತಾ → eater
- ಅಗ್ನಿಃ → Fire
- ಚ → and
- ಅನ್ನಂ → food
- ಚ → and

ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ಏಕಂ ತು ಮಿಥುನಮ್

One Prajāpati appearing as a pair

- ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ → Prajāpati
- ಏಕಂ → one
- ತು → indeed
- ಮಿಥುನಮ್ → pair, dual manifestation

ಗುಣಪ್ರಧಾನಕೃತಃ ಭೇದಃ

The distinction is due to difference in attributes

- ಗುಣ → quality, attribute
- ಪ್ರಧಾನ → predominance
- ಕೃತಃ → caused by
- ಭೇದಃ → distinction

ಕಥಮ್

How?

- ಕಥಮ್ → how

ರಯಿಃ ವೈ ಅನ್ನಂ ಏವ ಏತತ್ ಸರ್ವಮ್

Indeed all this is nothing but food (Rayi)

- ರಯಿಃ → Rayi
- ವೈ → indeed
- ಅನ್ನಂ → food
- ಏವ → alone
- ಏತತ್ → this
- ಸರ್ವಮ್ → all

ಕಿಂ ತತ್

What is that all?

- ಕಿಂ → what
- ತತ್ → that

ಯತ್ ಮೂರ್ತಂ ಚ ಸ್ಥೂಲಂ ಚ

Whatever is formed and gross

- ಯತ್ → whatever
- ಮೂರ್ತಂ → formed
- ಚ → and
- ಸ್ಥೂಲಂ → gross
- ಚ → and

ಅಮೂರ್ತಂ ಚ ಸೂಕ್ಷ್ಮಂ ಚ

And whatever is formless and subtle

- ಅಮೂರ್ತಂ → formless
- ಚ → and
- ಸೂಕ್ಷ್ಮಂ → subtle
- ಚ → and

ಮೂರ್ತಾಮೂರ್ತೇ ಅತ್ತ್ರನ್ನರೂಪೇ ಅಪಿ

Though existing as eater and eaten

- ಮೂರ್ತಾಮೂರ್ತೇ → formed and formless
- ಅತ್ತ್ರನ್ನ-ರೂಪೇ → in the form of eater and food
  - ಅತ್ತೃ → eater
  - ಅನ್ನ → food
  - ರೂಪೇ → in the form
- ಅಪಿ → even though

ರಯಿಃ ಏವ

They are still Rayi alone

- ರಯಿಃ → Rayi
- ಏವ → alone

ತಸ್ಮಾತ್

Therefore

- ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ → therefore

ಪ್ರವಿಭಕ್ತಾತ್ ಅಮೂರ್ತಾತ್

Separated from the formless aspect

- ಪ್ರವಿಭಕ್ತಾತ್ → distinguished, separated
- ಅಮೂರ್ತಾತ್ → from the formless

ಯತ್ ಅನ್ಯತ್ ಮೂರ್ತರೂಪಂ

That which is the formed aspect

- ಯತ್ → which
- ಅನ್ಯತ್ → distinct, other
- ಮೂರ್ತರೂಪಂ → having the nature of form

ಮೂರ್ತಿಃ ಸಾ ಏವ ರಯಿಃ ಅನ್ನಮ್

That form alone is called Rayi, food

- ಮೂರ್ತಿಃ → form
- ಸಾ ಏವ → that alone
- ರಯಿಃ → Rayi
- ಅನ್ನಮ್ → food

ಅಮೂರ್ತೇನ ಅತ್ತ್ರಾ ಅದ್ಯಮಾನತ್ವಾತ್

Because it is consumed by the formless eater

- ಅಮೂರ್ತೇನ → by the formless
- ಅತ್ತ್ರಾ → by the eater
- ಅದ್ಯಮಾನತ್ವಾತ್ → because of being eaten, consumed
