# ಪ್ರಶ್ನೋಪನಿಷತ್ — ಪ್ರಥಮ ಪ್ರಶ್ನ, ಮಂತ್ರ ೧೪
# Praśna Upaniṣad — First Question, Verse 14

> **Source of Ṭīkā:** Ānandāśrama Sanskrit Series No. 8 (Ānandagiri on Śaṅkara Bhāṣya)

---

## ಮೂಲ (Mūla)

> ಅನ್ನಂ ವೈ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಸ್ತತೋ ಹ ವೈ ತದ್ರೇತಸ್ತಸ್ಮಾದಿಮಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಯಂತ ಇತಿ ॥ ೧೪ ॥

### Overall Translation

Food (anna) is indeed Prajāpati. From that indeed comes seed (retas). From that
these creatures are born.

### Word-to-Word

ಅನ್ನಂ — food · ವೈ — indeed / verily · ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ — Prajāpati / Lord of creatures · ತತಃ — from that · ಹ ವೈ — indeed · ತತ್ — that · ರೇತಃ — seed / semen · ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ — from that · ಇಮಾಃ — these · ಪ್ರಜಾಃ — creatures / progeny · ಪ್ರಜಾಯಂತೇ — are born / come into being · ಇತಿ — thus ॥

---

## ಭಾಷ್ಯ (Bhāṣya)

> ಏವಂ ಕ್ರಮೇಣ ಪರಿಣಮ್ಯ — ತತ್ ಅನ್ನಂ ವೈ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ।
>
> ಕಥಮ್ ? ತತಃ ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಹ ವೈ ರೇತಃ ನೃಬೀಜಂ ತತ್ಪ್ರಜಾಕಾರಣಂ ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಯೋಷಿತಿ ಸಿಕ್ತಾತ್ ಇಮಾಃ ಮನುಷ್ಯಾದಿಲಕ್ಷಣಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಯಂತೇ ಯತ್ಪೃಷ್ಟಂ ಕುತೋ ಹ ವೈ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಯಂತ ಇತಿ ।
>
> ತದೇವಂ ಚಂದ್ರಾದಿತ್ಯಮಿಥುನಾದಿಕ್ರಮೇಣ ಅಹೋರಾತ್ರಾಂತೇನ ಅನ್ನರೇತೋದ್ವಾರೇಣ ಇಮಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಯಂತ ಇತಿ ನಿರ್ಣೀತಮ್ ॥

### Overall Translation

Having transformed thus in sequence — that food is indeed Prajāpati.

How? From that (food), indeed comes seed — the male seed, the cause of progeny —
from that, when deposited in a woman, these creatures characterised as human
beings and the like are born. This is what was asked: "from where indeed are
these creatures born?"

Thus it has been determined: these creatures are born through the sequence
beginning with the Moon-and-Sun pair, ending with the day-and-night, by the
gateway of food and seed.

### Word-to-Word

ಏವಂ — thus · ಕ್ರಮೇಣ — in sequence / by gradation · ಪರಿಣಮ್ಯ — having transformed / evolved · ತತ್ — that · ಅನ್ನಂ — food · ವೈ — indeed · ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ — Prajāpati ।

ಕಥಮ್ — how? · ತತಃ — from that · ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ — from that (food) · ಹ ವೈ — indeed · ರೇತಃ — seed / semen · ನೃಬೀಜಂ — the male seed · ತತ್ಪ್ರಜಾಕಾರಣಂ — the cause of progeny · ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ — from that · ಯೋಷಿತಿ ಸಿಕ್ತಾತ್ — when deposited in a woman · ಇಮಾಃ — these · ಮನುಷ್ಯಾದಿಲಕ್ಷಣಾಃ — characterised as human beings and the like · ಪ್ರಜಾಃ — creatures / progeny · ಪ್ರಜಾಯಂತೇ — are born · ಯತ್ ಪೃಷ್ಟಂ — what was asked · ಕುತಃ ಹ ವೈ — from where indeed · ಪ್ರಜಾಃ — creatures · ಪ್ರಜಾಯಂತೇ — are born · ಇತಿ — thus ।

ತತ್ — thus / that · ಏವಂ — in this way · ಚಂದ್ರಾದಿತ್ಯ-ಮಿಥುನ-ಆದಿ-ಕ್ರಮೇಣ — through the sequence beginning with the Moon-and-Sun pair · ಅಹೋರಾತ್ರ-ಅಂತೇನ — ending with the day-and-night · ಅನ್ನ-ರೇತಃ-ದ್ವಾರೇಣ — by the gateway of food and seed · ಇಮಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ — these creatures · ಪ್ರಜಾಯಂತೇ — are born · ಇತಿ — thus · ನಿರ್ಣೀತಂ — has been determined / concluded ॥

---

### Anvaya (Prose Order of the Bhāṣya)

The opening sentence of this Bhāṣya requires anvaya treatment — the participial
ಪರಿಣಮ್ಯ (having transformed) is the key to connecting the entire preceding
analysis to the final statement.

---

**Sentence — The culminating identification: food is Prajāpati**

*As written:*
> ಏವಂ ಕ್ರಮೇಣ ಪರಿಣಮ್ಯ — ತತ್ ಅನ್ನಂ ವೈ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ।

*Anvaya (resolved prose order):*
> [ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ] ರಯಿಪ್ರಾಣ-ಮಿಥುನ-ರೂಪಾತ್ [ಆರಭ್ಯ]
> ಸಂವತ್ಸರ-ಮಾಸ-ಪಕ್ಷ-ಅಹೋರಾತ್ರ-ಕ್ರಮೇಣ ಏವಂ ಪರಿಣಮ್ಯ —
> ತತ್ [ಅಂತಿಮಂ ರೂಪಂ] ಅನ್ನಂ [ಅಸ್ತಿ] ।
>
> [ಅತಃ] ಅನ್ನಂ ವೈ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ [ಇತ್ಯುಚ್ಯತೇ] ।

*English:* Prajāpati — having transformed through the sequence beginning from
the Rayi-Prāṇa pair, through Year, Month, Fortnight, and Day-and-Night — that
final form is food. Therefore food is indeed Prajāpati.

*Note:* The word ಏವಂ ಕ್ರಮೇಣ (thus in sequence) is the syntactic bridge that
connects the entire preceding analysis of verses 9–13 to this final statement.
The participial ಪರಿಣಮ್ಯ (having transformed/evolved) is crucial — it confirms
that Prajāpati has not become something other than himself but has evolved
(pariṇāma) into food through the chain of transformations. Ānandagiri makes this
explicit in his anvaya gloss.

## ಟೀಕಾ (Ṭīkā)

> ಏವಂ ಕ್ರಮೇಣ ಇತಿ ।
>
> ರಯಿಪ್ರಾಣಸಂವತ್ಸರಾದಿಕ್ರಮೇಣ ಪರಿಣಮ್ಯ ವ್ರೀಹ್ಯಾದ್ಯಾತ್ಮನಾ ವ್ಯವಸ್ಥಿತಃ ಸನ್ ಅನ್ನಂ ವೈ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ಅನ್ನಾತ್ಮಕಃ ಜಾತಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ಇತಿ ಅನ್ವಯಃ ।
>
> ಕಥಂ ಇತಿ ।
>
> ಅನ್ನರೂಪತ್ವೇಽಪಿ ತಸ್ಯ ಕಥಂ ಪ್ರಜಾಜನಕತ್ವಂ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ।
>
> ತತಃ ಇತಿ ।
>
> ಭಕ್ಷಿತಾತ್ ಅನ್ನಾತ್ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ।
>
> ರೇತಃ ಇತಿ ।
>
> ಶೋಣಿತಸ್ಯ ಅಪಿ ಉಪಲಕ್ಷಣಂ ತುಲ್ಯತ್ವಾತ್ ಇತಿ ॥ ೧೪ ॥

### Overall Translation

On "evaṃ krameṇa" (thus in sequence): Ānandagiri provides the full anvaya.
Having transformed through the sequence of Rayi, Prāṇa, Year, and so on — and
being established in the form of rice and other grains — Prajāpati has become
food-natured. The syntactic connection (anvaya) is: "Prajāpati, having become
food-natured (annātmaka), is food."

On "katham?" (how?): Ānandagiri explains the question being raised: even though
Prajāpati has the form of food, how does he become the generator of creatures?
This is the question Śaṅkara now answers.

On "tataḥ" (from that): Ānandagiri clarifies this means "from the food that has
been eaten" — i.e., it is consumed food that transforms into seed, not food in
an abstract sense.

On "retaḥ" (seed): Ānandagiri states that the word "retas" (male seed) is also
an upalakaṣaṇa (indicative term) for śoṇita (the female generative substance /
blood) — because both are equal in their role as causes of progeny.

### Word-to-Word

ಏವಂ ಕ್ರಮೇಣ ಇತಿ — on the phrase "evaṃ krameṇa (thus in sequence)" ।

ರಯಿಪ್ರಾಣ-ಸಂವತ್ಸರ-ಆದಿ-ಕ್ರಮೇಣ — through the sequence of Rayi, Prāṇa, Year, and so on · ಪರಿಣಮ್ಯ — having transformed / evolved · ವ್ರೀಹ್ಯಾದ್ಯಾತ್ಮನಾ — in the form of rice and other grains · ವ್ಯವಸ್ಥಿತಃ ಸನ್ — being established / situated · ಅನ್ನಂ ವೈ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ — food is indeed Prajāpati · ಅನ್ನಾತ್ಮಕಃ ಜಾತಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ — Prajāpati having become food-natured · ಇತಿ ಅನ್ವಯಃ — thus is the syntactic connection ।

ಕಥಂ ಇತಿ — on the word "katham (how?)" ।

ಅನ್ನರೂಪತ್ವೇ ಅಪಿ — even though having the form of food · ತಸ್ಯ — his / its · ಕಥಂ — how · ಪ್ರಜಾಜನಕತ್ವಂ — the quality of being the generator of creatures · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning (of the question) ।

ತತಃ ಇತಿ — on the word "tataḥ (from that)" ।

ಭಕ್ಷಿತಾತ್ — from what has been eaten / consumed · ಅನ್ನಾತ್ — from food · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

ರೇತಃ ಇತಿ — on the word "retaḥ (seed)" ।

ಶೋಣಿತಸ್ಯ ಅಪಿ — of the female generative substance (śoṇita / blood) too · ಉಪಲಕ್ಷಣಂ — indicative term (standing for a wider class) · ತುಲ್ಯತ್ವಾತ್ — because of being equal (in their role as causes of progeny) · ಇತಿ — thus ॥

## Summary of Mūla, Bhāṣya, and Ṭīkā

| Text | Author | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **ಮೂಲ** (Mūla) | Upaniṣad (Atharva-veda tradition) | The culminating answer to the first Praśna: food is Prajāpati; from food comes seed; from seed creatures are born |
| **ಭಾಷ್ಯ** (Bhāṣya) | Śaṅkarācārya (8th c. CE) | Ties together the entire sequence (Moon-Sun → Year → Month → Fortnight → Day-and-Night → food → seed → creatures); confirms the original question is now answered |
| **ಟೀಕಾ** (Ṭīkā) | Ānandagiri (13th c. CE) | Provides the full anvaya for "evaṃ krameṇa"; clarifies "tataḥ" as consumed food; identifies "retaḥ" as an upalakaṣaṇa for śoṇita (female substance) — both being equal causes |

### Core Philosophical Thread

1. **This verse is the answer** to the opening question of the entire first Praśna:
   "from where are these creatures born?" The answer, reached through six verses
   of analysis, is: from food, which is Prajāpati.
2. **The sequence is complete** — Rayi-Prāṇa → Year → Month → Fortnight →
   Day-and-Night → Food → Seed → Creatures. Prajāpati transforms (pariṇāma)
   through each level without becoming other than himself.
3. **"evaṃ krameṇa pariṇamya"** is the structural hinge of the entire first
   Praśna — it looks back at verses 9–13 and confirms they were all stages in
   one continuous transformation.
4. **Ānandagiri's upalakaṣaṇa note on "retaḥ"** is important for completeness —
   both male seed and female generative substance are equally causes of progeny,
   and the Śruti uses one to indicate both.
5. **The Rayi-Prāṇa dyad is maintained to the end** — food is Rayi (material,
   nourishing), and it is through the Prāṇa-aspect (digestion, transformation,
   vital activity) that food becomes seed and seed becomes creatures.
