# ಪ್ರಶ್ನೋಪನಿಷತ್ — ಪ್ರಥಮ ಪ್ರಶ್ನ, ಮಂತ್ರ ೧೨
# Praśna Upaniṣad — First Question, Verse 12

> **Source of Ṭīkā:** Ānandāśrama Sanskrit Series No. 8 (Ānandagiri on Śaṅkara Bhāṣya)

---

## ಮೂಲ (Mūla)

> ಮಾಸೋ ವೈ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಸ್ತಸ್ಯ ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷ ಏವ ರಯಿಃ ಶುಕ್ಲಃ ಪ್ರಾಣಸ್ತಸ್ಮಾದೇತ ಋಷಯಃ ಶುಕ್ಲ ಇಷ್ಟಂ ಕುರ್ವಂತೀತರ ಇತರಸ್ಮಿನ್ ॥ ೧೨ ॥

### Overall Translation

The Month (Māsa) is indeed Prajāpati. Of it, the dark fortnight (kṛṣṇapakṣa) is
Rayi (the material principle) and the bright fortnight (śuklapakṣa) is Prāṇa
(the life-force). Therefore these seers perform their sacrificial rites in the
bright (fortnight); the others perform in the other (the dark fortnight).

### Word-to-Word

## ಭಾಷ್ಯ (Bhāṣya)

> ಯಸ್ಮಿನ್ನಿದಂ ಪ್ರೋತಂ ವಿಶ್ವಂ ಸ ಏವ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಾಖ್ಯಃ ಸ್ವಾವಯವೇ ಮಾಸೇ ಕೃತ್ಸ್ನಃ ಪರಿಸಮಾಪ್ಯತೇ ।
>
> ಮಾಸೋ ವೈ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ಯಥೋಕ್ತಲಕ್ಷಣ ಏವ ಮಿಥುನಾತ್ಮಕಃ ।
>
> ತಸ್ಯ ಮಾಸಾತ್ಮನಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತೇರೇಕೋ ಭಾಗಃ ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷ ಏವ ರಯಿಃ ಅನ್ನಂ ಚಂದ್ರಮಾಃ ಅಪರೋ ಭಾಗಃ ಶುಕ್ಲಃ ಶುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷಃ ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ಆದಿತ್ಯೋಽತ್ತಾಗ್ನಿಃ ।
>
> ಯಸ್ಮಾಚ್ಛುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷಾತ್ಮಾನಂ ಪ್ರಾಣಂ ಸರ್ವಮೇವ ಪಶ್ಯಂತಿ, ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ಪ್ರಾಣದರ್ಶಿನ ಏತೇ ಋಷಯಃ ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷೇಽಪೀಷ್ಟಂ ಯಾಗಂ ಕುರ್ವಂತಃ ಶುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷ ಏವ ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ ।
>
> ಪ್ರಾಣವ್ಯತಿರೇಕೇಣ ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷಸ್ತೈರ್ನ ದೃಶ್ಯತೇ ಯಸ್ಮಾತ್ ;
>
> ಇತರೇ ತು ಪ್ರಾಣಂ ನ ಪಶ್ಯಂತೀತ್ಯದರ್ಶನಲಕ್ಷಣಂ ಕೃಷ್ಣಾತ್ಮಾನಮೇವ ಪಶ್ಯಂತಿ ।
>
> ಇತರೇ ಇತರಸ್ಮಿನ್ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷ ಏವ ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ ಶುಕ್ಲೇ ಕುರ್ವಂತೋಽಪಿ ॥

### Overall Translation

That very Prajāpati, named the Saṃvatsara (Year), in whom all this world is
strung — he is completely contained within his own limb, the Month (Māsa).

The Month is indeed Prajāpati — with exactly the characteristics as described
(the Year), and of the dyadic nature (mithunātmaka — of the nature of Rayi and
Prāṇa).

Of that Prajāpati whose nature is the Month, one portion is the dark fortnight —
which is Rayi, food, the Moon; the other portion is the bright — the bright
fortnight — which is Prāṇa, the Sun, the eater, the fire.

Because these seers see all as Prāṇa — as the one who is the Self of the bright
fortnight — therefore, these seers who are perceivers of Prāṇa, even when
performing their sacrificial rites (iṣṭa / yāga) in the dark fortnight, perform
them in the bright fortnight alone.

For by them, the dark fortnight is not seen as anything apart from Prāṇa.

The others, however, not seeing Prāṇa — they see only what is of the nature of
darkness (non-perceiving, kṛṣṇa).

The others perform in the dark fortnight alone — even when performing in the
bright.

### Anvaya (Prose Order of the Bhāṣya)

Two sentences in this Bhāṣya require explicit anvaya treatment.

---

**Sentence 1 — Seers perform in the bright even when in the dark**

*As written:*
> ಯಸ್ಮಾಚ್ಛುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷಾತ್ಮಾನಂ ಪ್ರಾಣಂ ಸರ್ವಮೇವ ಪಶ್ಯಂತಿ, ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಪ್ರಾಣದರ್ಶಿನ
> ಏತೇ ಋಷಯಃ ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷೇಽಪಿ ಇಷ್ಟಂ ಯಾಗಂ ಕುರ್ವಂತಃ ಶುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷ ಏವ ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ ।

*Anvaya (resolved prose order):*
> ಏತೇ ಋಷಯಃ ಪ್ರಾಣದರ್ಶಿನಃ [ಸಂತಃ] —
> ಯಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಶುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷ-ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ ಪ್ರಾಣಂ ಸರ್ವಂ ಏವ ಪಶ್ಯಂತಿ —
> ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷೇ ಅಪಿ ಇಷ್ಟಂ ಯಾಗಂ ಕುರ್ವಂತಃ [ಅಪಿ]
> ಶುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷೇ ಏವ ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ ।

*English:* These seers, being perceivers of Prāṇa — because they see Prāṇa (whose
Self is the bright fortnight) as everything — therefore, even while performing the
sacrifice in the dark fortnight, they perform in the bright fortnight alone.

*Note:* The yasmat…tasmāt (because…therefore) frame spans the entire sentence.
The participial ಕುರ್ವಂತಃ (while performing) and the finite verb ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ
(they perform) are both needed — one states the physical occasion (dark fortnight),
the other states the ontological reality (bright fortnight). Ānandagiri flags this
as the core anvaya difficulty.

---

**Sentence 2 — Others perform in the dark even when in the bright**

*As written:*
> ಇತರೇ ಇತರಸ್ಮಿನ್ ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷ ಏವ ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ ಶುಕ್ಲೇ ಕುರ್ವಂತೋಽಪಿ ।

*Anvaya (resolved prose order):*
> ಇತರೇ [ಪ್ರಾಣಂ ನ ಪಶ್ಯಂತಃ] —
> ಶುಕ್ಲೇ [ಪಕ್ಷೇ] ಕುರ್ವಂತಃ ಅಪಿ —
> ಇತರಸ್ಮಿನ್ ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷೇ ಏವ ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ ।

*English:* The others — not seeing Prāṇa — even while performing in the bright
fortnight, perform in the dark fortnight alone.

*Note:* This sentence is the exact parallel and inversion of Sentence 1 — same
structure (participial + finite verb), opposite result. Ānandagiri's Ṭīkā makes
the symmetry explicit: just as knowledge transforms dark into bright for the seers,
ignorance transforms bright into dark for the others.

## ಟೀಕಾ (Ṭīkā)

> ಕಾರಣತ್ವೇ ಶ್ಲೋಕೋಕ್ತಂ ಜಗದಾಶ್ರಯತ್ವಂ ಹೇತುಮಾಹ – ಯಸ್ಮಿನ್ನಿತಿ ।
>
> ಸಂವತ್ಸರಸ್ಯಾಪಿ ಮಾಸಾಹೋರಾತ್ರರೂಪವ್ಯತಿರೇಕೇಣ ಔಷಧ್ಯಾದಿಜನಕತ್ವಾಭಾವಾತ್ ತಸ್ಯ ಮಾಸಾದ್ಯಾತ್ಮಕತ್ವಮಾಹ – ಸ ಏವೇತಿ ।
>
> ಯಥೋಕ್ತೇತಿ ।
>
> ಸಂವತ್ಸರರೂಪಃ ರಯಿಪ್ರಾಣಮಿಥುನಾತ್ಮಕ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ।
>
> ಶುಕ್ಲಕೃಷ್ಣಯೋಃ ಉಭಯೋಃ ಅಪಿ ದರ್ಶಪೂರ್ಣಮಾಸಾದಿಕರ್ಮಾನುಷ್ಠಾನದರ್ಶನಾತ್ ತಸ್ಮಾದೇತ ಋಷಯಃ ಇತ್ಯಾದಿ ವಾಕ್ಯಂ ಅನುಪಪನ್ನಮ್ ಇತ್ಯಾಶಂಕ್ಯ ಶುಕ್ಲಸ್ಯ ಪ್ರಾಣಾತ್ಮತ್ವಜ್ಞಾನಸ್ತುತಿಪರತಯಾ ವ್ಯಾಚಷ್ಟೇ – ಯಸ್ಮಾದಿತಿ ।
>
> ಯಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಪ್ರಾಣಂ ಸರ್ವಮೇವ ಸರ್ವಾತ್ಮಕಮೇವ ಪಶ್ಯಂತಿ ಯಸ್ಮಾಚ್ಚ ಪ್ರಾಣವ್ಯತಿರೇಕೇಣ ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷಃ ತೈಃ ನ ದೃಶ್ಯತೇ ತಸ್ಮಾದ್ ಇತ್ಯನ್ವಯಃ ।
>
> ಪ್ರಾಣಸ್ಯ ಶುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷಾತ್ಮಕತ್ವಾತ್ ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷಾದಿಸರ್ವಜಗತಃ ಪ್ರಾಣಾತ್ಮತ್ವಾತ್ ಪ್ರಾಣದ್ವಾರಾ ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷಸ್ಯಾಪಿ ಶುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷತ್ವೇ ಸತಿ ಕೃಷ್ಣೇ ಕುರ್ವಂತೋಽಪಿ ಪ್ರಕಾಶಾತ್ಮಕೇ ಶುಕ್ಲ ಏವ ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ ಇತಿ ಶುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷೇ ಪ್ರಾಣತ್ವಜ್ಞಾನಸ್ಯ ಸ್ತುತಿಃ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ।
>
> ಏತತ್ ಸ್ತುತ್ಯರ್ಥಮೇವ ಜ್ಞಾನರಹಿತಾನ್ ನಿಂದತಿ – ಇತರೇ ತ್ವಿತಿ ।
>
> ಯೇ ತು ಸರ್ವಾತ್ಮಾನಂ ಪ್ರಾಣಂ ನ ಪಶ್ಯಂತ್ಯಜ್ಞತ್ವಾತ್ ತೇಷಾಂ ಶುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷಃ ಪ್ರಾಣತ್ವೇನ ಅಜ್ಞಾಯಮಾನತ್ವಾತ್ ಅಜ್ಞಾನಾತ್ಮಕಃ ಸನ್ ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷತ್ವಮಾಪದ್ಯತೇ ಅತಃ ಶುಕ್ಲೇ ಕುರ್ವಂತೋಽಪಿ ಅದರ್ಶನಾತ್ಮಕತ್ವಾತ್ ಪ್ರಕಾಶರಹಿತೇ ಕೃಷ್ಣ ಏವ ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ ಇತಿ ತೇ ನಿಂದ್ಯಂತೇ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ।
>
> ಉಕ್ತಮರ್ಥಂ ಶ್ರುತ್ಯಾ ಆರೂಢಂ ಕರೋತಿ – ಇತರ ಇತಿ ॥ ೧೨ ॥

### Overall Translation

Ānandagiri opens by saying that Śaṅkara, in order to state the reason for the
world-support (jagadāśrayatva) mentioned in the śloka as proof of causality
(kāraṇatva), begins with "yasmin" (in whom).

Then, to show that the Year too — without the Month, day, and night — has no
capacity to produce herbs etc., and therefore the Year is of the nature of the
Month etc., Śaṅkara says "sa eva" (that very one). On "yathoktam" (as described):
Ānandagiri explains this means "of the form of the Year, of the nature of the
Rayi-Prāṇa pair."

Ānandagiri then raises an objection: since we see the performance of darśa-
pūrṇamāsa (fortnightly sacrificial rites) and other rites in both the bright and
dark fortnights, the Śruti statement "therefore these seers perform in the bright"
would be contradicted — it would be inconsistent (anupapanna). To resolve this,
Ānandagiri explains that Śaṅkara's passage beginning "yasmāt" is aimed at
praising the knowledge that the bright fortnight is the Self of Prāṇa — not at
making a purely factual claim about when rites are performed.

The anvaya is: because (yasmāt) they see Prāṇa as everything, as the Self of all,
and because (yasmāt) the dark fortnight is not seen by them as anything apart from
Prāṇa — therefore (tasmāt) they perform in the bright alone.

Since Prāṇa is of the nature of the bright fortnight, and since all the world
including the dark fortnight is of the nature of Prāṇa — through Prāṇa, the dark
fortnight too becomes bright. Therefore those performing in the dark are actually
performing in the bright (light-natured) alone. This is the praise (stuti) of the
knowledge that the bright fortnight is Prāṇa.

For the very purpose of this praise, Śaṅkara censures those who lack this
knowledge — with the words "itare tu" (the others however). Those who do not see
Prāṇa as the Self of all — because they do not know the bright fortnight as Prāṇa,
their bright fortnight becomes of the nature of ignorance and takes on the quality
of the dark fortnight. Therefore even while performing in the bright, they perform
in the dark (lightless) alone — and thus they are censured.

Finally, Ānandagiri says Śaṅkara then establishes the stated meaning through the
Śruti itself — with "itara" (the other / dark fortnight).

ಯಥೋಕ್ತ ಇತಿ — on the word "yathoktam (as described)" ।

ಸಂವತ್ಸರರೂಪಃ — of the form of the Year · ರಯಿಪ್ರಾಣ-ಮಿಥುನಾತ್ಮಕಃ — of the nature of the Rayi-Prāṇa pair · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

ಶುಕ್ಲಕೃಷ್ಣಯೋಃ ಉಭಯೋಃ ಅಪಿ — in both the bright and dark (fortnights) · ದರ್ಶಪೂರ್ಣಮಾಸಾದಿ-ಕರ್ಮ-ಅನುಷ್ಠಾನ-ದರ್ಶನಾತ್ — because of observing the performance of darśa-pūrṇamāsa and other rites · ತಸ್ಮಾದೇತ ಋಷಯಃ ಇತ್ಯಾದಿ ವಾಕ್ಯಂ — the statement "therefore these seers" · ಅನುಪಪನ್ನಂ — inconsistent / contradicted · ಇತಿ ಆಶಂಕ್ಯ — anticipating this objection · ಶುಕ್ಲಸ್ಯ — of the bright (fortnight) · ಪ್ರಾಣಾತ್ಮತ್ವ-ಜ್ಞಾನ-ಸ್ತುತಿ-ಪರತಯಾ — with the intent of praising the knowledge that (the bright) is the Self of Prāṇa · ವ್ಯಾಚಷ್ಟೇ — (Śaṅkara) explains · ಯಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಇತಿ — with the word "yasmāt (because)" ।

ಯಸ್ಮಾತ್ — because · ಪ್ರಾಣಂ — Prāṇa · ಸರ್ವಂ ಏವ — as everything · ಸರ್ವಾತ್ಮಕಂ ಏವ — as the Self of all · ಪಶ್ಯಂತಿ — they see · ಯಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಚ — and because · ಪ್ರಾಣ-ವ್ಯತಿರೇಕೇಣ — apart from Prāṇa · ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷಃ — the dark fortnight · ತೈಃ — by them · ನ ದೃಶ್ಯತೇ — is not seen · ತಸ್ಮಾತ್ — therefore · ಇತಿ ಅನ್ವಯಃ — thus is the syntactic connection ।

ಪ್ರಾಣಸ್ಯ — of Prāṇa · ಶುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷ-ಆತ್ಮಕತ್ವಾತ್ — because of being of the nature of the bright fortnight · ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷಾದಿ-ಸರ್ವಜಗತಃ — of all the world including the dark fortnight · ಪ್ರಾಣಾತ್ಮತ್ವಾತ್ — because of being of the nature of Prāṇa · ಪ್ರಾಣದ್ವಾರಾ — through Prāṇa · ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷಸ್ಯ ಅಪಿ — even of the dark fortnight · ಶುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷತ್ವೇ ಸತಿ — when it has the nature of the bright fortnight · ಕೃಷ್ಣೇ ಕುರ್ವಂತಃ ಅಪಿ — even while performing in the dark · ಪ್ರಕಾಶಾತ್ಮಕೇ — in what is of the nature of light · ಶುಕ್ಲೇ ಏವ — in the bright alone · ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ — they perform · ಇತಿ — thus · ಶುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷೇ ಪ್ರಾಣತ್ವ-ಜ್ಞಾನಸ್ಯ — of the knowledge that the bright fortnight is Prāṇa · ಸ್ತುತಿಃ — praise · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

ಏತತ್ — this (praise) · ಸ್ತುತ್ಯರ್ಥಂ ಏವ — for the very purpose of this praise · ಜ್ಞಾನರಹಿತಾನ್ — those devoid of knowledge · ನಿಂದತಿ — censures · ಇತರೇ ತು ಇತಿ — with the words "itare tu (the others however)" ।

ಯೇ ತು — those who however · ಸರ್ವಾತ್ಮಾನಂ ಪ್ರಾಣಂ — Prāṇa as the Self of all · ನ ಪಶ್ಯಂತಿ — do not see · ಅಜ್ಞತ್ವಾತ್ — because of ignorance · ತೇಷಾಂ — for them · ಶುಕ್ಲಪಕ್ಷಃ — the bright fortnight · ಪ್ರಾಣತ್ವೇನ ಅಜ್ಞಾಯಮಾನತ್ವಾತ್ — because it is not known as Prāṇa · ಅಜ್ಞಾನಾತ್ಮಕಃ ಸನ್ — being of the nature of ignorance · ಕೃಷ್ಣಪಕ್ಷತ್ವಂ ಆಪದ್ಯತೇ — takes on the nature of the dark fortnight · ಅತಃ — therefore · ಶುಕ್ಲೇ ಕುರ್ವಂತಃ ಅಪಿ — even while performing in the bright · ಅದರ್ಶನಾತ್ಮಕತ್ವಾತ್ — because of being of the nature of non-seeing / darkness · ಪ್ರಕಾಶರಹಿತೇ — in what is devoid of light · ಕೃಷ್ಣ ಏವ — in the dark alone · ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ — they perform · ಇತಿ — thus · ತೇ ನಿಂದ್ಯಂತೇ — they are censured · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

ಉಕ್ತಂ ಅರ್ಥಂ — the stated meaning · ಶ್ರುತ್ಯಾ — through the Śruti · ಆರೂಢಂ ಕರೋತಿ — establishes / confirms with authority · ಇತರ ಇತಿ — with the word "itara (the other / dark fortnight)" ॥

## Summary of Mūla, Bhāṣya, and Ṭīkā

| Text | Author | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **ಮೂಲ** (Mūla) | Upaniṣad (Atharva-veda tradition) | Applies the Rayi-Prāṇa analysis to the Month — dark fortnight is Rayi, bright is Prāṇa; seers perform in the bright, the others in the dark |
| **ಭಾಷ್ಯ** (Bhāṣya) | Śaṅkarācārya (8th c. CE) | Shows the Month as Prajāpati containing the Year; unpacks dark/bright as Moon/Sun, food/fire; explains that seers who see Prāṇa as all perform in the bright even when physically in the dark — and vice versa for the ignorant |
| **ಟೀಕಾ** (Ṭīkā) | Ānandagiri (13th c. CE) | Resolves the apparent contradiction (anupapanna) that rites are observed in both fortnights; establishes the passage as a stuti (praise) of Prāṇa-knowledge and a nindā (censure) of ignorance; provides the full anvaya for the yasmāt…tasmāt sentence |

### Core Philosophical Thread

1. **The Month is Prajāpati** — it contains the Year completely within itself as
   its own limb (avayava). The Year is non-different from the Month in the same
   way cause and effect are non-different.
2. **Dark fortnight = Rayi = Moon = food; Bright fortnight = Prāṇa = Sun = fire.**
   The Rayi-Prāṇa dyad operates at every level — Year, Month, fortnight.
3. **The key teaching** is epistemological, not ritual: those who see Prāṇa as the
   Self of all transform even the dark into the bright by their knowledge. Those
   who do not see Prāṇa transform even the bright into the dark by their ignorance.
4. **Ānandagiri's key contribution** is identifying the passage as stuti-nindā
   (praise and censure) — a standard Vedic rhetorical form — rather than a factual
   description of when rites are performed. This resolves the apparent contradiction
   that rites are in fact performed in both fortnights.
5. **The anvaya of yasmāt…tasmāt** is the logical spine: knowledge of Prāṇa's
   identity with the bright fortnight means the dark fortnight is never seen as
   other than Prāṇa — hence performance anywhere is performance in the bright.
