## ಭಾಷ್ಯ

<div class="bhasya">
ತಸ್ಮೈ ಏವಂ ಪೃಷ್ಟವತೇ ಸ ಹ ಉವಾಚ ತದಪಾಕರಣಾಯಾಹ — ಪ್ರಜಾಕಾಮಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಆತ್ಮನಃ ಸಿಸೃಕ್ಷುಃ ವೈ, ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿಃ ಸರ್ವಾತ್ಮಾ ಸನ್ ಜಗತ್ಸ್ರಕ್ಷ್ಯಾಮೀತ್ಯೇವಂ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನವಾನ್ಯಥೋಕ್ತಕಾರೀ ತದ್ಭಾವಭಾವಿತಃ ಕಲ್ಪಾದೌ ನಿರ್ವೃತ್ತೋ ಹಿರಣ್ಯಗರ್ಭಃ, ಸೃಜ್ಯಮಾನಾನಾಂ ಪ್ರಜಾನಾಂ ಸ್ಥಾವರಜಂಗಮಾನಾಂ ಪತಿಃ ಸನ್ , ಜನ್ಮಾಂತರಭಾವಿತಂ ಜ್ಞಾನಂ ಶ್ರುತಿಪ್ರಕಾಶಿತಾರ್ಥವಿಷಯಂ ತಪಃ, ಅನ್ವಾಲೋಚಯತ್ ಅತಪ್ಯತ । ಅಥ ತು ಸಃ ಏವಂ ತಪಃ ತಪ್ತ್ವಾ ಶ್ರೌತಂ ಜ್ಞಾನಮನ್ವಾಲೋಚ್ಯ, ಸೃಷ್ಟಿಸಾಧನಭೂತಂ ಮಿಥುನಮ್ ಉತ್ಪಾದಯತೇ ಮಿಥುನಂ ದ್ವಂದ್ವಮುತ್ಪಾದಿತವಾನ್ ರಯಿಂ ಚ ಸೋಮಮನ್ನಂ ಪ್ರಾಣಂ ಚ ಅಗ್ನಿಮತ್ತಾರಮ್ ಇತ್ಯೇತೌ ಅಗ್ನೀಷೋಮೌ ಅತ್ರನ್ನಭೂತೌ ಮೇ ಮಮ ಬಹುಧಾ ಅನೇಕಧಾ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಕರಿಷ್ಯತಃ ಇತಿ ಏವಂ ಸಂಚಿಂತ್ಯ ಅಂಡೋತ್ಪತ್ತಿಕ್ರಮೇಣ ಸೂರ್ಯಾಚಂದ್ರಮಸಾವಕಲ್ಪಯತ್ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
To him who had asked in this manner, Pippalāda replied in order to remove his doubt.

Prajāpati, desiring to create beings from himself, being the Self of all and endowed with the knowledge, "I shall create the universe," and acting in accordance with that knowledge, manifested as Hiraṇyagarbha at the beginning of a cycle of creation. As the lord of all beings that were to be created, both stationary and moving, he contemplated upon tapas, which here means knowledge concerning the truths revealed by the scriptures and carried forward from previous births.

Having thus practiced that tapas, and having reflected upon the scriptural knowledge, he created a pair which would serve as the means of creation. He produced the duality of Rayi and Prāṇa — Rayi being the Moon, food; and Prāṇa being Fire, the eater of food. Thinking, "These two, Agni and Soma, food and eater, will generate for me beings in many forms," he projected the Sun and Moon through the process of cosmic manifestation beginning from the cosmic egg.
</div>
