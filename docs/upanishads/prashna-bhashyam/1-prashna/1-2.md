# Praśna Upaniṣad 1.2

## ಮಂತ್ರ 1.2

<div class="mantra">
ತಾನ್ಹ ಸ ಋಷಿರುವಾಚ ಭೂಯ ಏವ ತಪಸಾ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯೇಣ ಶ್ರದ್ಧಯಾ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಂ ಸಂವತ್ಸ್ಯಥ ಯಥಾಕಾಮಂ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಾನ್ಪೃಚ್ಛತ ಯದಿ ವಿಜ್ಞಾಸ್ಯಾಮಃ ಸರ್ವಂ ಹ ವೋ ವಕ್ಷ್ಯಾಮ ಇತಿ ॥ ೨ ॥

## ಭಾಷ್ಯ 1.1

<div class="bhasya">
ತಾನ್ ಏವಮುಪಗತಾನ್ ಸಃ ಹ ಕಿಲ ಋಷಿಃ ಉವಾಚ ಭೂಯಃ ಪುನರೇವ — ಯದ್ಯಪಿ ಯೂಯಂ ಪೂರ್ವಂ ತಪಸ್ವಿನ ಏವ, ತಥಾಪೀಹ ತಪಸಾ ಇಂದ್ರಿಯಸಂಯಮೇನ ವಿಶೇಷತೋ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯೇಣ ಶ್ರದ್ಧಯಾ ಚ ಆಸ್ತಿಕ್ಯಬುದ್ಧ್ಯಾ ಆದರವಂತಃ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಂ ಕಾಲಂ ಸಂವತ್ಸ್ಯಥ ಸಮ್ಯಗ್ಗುರುಶುಶ್ರೂಷಾಪರಾಃ ಸಂತೋ ವತ್ಸ್ಯಥ । ತತಃ ಯಥಾಕಾಮಂ ಯೋ ಯಸ್ಯ ಕಾಮಸ್ತಮನತಿಕ್ರಮ್ಯ ಯದ್ವಿಷಯೇ ಯಸ್ಯ ಜಿಜ್ಞಾಸಾ ತದ್ವಿಷಯಾನ್ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಾನ್ ಪೃಚ್ಛತ । ಯದಿ ತದ್ಯುಷ್ಮತ್ಪೃಷ್ಟಂ ವಿಜ್ಞಾಸ್ಯಾಮಃ । ಅನುದ್ಧತತ್ವಪ್ರದರ್ಶನಾರ್ಥೋ ಯದಿ - ಶಬ್ದೋ ನಾಜ್ಞಾನಸಂಶಯಾರ್ಥಃ ಪ್ರಶ್ನನಿರ್ಣಯಾದವಸೀಯತೇ ಸರ್ವಂ ಹ ವೋ ವಃ ಪೃಷ್ಟಾರ್ಥಂ ವಕ್ಷ್ಯಾಮ ಇತಿ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
To those who had approached him in that manner, the sage indeed said again:

"Although you are already practitioners of austerity, nevertheless, remain here for a year with discipline — practicing austerity in the form of control of the senses, especially brahmacarya, and faith, that is, a trusting attitude toward the authority of the scriptures and the teacher. Live here with reverence and with wholehearted dedication to serving the teacher.

Thereafter, ask questions according to your respective desires. Whatever subject each of you wishes to know, and whatever inquiry arises in each one's mind, ask questions concerning that very subject.

If we know the answer to what you ask, we shall explain it to you."

The word 'if' (yadi) is used only to indicate the sage's humility and freedom from arrogance; it is not intended to imply ignorance or doubt. This is evident from the fact that all the questions are subsequently answered and resolved.

Thus he says:

"We shall indeed explain to you everything concerning the matters that you ask."
</div>

- ತತ್ → that
- ಯುಷ್ಮತ್-ಪೃಷ್ಟಂ → asked by you
- ಯುಷ್ಮತ್ → by you
- ಪೃಷ್ಟಂ → asked

ಅನುದ್ಧತತ್ವಪ್ರದರ್ಶನಾರ್ಥಃ ಯದಿ-ಶಬ್ದಃ

The word "if" is used to indicate absence of arrogance.

- ಅನುದ್ಧತತ್ವ-ಪ್ರದರ್ಶನಾರ್ಥಃ → for demonstrating humility
- ಅನುದ್ಧತತ್ವ → non-arrogance, humility
- ಪ್ರದರ್ಶನ → showing
- ಅರ್ಥಃ → purpose
- ಯದಿ-ಶಬ್ದಃ → the word "if"

This is an important observation of Śaṅkara.

ನ ಅಜ್ಞಾನಸಂಶಯಾರ್ಥಃ

It is not intended to suggest ignorance or doubt.

- ನ → not
- ಅಜ್ಞಾನ → ignorance
- ಸಂಶಯ → doubt
- ಅರ್ಥಃ → meaning, purpose
- ಪ್ರಶ್ನನಿರ್ಣಯಾತ್ ಅವಸೀಯತೇ

This is understood from the subsequent resolution of the questions.

- ಪ್ರಶ್ನ-ನಿರ್ಣಯಾತ್ → from the determination of the questions
- ಪ್ರಶ್ನ → questions
- ನಿರ್ಣಯ → ascertainment, resolution
- ಅವಸೀಯತೇ → is understood, is concluded
