# Praśna Upaniṣad 1.3

## ಭಾಷ್ಯ 1.1

<div class="bhasya">
ಅಥ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಾದೂರ್ಧ್ವಂ ಕಬಂಧೀ ಕಾತ್ಯಾಯನಃ ಉಪೇತ್ಯ ಉಪಗಮ್ಯ ಪಪ್ರಚ್ಛ ಪೃಷ್ಟವಾನ್ — ಹೇ ಭಗವನ್ , ಕುತಃ ಕಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಹ ವೈ ಇಮಾಃ ಬ್ರಾಹ್ಮಣಾದ್ಯಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಯಂತೇ ಉತ್ಪದ್ಯಂತೇ ಇತಿ । ಅಪರವಿದ್ಯಾಕರ್ಮಣೋಃ ಸಮುಚ್ಚಿತಯೋರ್ಯತ್ಕಾರ್ಯಂ ಯಾ ಗತಿಸ್ತದ್ವಕ್ತವ್ಯಮಿತಿ ತದರ್ಥೋಽಯಂ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಃ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
Then, after the completion of the year, Kabandhī Kātyāyana approached the teacher and asked:

"O Revered Sir, from what cause indeed are these beings, beginning with the brāhmaṇas, born?"

This question is intended to elicit an explanation of the result and destination attained through the combined pursuit of aparā vidyā (lower knowledge) and karma (Vedic rituals).
</div>

ಅಥ ಸಂವತ್ಸರಾದೂರ್ಧ್ವಂ

Then, after the completion of the year,

- ಅಥ → then
- ಸಂವತ್ಸರಾತ್ → from the year, after the year
- ಊರ್ಧ್ವಂ → afterwards, subsequently

ಕಬಂಧೀ ಕಾತ್ಯಾಯನಃ ಉಪೇತ್ಯ ಉಪಗಮ್ಯ

Kabandhī Kātyāyana, having approached the teacher,

- ಕಬಂಧೀ → Kabandhī
- ಕಾತ್ಯಾಯನಃ → descendant of Kātyāyana
- ಉಪೇತ್ಯ → having approached
- ಉಪಗಮ್ಯ → having come near

Śaṅkara uses both expressions simply to emphasize respectful approach to the teacher.

ಪಪ್ರಚ್ಛ ಪೃಷ್ಟವಾನ್

Asked, inquired

- ಪಪ್ರಚ್ಛ → asked
- ಪೃಷ್ಟವಾನ್ → one who questioned

ಹೇ ಭಗವನ್

O Revered Sir,

- ಹೇ → O!
- ಭಗವನ್ → venerable master

ಕುತಃ ಕಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಹ ವೈ

From what cause indeed?

- ಕುತಃ → from where
- ಕಸ್ಮಾತ್ → from what cause
- ಹ ವೈ → indeed, verily

Śaṅkara glosses ಕುತಃ with ಕಸ್ಮಾತ್, making it clear that the inquiry concerns the cause of creation.

ಇಮಾಃ ಬ್ರಾಹ್ಮಣಾದ್ಯಾಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಃ

These beings, beginning with the brāhmaṇas,

- ಇಮಾಃ → these
- ಬ್ರಾಹ್ಮಣ-ಆದ್ಯಾಃ → beginning with the brāhmaṇas
- ಬ್ರಾಹ್ಮಣ → brāhmaṇa
- ಆದ್ಯಾಃ → and others, beginning with
- ಪ್ರಜಾಃ → creatures, beings

Here "brāhmaṇas and others" stands for all embodied beings.

ಪ್ರಜಾಯಂತೇ ಉತ್ಪದ್ಯಂತೇ

Are born, come into existence

- ಪ್ರಜಾಯಂತೇ → are born
- ಉತ್ಪದ್ಯಂತೇ → arise, are produced

The second word is explanatory of the first.

ಅಪರವಿದ್ಯಾಕರ್ಮಣೋಃ ಸಮುಚ್ಚಿತಯೋಃ

Of aparā vidyā and karma taken together,

- ಅಪರ-ವಿದ್ಯಾ → lower knowledge (primarily Vedic learning)
- ಕರ್ಮಣೋಃ → of karma
- ಸಮುಚ್ಚಿತಯೋಃ → when combined together

This is a very important phrase.

ಯತ್ ಕಾರ್ಯಂ

What result is produced,

- ಯತ್ → whatever
- ಕಾರ್ಯಂ → effect, result

ಯಾ ಗತಿಃ

What destination is attained,

- ಯಾ → what
- ಗತಿಃ → destination, course, attainment

ತತ್ ವಕ್ತವ್ಯಂ ಇತಿ

That is what is to be explained.

- ತತ್ → that
- ವಕ್ತವ್ಯಂ → should be explained
- ಇತಿ → thus

ತದರ್ಥಃ ಅಯಂ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಃ

Such is the intention of this question.

- ತದರ್ಥಃ → having that purpose
- ಅಯಂ → this
- ಪ್ರಶ್ನಃ → question


Important Bhāṣya Point

At first glance, Kabandhī appears to be asking a simple cosmological question:

"From where are beings born?"

But Śaṅkara sees a deeper intention.

The question is really:

"What is the origin, result, and destination of the world experienced by those who pursue Vedic learning and ritual action?"

In other words, the inquiry is not merely about physical birth. It is about the creation-order (sṛṣṭi) that forms the field in which karma operates and produces results.

Therefore Śaṅkara remarks:

ಅಪರವಿದ್ಯಾಕರ್ಮಣೋಃ ಸಮುಚ್ಚಿತಯೋಃ ಯತ್ ಕಾರ್ಯಂ ಯಾ ಗತಿಃ

"The question seeks to know the result and destination associated with the combined pursuit of aparā vidyā and karma."

This prepares the way for Pippalāda's answer concerning Prajāpati, Rayi, and Prāṇa, which explains the cosmic order governing worldly existence.
