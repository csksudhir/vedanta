# Praśna Upaniṣad 1.7

## ಮಂತ್ರ

<div class="mantra">
ಸ ಏಷ ವೈಶ್ವಾನರೋ ವಿಶ್ವರೂಪಃ ಪ್ರಾಣೋಽಗ್ನಿರುದಯತೇ । ತದೇತದೃಚಾಭ್ಯುಕ್ತಮ್ ॥ ೭ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
This very Prāṇa, rising as the Sun, is Vaiśvānara, the cosmic Fire, of universal form. Concerning this, the following Ṛg-vedic mantra has been declared.
</div>

### ಮಂತ್ರ ಪದಾರ್ಥ

ಸಃ ಏಷಃ

This very one

- ಸಃ → he
- ಏಷಃ → this very one

ವೈಶ್ವಾನರಃ

Vaiśvānara, the universal Fire

- ವೈಶ್ವಾನರಃ → the cosmic fire, Vaiśvānara

ವಿಶ್ವರೂಪಃ

Of universal form

- ವಿಶ್ವ → all, universe
- ರೂಪಃ → form

ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ಅಗ್ನಿಃ ಉದಯತೇ

Prāṇa, as Fire, rises

- ಪ್ರಾಣಃ → Prāṇa
- ಅಗ್ನಿಃ → Fire
- ಉದಯತೇ → rises

ತತ್ ಏತತ್ ಋಚಾ ಅಭ್ಯುಕ್ತಮ್

This has been declared by a Vedic verse

- ತತ್ → that
- ಏತತ್ → this
- ಋಚಾ → by a Ṛg-vedic mantra
- ಅಭ್ಯುಕ್ತಮ್ → has been stated, declared

## ಭಾಷ್ಯ

<div class="bhasya">
ಸ ಏಷಃ ಅತ್ತಾ ಪ್ರಾಣೋ ವೈಶ್ವಾನರಃ ಸರ್ವಾತ್ಮಾ ವಿಶ್ವರೂಪಃ ವಿಶ್ವಾತ್ಮತ್ವಾಚ್ಚ ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ಅಗ್ನಿಶ್ಚ ಸ ಏವಾತ್ತಾ ಉದಯತೇ ಉದ್ಗಚ್ಛತಿ ಪ್ರತ್ಯಹಂ ಸರ್ವಾ ದಿಶಃ ಆತ್ಮಸಾತ್ಕುರ್ವನ್ । ತದೇತತ್ ಉಕ್ತಂ ವಸ್ತು ಋಚಾ ಮಂತ್ರೇಣಾಪಿ ಅಭ್ಯುಕ್ತಮ್ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
This very Prāṇa, the eater, is Vaiśvānara, the Self of all, possessing the form of the entire universe. Because he is the Self of all, Prāṇa and Fire are one and the same. That very eater rises every day, ascending while bringing all directions under his sway.

This very truth that has been explained is also declared in a Ṛg-vedic mantra.
</div>

### ಭಾಷ್ಯ ಪದಾರ್ಥ

ಸಃ ಏಷಃ ಅತ್ತಾ ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ವೈಶ್ವಾನರಃ

This very Prāṇa, the eater, is Vaiśvānara

- ಸಃ → he
- ಏಷಃ → this very one
- ಅತ್ತಾ → eater
- ಪ್ರಾಣಃ → Prāṇa
- ವೈಶ್ವಾನರಃ → Vaiśvānara, the universal Fire

ಸರ್ವಾತ್ಮಾ ವಿಶ್ವರೂಪಃ

The Self of all, possessing universal form

- ಸರ್ವ → all
- ಆತ್ಮಾ → Self
- ವಿಶ್ವ → universe
- ರೂಪಃ → form

ವಿಶ್ವಾತ್ಮತ್ವಾತ್ ಚ

And because he is the Self of the universe

- ವಿಶ್ವ → universe
- ಆತ್ಮತ್ವಾತ್ → because of being the Self
- ಚ → and

ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ಅಗ್ನಿಃ ಚ

Prāṇa and Fire

- ಪ್ರಾಣಃ → Prāṇa
- ಅಗ್ನಿಃ → Fire
- ಚ → and

ಸಃ ಏವ ಅತ್ತಾ

That very one is the eater

- ಸಃ → he
- ಏವ → indeed
- ಅತ್ತಾ → eater

ಉದಯತೇ ಉದ್ಗಚ್ಛತಿ

Rises and ascends

- ಉದಯತೇ → rises
- ಉದ್ಗಚ್ಛತಿ → ascends

ಪ್ರತ್ಯಹಂ

Every day

- ಪ್ರತಿ → each
- ಅಹಂ → day

ಸರ್ವಾಃ ದಿಶಃ ಆತ್ಮಸಾತ್ಕುರ್ವನ್

Bringing all directions under himself

- ಸರ್ವಾಃ → all
- ದಿಶಃ → directions
- ಆತ್ಮಸಾತ್-ಕುರ್ವನ್ → making his own, bringing under his control

ತತ್ ಏತತ್ ಉಕ್ತಂ ವಸ್ತು

This very truth that has been taught

- ತತ್ → that
- ಏತತ್ → this
- ಉಕ್ತಂ → spoken, taught
- ವಸ್ತು → reality, subject matter

ಋಚಾ ಮಂತ್ರೇಣ ಅಪಿ ಅಭ್ಯುಕ್ತಮ್

Has also been declared by a Vedic mantra

- ಋಚಾ → by a Ṛg-vedic verse
- ಮಂತ್ರೇಣ → by a mantra
- ಅಪಿ → also
- ಅಭ್ಯುಕ್ತಮ್ → declared, stated
