# ಪ್ರಶ್ನೋಪನಿಷತ್ — ದ್ವಿತೀಯ ಪ್ರಶ್ನ, ಮಂತ್ರ ೩
# Praśna Upaniṣad — Second Question, Verse 3

> **Source of Ṭīkā:** Ānandāśrama Sanskrit Series No. 8 (Ānandagiri on Śaṅkara Bhāṣya)

---

## ಮೂಲ (Mūla)

> ತಾನ್ ವರಿಷ್ಠಃ ಪ್ರಾಣ ಉವಾಚ ಮಾ ಮೋಹಮಾಪದ್ಯಥ ।
>
> ಅಹಮೇವೈತತ್ ಪಂಚಧಾ ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ ಪ್ರವಿಭಜ್ಯ ಏತದ್ಬಾಣಮವಷ್ಟಭ್ಯ ವಿಧಾರಯಾಮಿ ।
>
> ಇತಿ ತೇ ಅಶ್ರದ್ದಧಾನಾ ಬಭೂವುಃ ॥ ೩ ॥

### Overall Translation

To them the foremost — Prāṇa — said: "Do not fall into delusion. I alone,
having divided myself fivefold, sustain this body (bāṇa) by holding it firmly."

At this, they became disbelieving.

### Word-to-Word

ತಾನ್ — to them · ವರಿಷ್ಠಃ — the foremost · ಪ್ರಾಣಃ — Prāṇa · ಉವಾಚ — said · ಮಾ — do not · ಮೋಹಂ ಆಪದ್ಯಥ — fall into delusion ।

ಅಹಂ ಏವ — I alone · ಏತತ್ — this · ಪಂಚಧಾ — fivefold · ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ ಪ್ರವಿಭಜ್ಯ — having divided myself · ಏತತ್ ಬಾಣಂ — this body · ಅವಷ್ಟಭ್ಯ — having held firmly · ವಿಧಾರಯಾಮಿ — I sustain ।

ಇತಿ — thus · ತೇ — they · ಅಶ್ರದ್ದಧಾನಾಃ — disbelieving · ಬಭೂವುಃ — became ॥

---

## ಭಾಷ್ಯ (Bhāṣya)

> ತಾನ್ ಏವಂ ಅಭಿಮಾನವತಃ ವರಿಷ್ಠಃ ಮುಖ್ಯಃ ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ಉವಾಚ ಉಕ್ತವಾನ್ — ಮಾ ಮ್ ಏವಂ ಮೋಹಂ ಆಪದ್ಯಥ ಅವಿವೇಕಿತಯಾ ಅಭಿಮಾನಂ ಮಾ ಕುರುತ ।
>
> ಯಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಅಹಂ ಏವ ಏತದ್ ಬಾಣಂ ಅವಷ್ಟಭ್ಯ ವಿಧಾರಯಾಮಿ ಪಂಚಧಾ ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ ಪ್ರವಿಭಜ್ಯ ಪ್ರಾಣ-ಆದಿ-ವೃತ್ತಿ-ಭೇದಂ ಸ್ವಸ್ಯ ಕೃತ್ವಾ ವಿಧಾರಯಾಮಿ ।
>
> ಇತಿ ಉಕ್ತವತಿ ಚ ತಸ್ಮಿನ್ ತೇ ಅಶ್ರದ್ದಧಾನಾಃ ಅಪ್ರತ್ಯಯವಂತಃ ಬಭೂವುಃ — ಕಥಮ್ ಏತದ್ ಏವಂ ಇತಿ ॥

### Overall Translation

To those who were thus full of pride, the foremost — the principal Prāṇa — said:
"Do not fall into delusion — do not, through lack of discrimination, make this
proud claim.

For I alone sustain this body — having divided myself fivefold, having made the
distinctions of prāṇa and the other functions my own, I sustain it."

When he had said this, they became disbelieving, without conviction —
thinking "how can this be so?"

### Word-to-Word

ತಾನ್ — to them · ಏವಂ ಅಭಿಮಾನವತಃ — who were thus full of pride · ವರಿಷ್ಠಃ ಮುಖ್ಯಃ — the foremost / the principal · ಪ್ರಾಣಃ ಉವಾಚ — Prāṇa said · ಮಾ ಮ್ ಏವಂ — do not thus · ಮೋಹಂ ಆಪದ್ಯಥ — fall into delusion · ಅವಿವೇಕಿತಯಾ — through lack of discrimination · ಅಭಿಮಾನಂ ಮಾ ಕುರುತ — do not make this proud claim ।

ಯಸ್ಮಾತ್ — because · ಅಹಂ ಏವ — I alone · ಏತದ್ ಬಾಣಂ — this body · ಅವಷ್ಟಭ್ಯ ವಿಧಾರಯಾಮಿ — sustain by holding firmly · ಪಂಚಧಾ — fivefold · ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ ಪ್ರವಿಭಜ್ಯ — having divided myself · ಪ್ರಾಣ-ಆದಿ-ವೃತ್ತಿ-ಭೇದಂ — distinctions of prāṇa and other functions · ಸ್ವಸ್ಯ ಕೃತ್ವಾ — having made as my own · ವಿಧಾರಯಾಮಿ — I sustain ।

ಇತಿ ಉಕ್ತವತಿ ಚ ತಸ್ಮಿನ್ — when he had said this · ತೇ — they · ಅಶ್ರದ್ದಧಾನಾಃ — disbelieving · ಅಪ್ರತ್ಯಯವಂತಃ — without conviction · ಬಭೂವುಃ — became · ಕಥಂ ಏತದ್ ಏವಂ ಇತಿ — "how can this be so?" ॥

---

## ಟೀಕಾ (Ṭīkā)

> ಕಥಂ ಏತದ್ ಇತಿ ।
>
> ಏತದ್ ಅಹಂ ಏವ ಏತದ್ ಇತಿ ಆದ್ಯ-ಉಕ್ತಂ ।
>
> ಏವಂ ಯಥಾಭೂತಂ ಕಥಂ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ॥ ೩ ॥

### Overall Translation

On "katham etat" (how can this be so?): Ānandagiri explains that what is referred
to is what was stated earlier — "I alone sustain this" etc. The meaning is:
"how can what was stated thus be as described?" — the other faculties could not
believe Prāṇa's claim.

### Word-to-Word

ಕಥಂ ಏತದ್ ಇತಿ — on "katham etat (how can this be so?)" ।

ಏತದ್ ಅಹಂ ಏವ ಏತದ್ ಇತಿ ಆದ್ಯ-ಉಕ್ತಂ — what was stated earlier as "I alone sustain this" ।

ಏವಂ ಯಥಾಭೂತಂ ಕಥಂ — how can what was stated thus be as described? · ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ॥

## Summary of Mūla, Bhāṣya, and Ṭīkā

| Text | Author | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **ಮೂಲ** (Mūla) | Upaniṣad (Atharva-veda tradition) | Prāṇa, the foremost, claims sole supremacy — having divided itself fivefold it alone sustains the body; the others are unconvinced |
| **ಭಾṣya** (Bhāṣya) | Śaṅkarācārya (8th c. CE) | Explains Prāṇa's claim precisely — the fivefold division is into prāṇa, apāna and the other functions; their disbelief is expressed as "how can this be so?" |
| **ಟೀಕಾ** (Ṭīkā) | Ānandagiri (13th c. CE) | Identifies "katham etat" as referring back to Prāṇa's specific claim of sole sustaining |
