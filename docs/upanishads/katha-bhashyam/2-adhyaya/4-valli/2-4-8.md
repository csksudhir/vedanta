# Kaṭha Upaniṣad 2.4.8

## ಮಂತ್ರ

<div class="mantra">
ಅರಣ್ಯೋರ್ನಿರ್ಹಿತೋ ಜಾತವೇದಾ ಗರ್ಭ ಇವ ಸುಭೃತೋ ಗರ್ಭಿಣೀಭಿಃ ।
ದಿವೇ ದಿವ ಈಡ್ಯೋ ಜಾಗೃವದ್ಭಿರ್ಹವಿಷ್ಮದ್ಭಿರ್ಮನುಷ್ಯೇಭಿರಗ್ನಿಃ । ಏತದ್ವೈ ತತ್ ॥ ೮ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
Agni, the all-knowing fire, lies hidden in the two araṇi sticks, carefully preserved like an embryo by pregnant women. Day after day, he is worshipped by vigilant men who offer oblations. Verily, this is That.
</div>

## ಭಾಷ್ಯ

<div class="bhasya">
ಕಿಂಚ, ಯಃ ಅಧಿಯಜ್ಞಮ್ ಉತ್ತರಾಧರಾರಣ್ಯೋರ್ನಿಹಿತಃ ಸ್ಥಿತಃ ಜಾತವೇದಾ ಅಗ್ನಿಃ ಪುನಃ ಸರ್ವಹವಿಷಾಂ ಭೋಕ್ತಾ ಅಧ್ಯಾತ್ಮಂ ಚ ಯೋಗಿಭಿಃ, ಗರ್ಭ ಇವ ಗರ್ಭಿಣೀಭಿಃ ಅಂತರ್ವತ್ನೀಭಿರಗರ್ಹಿತಾನ್ನಭೋಜನಾದಿನಾ ಯಥಾ ಗರ್ಭಃ ಸುಭೃತಃ ಸುಷ್ಠು ಸಮ್ಯಗ್ಭೃತೋ ಲೋಕೇ, ಇತ್ಥಮೇವ ಋತ್ವಿಗ್ಭಿರ್ಯೋಗಿಭಿಶ್ಚ ಸುಭೃತ ಇತ್ಯೇತತ್ । ಕಿಂಚ, ದಿವೇ ದಿವೇ ಅಹನ್ಯಹನಿ ಈಡ್ಯಃ ಸ್ತುತ್ಯೋ ವಂದ್ಯಶ್ಚ ಕರ್ಮಿಭಿರ್ಯೋಗಿಭಿಶ್ಚಾಧ್ವರೇ ಹೃದಯೇ ಚ ಜಾಗೃವದ್ಭಿಃ ಜಾಗರಣಶೀಲೈಃ ಅಪ್ರಮತ್ತೈರಿತ್ಯೇತತ್ । ಹವಿಷ್ಮದ್ಭಿಃ ಆಜ್ಯಾದಿಮದ್ಭಿಃ ಧ್ಯಾನಭಾವನಾವದ್ಭಿಶ್ಚ ಮನುಷ್ಯೇಭಿಃ ಮನುಷ್ಯೈಃ ಅಗ್ನಿಃ ; ಏತದ್ವೈ ತತ್ ತದೇವ ಪ್ರಕೃತಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
Further, that Agni, known as Jātavedas, who in the sacrificial context is hidden and established in the upper and lower araṇi sticks, and who is the enjoyer of all sacrificial offerings, is also realized inwardly by yogins.

Just as an embryo is carefully nourished and protected by pregnant women through proper food and conduct, so too this Agni is carefully sustained by priests through ritual and by yogins through contemplation.

Moreover, day after day he is worthy of praise and worship by ritualists in the sacrifice and by yogins in the heart. He is worshipped by vigilant and attentive persons who are free from negligence.

He is served by people possessing offerings such as ghee and other sacrificial materials, and also by those endowed with meditation and contemplation.

Verily, this Agni is that very Brahman which is the subject under discussion.
</div>
