# Kaṭha Upaniṣad 2.4.5

## ಮಂತ್ರ

<div class="mantra">
ಯ ಇಮಂ ಮಧ್ವದಂ ವೇದ ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ ಜೀವಮಂತಿಕಾತ್ ।
ಈಶಾನಂ ಭೂತಭವ್ಯಸ್ಯ ನ ತತೋ ವಿಜುಗುಪ್ಸತೇ । ಏತದ್ವೈ ತತ್ ॥ ೫ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
He who knows this Self as the enjoyer of the fruits of actions, as the living principle dwelling near (in the body), and as the Lord of the past and the future, does not seek to protect himself from anything thereafter. Verily, this is That.
</div>

- ಜೀವಂ → the living being, life-principle
- ಅಂತಿಕಾತ್ → near, dwelling within the body

## ಭಾಷ್ಯ

<div class="bhasya">
ಕಿಂಚ, ಯಃ ಕಶ್ಚಿತ್ ಇಮಂ ಮಧ್ವದಂ ಕರ್ಮಫಲಭುಜಂ ಜೀವಂ ಪ್ರಾಣಾದಿಕಲಾಪಸ್ಯ ಧಾರಯಿತಾರಮಾತ್ಮಾನಂ ವೇದ ವಿಜಾನಾತಿ ಅಂತಿಕಾತ್ ಅಂತಿಕೇ ಸಮೀಪೇ ಈಶಾನಮ್ ಈಶಿತಾರಂ ಭೂತಭವ್ಯಸ್ಯ ಕಾಲತ್ರಯಸ್ಯ, ತತಃ ತದ್ವಿಜ್ಞಾನಾದೂರ್ಧ್ವಮಾತ್ಮಾನಂ ನ ವಿಜುಗುಪ್ಸತೇ ನ ಗೋಪಾಯಿತುಮಿಚ್ಛತಿ, ಅಭಯಪ್ರಾಪ್ತತ್ವಾತ್ । ಯಾವದ್ಧಿ ಭಯಮಧ್ಯಸ್ಥೋಽನಿತ್ಯಮಾತ್ಮಾನಂ ಮನ್ಯತೇ ತಾವದ್ಗೋಪಾಯಿತುಮಿಚ್ಛತ್ಯಾತ್ಮಾನಮ್ । ಯದಾ ತು ನಿತ್ಯಮದ್ವೈತಮಾತ್ಮಾನಂ ವಿಜಾನಾತಿ, ತದಾ ಕಃ ಕಿಂ ಕುತೋ ವಾ ಗೋಪಾಯಿತುಮಿಚ್ಛೇತ್ । ಏತದ್ವೈ ತದಿತಿ ಪೂರ್ವವತ್ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
Further, whoever knows this Self as the enjoyer of the fruits of actions, as the living principle, as the sustainer of the aggregate of prāṇas and senses, as dwelling nearby in the body, and as the ruler of the past, present, and future — after gaining this knowledge, such a person no longer seeks to protect himself, for he has attained fearlessness.

So long as a person remains subject to fear and regards the Self as impermanent, he seeks to protect himself. But when he realizes the Self as eternal and non-dual, then who would protect whom, from what, and for what reason?

“Verily, this is That” — as explained before.
</div>

- ಪ್ರಾಣಾದಿ → prāṇa and the rest
