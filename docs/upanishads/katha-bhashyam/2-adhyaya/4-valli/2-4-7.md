# Kaṭha Upaniṣad 2.4.7

## ಮಂತ್ರ

<div class="mantra">
ಯಾ ಪ್ರಾಣೇನ ಸಂಭವತಿ ಅದಿತಿರ್ದೇವತಾಮಯೀ ।
ಗುಹಾಂ ಪ್ರವಿಶ್ಯ ತಿಷ್ಠಂತೀಂ ಯಾ ಭೂತೇಭಿರ್ವ್ಯಜಾಯತ । ಏತದ್ವೈ ತತ್ ॥ ೭ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
That Aditi, who is constituted of all the deities, who manifests along with Prāṇa, who enters and abides in the cave of the heart, and who appears through the elements—he who realizes Her thus, realizes That. Verily, this is That.
</div>

### ಮಂತ್ರ ಪದಾರ್ಥ

ಯಾ

Who

- ಯಾ → who, which

ಪ್ರಾಣೇನ ಸಂಭವತಿ

Comes forth together with Prāṇa

- ಪ್ರಾಣೇನ → along with Prāṇa
- ಸಂಭವತಿ → arises, manifests

ಅದಿತಿಃ ದೇವತಾಮಯೀ

Aditi, consisting of all the deities

- ಅದಿತಿಃ → Aditi
- ದೇವತಾ-ಮಯೀ → constituted of the deities

ಗುಹಾಂ ಪ್ರವಿಶ್ಯ ತಿಷ್ಠಂತೀಂ

Having entered the cave (of the heart), abiding there

- ಗುಹಾಂ → the cave, the heart (Jiva tadatmya)
- ಪ್ರವಿಶ್ಯ → having entered
- ತಿಷ್ಠಂತೀಂ → abiding

ಯಾ ಭೂತೇಭಿಃ ವ್ಯಜಾಯತ

Who manifested through the elements

- ಯಾ → who
- ಭೂತೇಭಿಃ → through the elements
- ವ್ಯಜಾಯತ → manifested, became differentiated

ಏತತ್ ವೈ ತತ್

- ಏತತ್ → this
- ವೈ → indeed
- ತತ್ → That (Brahman)

## ಭಾಷ್ಯ

<div class="bhasya">
ಕಿಂಚ, ಯಾ ಸರ್ವದೇವತಾಮಯೀ ಸರ್ವದೇವತಾತ್ಮಿಕಾ ಪ್ರಾಣೇನ ಹಿರಣ್ಯಗರ್ಭರೂಪೇಣ ಪರಸ್ಮಾದ್ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಣಃ ಸಂಭವತಿ ಶಬ್ದಾದೀನಾಮದನಾತ್ ಅದಿತಿಃ ತಾಂ ಪೂರ್ವವದ್ಗುಹಾಂ ಪ್ರವಿಶ್ಯ ತಿಷ್ಠಂತೀಮ್ ಅದಿತಿಮ್ । ತಾಮೇವ ವಿಶಿನಷ್ಟಿ — ಯಾ ಭೂತೇಭಿಃ ಭೂತೈಃ ಸಮನ್ವಿತಾ ವ್ಯಜಾಯತ ಉತ್ಪನ್ನೇತ್ಯೇತತ್ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
Further, that Aditi, who is of the nature of all the deities and constituted by all the deities, manifests from the Supreme Brahman in the form of Hiraṇyagarbha associated with Prāṇa. She is called Aditi because she consumes (or receives) sound and the other sense objects.

That very Aditi, having entered the cave of the heart as explained before, abides there.

The mantra further specifies her nature: she is the one who manifested together with the elements; that is, she came into existence associated with the elements.
</div>

### ಭಾಷ್ಯ ಪದಾರ್ಥ

ಕಿಂಚ

Further

- ಕಿಂಚ → moreover, further

ಯಾ ಸರ್ವದೇವತಾಮಯೀ

Who is constituted of all the deities

- ಯಾ → who
- ಸರ್ವ → all
- ದೇವತಾ → deities
- ಮಯೀ → consisting of, constituted of

ಸರ್ವದೇವತಾತ್ಮಿಕಾ

Whose nature is all the deities

- ಸರ್ವ → all
- ದೇವತಾ → deities
- ಆತ್ಮಿಕಾ → having the nature of

ಪ್ರಾಣೇನ ಹಿರಣ್ಯಗರ್ಭರೂಪೇಣ

In the form of Hiraṇyagarbha associated with Prāṇa

- ಪ್ರಾಣೇನ → with Prāṇa
- ಹಿರಣ್ಯಗರ್ಭ → Hiraṇyagarbha
- ರೂಪೇಣ → in the form of

ಪರಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಣಃ ಸಂಭವತಿ

Arises from the Supreme Brahman

- ಪರಸ್ಮಾತ್ → from the Supreme
- ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಣಃ → Brahman
- ಸಂಭವತಿ → manifests, arises

ಶಬ್ದಾದೀನಾಂ ಅದನಾತ್ ಅದಿತಿಃ

She is called Aditi because she consumes sound and other objects

- ಶಬ್ದಾದೀನಾಂ → sound and other sense objects
- ಅದನಾತ್ → because of consuming
- ಅದಿತಿಃ → Aditi

ತಾಂ ಪೂರ್ವವತ್ ಗುಹಾಂ ಪ್ರವಿಶ್ಯ ತಿಷ್ಠಂತೀಂ ಅದಿತಿಂ

That Aditi who, as explained before, enters the cave and abides there

- ತಾಂ → that
- ಪೂರ್ವವತ್ → as before
- ಗುಹಾಂ → the cave of the heart
- ಪ್ರವಿಶ್ಯ → having entered
- ತಿಷ್ಠಂತೀಂ → abiding
- ಅದಿತಿಂ → Aditi

ತಾಮೇವ ವಿಶಿನಷ್ಟಿ

The mantra further specifies her

- ತಾಂ ಏವ → that very one
- ವಿಶಿನಷ್ಟಿ → distinguishes, explains further

ಯಾ ಭೂತೇಭಿಃ ಭೂತೈಃ ಸಮನ್ವಿತಾ

Who is associated with the elements

- ಯಾ → who
- ಭೂತೇಭಿಃ → with the elements
- ಭೂತೈಃ → by the elements
- ಸಮನ್ವಿತಾ → associated, accompanied

ವ್ಯಜಾಯತ ಉತ್ಪನ್ನಾ

Manifested, came into existence

- ವ್ಯಜಾಯತ → manifested
- ಉತ್ಪನ್ನಾ → arose, came into being

ಇತಿ ಏತತ್

This is the meaning

- ಇತಿ → thus
- ಏತತ್ → this
