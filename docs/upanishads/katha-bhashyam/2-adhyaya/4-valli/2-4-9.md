# Kaṭha Upaniṣad 2.4.9

## ಮಂತ್ರ

<div class="mantra">
ಯತಶ್ಚೋದೇತಿ ಸೂರ್ಯಃ ಅಸ್ತಂ ಯತ್ರ ಚ ಗಚ್ಛತಿ ।
ತಂ ದೇವಾಃ ಸರ್ವೇ ಅರ್ಪಿತಾಸ್ತದು ನಾತ್ಯೇತಿ ಕಶ್ಚನ । ಏತದ್ವೈ ತತ್ ॥ ೯ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
From whom the Sun rises, and into whom it sets; upon whom all the deities depend; whom none can ever transcend—verily, this is That.
</div>

### ಮಂತ್ರ ಪದಾರ್ಥ

ಯತಃ ಚ ಉದೇತಿ ಸೂರ್ಯಃ

From whom the Sun rises

- ಯತಃ → from whom
- ಚ → and
- ಉದೇತಿ → rises
- ಸೂರ್ಯಃ → the Sun

ಅಸ್ತಂ ಯತ್ರ ಚ ಗಚ್ಛತಿ

And into whom it sets

- ಅಸ್ತಂ → setting, disappearance
- ಯತ್ರ → into whom, wherein
- ಚ → and
- ಗಚ್ಛತಿ → goes

ತಂ ದೇವಾಃ ಸರ್ವೇ ಅರ್ಪಿತಾಃ

Upon whom all the deities are established

- ತಂ → in Him
- ದೇವಾಃ → the deities
- ಸರ್ವೇ → all
- ಅರ್ಪಿತಾಃ → established, dependent, rooted

ತತ್ ಉ ನ ಅತಿ ಏತಿ ಕಶ್ಚನ

No one goes beyond Him

- ತತ್ → Him
- ಉ → indeed
- ನ → not
- ಅತಿ ಏತಿ → transcends, goes beyond
- ಕಶ್ಚನ → anyone

ಏತತ್ ವೈ ತತ್

Verily, this is That

- ಏತತ್ → this
- ವೈ → indeed
- ತತ್ → That (Brahman)

## ಭಾಷ್ಯ

<div class="bhasya">
ಕಿಂಚ, ಯತಶ್ಚ ಯಸ್ಮಾತ್ಪ್ರಾಣಾತ್ ಉದೇತಿ ಉತ್ತಿಷ್ಠತಿ ಸೂರ್ಯಃ, ಅಸ್ತಂ ನಿಮ್ಲೋಚನಂ ತಿರೋಧಾನಂ ಯತ್ರ ಯಸ್ಮಿನ್ನೇವ ಚ ಪ್ರಾಣೇ ಅಹನ್ಯಹನಿ ಗಚ್ಛತಿ, ತಂ ಪ್ರಾಣಮಾತ್ಮಾನಂ ದೇವಾಃ ಸರ್ವೇ ಅಗ್ನ್ಯಾದಯಃ ಅಧಿದೈವಂ ವಾಗಾದಯಶ್ಚಾಧ್ಯಾತ್ಮಂ ಸರ್ವೇ ವಿಶ್ವೇ ಅರಾ ಇವ ರಥನಾಭೌ ಅರ್ಪಿತಾಃ ಸಂಪ್ರವೇಶಿತಾಃ ಸ್ಥಿತಿಕಾಲೇ । ಸೋಽಪಿ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮೈವ । ತತ್ ಏತತ್ಸರ್ವಾತ್ಮಕಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ, ತದು ನಾತ್ಯೇತಿ ನಾತೀತ್ಯ ತದಾತ್ಮಕತಾಂ ತದನ್ಯತ್ವಂ ಗಚ್ಛತಿ, ಕಶ್ಚನ ಕಶ್ಚಿದಪಿ ಏತದ್ವೈ ತತ್ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
Further, from that Prāṇa the Sun rises and becomes manifest; and into that very Prāṇa it sets and disappears day after day.

Upon that Prāṇa, which is the Self, all the deities such as Agni in the cosmic sphere, and speech and the other faculties in the individual sphere, are established. They are fixed in it just as the spokes of a wheel are fixed in the hub.

That Prāṇa is itself Brahman.

This Brahman is the Self of all. No one can go beyond It. No one can transcend Its nature and become something other than It, for there exists nothing apart from It.

Verily, this is That.
</div>

### ಭಾಷ್ಯ ಪದಾರ್ಥ

ಯತಃ ಚ ಯಸ್ಮಾತ್ ಪ್ರಾಣಾತ್

From that Prāṇa

- ಯತಃ → from whom
- ಚ → and
- ಯಸ್ಮಾತ್ → from which
- ಪ್ರಾಣಾತ್ → from Prāṇa

ಉದೇತಿ ಉತ್ತಿಷ್ಠತಿ ಸೂರ್ಯಃ

The Sun rises and becomes manifest

- ಉದೇತಿ → rises
- ಉತ್ತಿಷ್ಠತಿ → appears, becomes manifest
- ಸೂರ್ಯಃ → the Sun

ಅಸ್ತಂ ನಿಮ್ಲೋಚನಂ ತಿರೋಧಾನಂ

Setting and disappearance

- ಅಸ್ತಂ → setting
- ನಿಮ್ಲೋಚನಂ → disappearance below the horizon
- ತಿರೋಧಾನಂ → concealment, withdrawal

ಯತ್ರ ಯಸ್ಮಿನ್ ಏವ ಚ ಪ್ರಾಣೇ

Into that very Prāṇa

- ಯತ್ರ → into which
- ಯಸ್ಮಿನ್ → in which
- ಏವ → indeed
- ಚ → and
- ಪ್ರಾಣೇ → in Prāṇa

ಅಹನಿ ಅಹನಿ ಗಚ್ಛತಿ

It goes day after day

- ಅಹನಿ ಅಹನಿ → day after day
- ಗಚ್ಛತಿ → goes

ತಂ ಪ್ರಾಣಂ ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ

That Prāṇa which is the Self

- ತಂ → that
- ಪ್ರಾಣಂ → Prāṇa
- ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ → the Self

ದೇವಾಃ ಸರ್ವೇ ಅಗ್ನ್ಯಾದಯಃ ಅಧಿದೈವಂ

All deities such as Agni in the cosmic sphere

- ದೇವಾಃ → deities
- ಸರ್ವೇ → all
- ಅಗ್ನ್ಯಾದಯಃ → Agni and others
- ಅಧಿದೈವಂ → in the cosmic aspect

ವಾಗಾದಯಃ ಚ ಅಧ್ಯಾತ್ಮಂ

And speech and the rest in the individual sphere

- ವಾಕ್ → speech
- ಆದಯಃ → and others
- ಅಧ್ಯಾತ್ಮಂ → in the individual aspect

ಸರ್ವೇ ವಿಶ್ವೇ

All of them entirely

- ಸರ್ವೇ → all
- ವಿಶ್ವೇ → entirely, universally

ಅರಾಃ ಇವ ರಥನಾಭೌ

Like spokes in the hub of a wheel

- ಅರಾಃ → spokes
- ಇವ → like
- ರಥ → chariot
- ನಾಭೌ → in the hub (where the axle fits)

ಅರ್ಪಿತಾಃ

Are fixed and established

- ಅರ್ಪಿತಾಃ → placed, fixed, established

ಸಂಪ್ರವೇಶಿತಾಃ ಸ್ಥಿತಿಕಾಲೇ

Established therein during their existence

- ಸಂಪ್ರವೇಶಿತಾಃ → entered into, established
- ಸ್ಥಿತಿಕಾಲೇ → during the period of existence

ಸಃ ಅಪಿ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ ಏವ

That indeed is Brahman

- ಸಃ → that
- ಅಪಿ → indeed
- ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ → Brahman
- ಏವ → alone

ತತ್ ಏತತ್ ಸರ್ವಾತ್ಮಕಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ

This Brahman is the Self of all

- ತತ್ ಏತತ್ → this indeed
- ಸರ್ವಾತ್ಮಕಂ → of the nature of all selves
- ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ → Brahman

ತತ್ ಉ ನ ಅತೀಯತಿ

No one transcends It

- ತತ್ → It
- ಉ → indeed
- ನ → not
- ಅತೀಯತಿ → goes beyond

ನ ಅತೀತ್ಯ ತದಾತ್ಮಕತಾಂ

One cannot go beyond one's identity with It

- ನ → not
- ಅತೀತ್ಯ → transcending
- ತದಾತ್ಮಕತಾಂ → Its very nature

ತದನ್ಯತ್ವಂ ಗಚ್ಛತಿ

Nor become something different from It

- ತತ್ → from That
- ಅನ್ಯತ್ವಂ → otherness
- ಗಚ್ಛತಿ → attains

ಕಶ್ಚನ ಕಶ್ಚಿತ್ ಅಪಿ

No one whatsoever

- ಕಶ್ಚನ → anyone
- ಕಶ್ಚಿತ್ ಅಪಿ → whatsoever

ಏತದ್ವೈ ತತ್

Verily, this is That

- ಏತತ್ → this
- ವೈ → indeed
- ತತ್ → That (Brahman)
