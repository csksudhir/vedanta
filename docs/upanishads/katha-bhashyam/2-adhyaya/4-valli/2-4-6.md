# Kaṭha Upaniṣad 2.4.6

## ಮಂತ್ರ

<div class="mantra">
ಯಃ ಪೂರ್ವಂ ತಪಸೋ ಜಾತಮದ್ಭ್ಯಃ ಪೂರ್ವಮಜಾಯತ ।
ಗುಹಾಂ ಪ್ರವಿಶ್ಯ ತಿಷ್ಠಂತಂ ಯೋ ಭೂತೇಭಿರ್ವ್ಯಪಶ್ಯತ । ಏತದ್ವೈ ತತ್ ॥ ೬ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
He who sees That which was born from tapas in the beginning, which came into being before the waters, and which, having entered the cave of the heart, abides there together with the elements—he indeed realizes That. Verily, this is That.
</div>

## ಭಾಷ್ಯ

<div class="bhasya">
ಯಃ ಪ್ರತ್ಯಗಾತ್ಮೇಶ್ವರಭಾವೇನ ನಿರ್ದಿಷ್ಟಃ, ಸ ಸರ್ವಾತ್ಮೇತ್ಯೇತದ್ದರ್ಶಯತಿ — ಯಃ ಕಶ್ಚಿನ್ಮುಮುಕ್ಷುಃ ಪೂರ್ವಂ ಪ್ರಥಮಂ ತಪಸಃ ಜ್ಞಾನಾದಿಲಕ್ಷಣಾದ್ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಣ ಇತ್ಯೇತತ್ ; ಜಾತಮ್ ಉತ್ಪನ್ನಂ ಹಿರಣ್ಯಗರ್ಭಮ್ । ಕಿಮಪೇಕ್ಷ್ಯ ಪೂರ್ವಮಿತಿ, ಆಹ — ಅದ್ಭ್ಯಃ ಪೂರ್ವಮ್ ಅಪ್ಸಹಿತೇಭ್ಯಃ ಪಂಚಭೂತೇಭ್ಯಃ, ನ ಕೇವಲಾಭ್ಯೋಽದ್ಭ್ಯ ಇತ್ಯಭಿಪ್ರಾಯಃ । ಅಜಾಯತ ಉತ್ಪನ್ನಃ ಯಸ್ತಂ ಪ್ರಥಮಜಂ ದೇವಾದಿಶರೀರಾಣ್ಯುತ್ಪಾದ್ಯ ಸರ್ವಪ್ರಾಣಿಗುಹಾಂ ಹೃದಯಾಕಾಶಂ ಪ್ರವಿಶ್ಯ ತಿಷ್ಠಂತಂ ಶಬ್ದಾದೀನುಪಲಭಮಾನಂ ಭೂತೇಭಿಃ ಭೂತೈಃ ಕಾರ್ಯಕರಣಲಕ್ಷಣೈಃ ಸಹ ತಿಷ್ಠಂತಂ ಯೋ ವ್ಯಪಶ್ಯತ ಯಃ ಪಶ್ಯತೀತ್ಯೇತತ್ ; ಯ ಏವಂ ಪಶ್ಯತಿ, ಸ ಏತದೇವ ಪಶ್ಯತಿ — ಯತ್ತತ್ಪ್ರಕೃತಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮ ॥
</div>

<div class="translation">
The Self that was previously described as the inner ruler (Pratyagātman and Īśvara) is now shown to be the Self of all.

Whoever is a seeker of liberation and realizes Hiraṇyagarbha, the first-born, who arose from Brahman characterized by tapas, knowledge, and the like, and who was born before the waters—that is, before the five elements beginning with water—such a person truly sees.

Having first produced the bodies of the gods and other beings, that Hiraṇyagarbha entered the heart-space, the cave of all living beings, and remains there, experiencing sound and other sense objects, together with the body, senses, and mind.

Whoever sees Him in this manner truly sees. Such a person sees none other than the Brahman that is the subject under discussion in this section.
</div>

### ಭಾಷ್ಯ ಪದಾರ್ಥ
