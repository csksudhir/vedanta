    ಪ್ರಶ್ನೋಪನಿಷತ್ — ದ್ವಿತೀಯ ಪ್ರಶ್ನ, ಮಂತ್ರ ೨

    Praśna Upaniṣad — Second Question, Verse 2

    Source of Ṭīkā: Ānandāśrama Sanskrit Series No. 8 (Ānandagiri on Śaṅkara Bhāṣya)

    ಮೂಲ (Mūla)

    ತಸ್ಮೈ ಸ ಹೋವಾಚ । ಆಕಾಶೋ ಹ ವಾ ಏಷ ದೇವೋ ವಾಯುರಗ್ನಿರಾಪಃ ಪೃಥಿವೀ ವಾಙ್ಮನಶ್ಚಕ್ಷುಃ ಶ್ರೋತ್ರಂ ಚ ।

    ತೇ ಪ್ರಕಾಶ್ಯ ಅಭಿವದಂತಿ ವಯಮೇತದ್ಬಾಣಮವಷ್ಟಭ್ಯ ವಿಧಾರಯಾಮಃ ॥ ೨ ॥

    Overall Translation

    To him (Bhārgava) the sage replied. Ākāśa, Vāyu, Fire, Water, Earth — these are the gods; and speech, mind, eye, and ear (are also gods).

    They, having illumined their greatness, declare: "We sustain this body (bāṇa) by holding it firmly."

    Word-to-Word

    ತಸ್ಮೈ — to him · ಸಃ ಹ ಉವಾಚ — he indeed said · ಆಕಾಶಃ — ākāśa (space) · ಹ ವಾ — indeed · ಏಷಃ — this · ದೇವಃ — god / divine power · ವಾಯುಃ — wind / air · ಅಗ್ನಿಃ — fire · ಆಪಃ — water · ಪೃಥಿವೀ — earth · ವಾಕ್ — speech · ಮನಃ — mind · ಚಕ್ಷುಃ — eye · ಶ್ರೋತ್ರಂ ಚ — and ear ।

    ತೇ — they · ಪ್ರಕಾಶ್ಯ — having illumined / made manifest their greatness · ಅಭಿವದಂತಿ — declare · ವಯಂ — we · ಏತತ್ — this · ಬಾಣಂ — body (bāṇa) · ಅವಷ್ಟಭ್ಯ — having held firmly · ವಿಧಾರಯಾಮಃ — we sustain ॥

    ಭಾಷ್ಯ (Bhāṣya)

    ಏವಂ ಪೃಷ್ಟವತೇ ತಸ್ಮೈ ಸ ಹ ಉವಾಚ — ಆಕಾಶಃ ಹ ವೈ ಏಷಃ ದೇವಃ ವಾಯುಃ ಅಗ್ನಿಃ ಆಪಃ ಪೃಥಿವೀ ಇತ್ಯೇತಾನಿ ಪಂಚ ಮಹಾಭೂತಾನಿ ಶರೀರಾರಂಭಕಾಣಿ ವಾಙ್ಮನಶ್ಚಕ್ಷುಃ ಶ್ರೋತ್ರಂ ಇತಿ ಆದೀನಿ ಕರ್ಮೇಂದ್ರಿಯ-ಬುದ್ಧೀಂದ್ರಿಯಾಣಿ ಚ ।

    ಕಾರ್ಯಲಕ್ಷಣಾಃ ಕರಣಲಕ್ಷಣಾಃ ಚ ತೇ ದೇವಾಃ ಆತ್ಮನಃ ಮಾಹಾತ್ಮ್ಯಂ ಪ್ರಕಾಶ್ಯಂ ಪ್ರಕಾಶ್ಯ ಅಭಿವದಂತಿ ಸ್ಪರ್ಧಮಾನಾಃ ಅಹಂಶ್ರೇಷ್ಠತಾಯೈ ।

    ಕಥಂ ವದಂತಿ ? ವಯಂ ಏತತ್ ಬಾಣಂ ಕಾರ್ಯಕರಣಸಂಘಾತಂ ಅವಷ್ಟಭ್ಯ ಪ್ರಾಸಾದಂ ಇವ ಸ್ತಂಭಾದಯಃ ಅವಿಶಿಥಿಲೀಕೃತ್ಯ ವಿಧಾರಯಾಮಃ ।

    ಮಯಾ ಏವ ಏಕೇನ ಅಯಂ ಸಂಘಾತಃ ಧ್ರಿಯತೇ ಇತಿ ಏಕೈಕಸ್ಯ ಅಭಿಪ್ರಾಯಃ ॥

    Overall Translation

    To him who had asked thus, he (Pippalāda) replied: Ākāśa — this is the god; Vāyu, Agni, Waters, Earth — these five great elements are the initiators of the body; and speech, mind, eye, ear and the rest — these are the cognitive and action faculties.

    Those gods — characterised as effects and as instruments — repeatedly illumining their own greatness, declare while competing for the position of "I am the foremost."

    How do they declare? "We sustain this bāṇa — the aggregate of effects and instruments — having firmly held it without loosening it, like pillars holding a palace." Each one's intent is: "By me alone is this aggregate upheld."

    Word-to-Word

    ಏವಂ ಪೃಷ್ಟವತೇ — to the one who had asked thus · ತಸ್ಮೈ ಸ ಹ ಉವಾಚ — to him he indeed said · ಆಕಾಶಃ ಹ ವೈ ಏಷಃ ದೇವಃ — this ākāśa is indeed the god · ವಾಯುಃ ಅಗ್ನಿಃ ಆಪಃ ಪೃಥಿವೀ — vāyu, fire, water, earth · ಇತ್ಯೇತಾನಿ — these · ಪಂಚ ಮಹಾಭೂತಾನಿ — five great elements · ಶರೀರಾರಂಭಕಾಣಿ — initiators of the body · ವಾಕ್-ಮನಃ-ಚಕ್ಷುಃ-ಶ್ರೋತ್ರಂ — speech, mind, eye, ear · ಇತಿ ಆದೀನಿ — and the rest · ಕರ್ಮೇಂದ್ರಿಯ-ಬುದ್ಧೀಂದ್ರಿಯಾಣಿ ಚ — action and cognitive faculties ।

    ಕಾರ್ಯಲಕ್ಷಣಾಃ — characterised as effects · ಕರಣಲಕ್ಷಣಾಃ ಚ — and as instruments · ತೇ ದೇವಾಃ — those gods · ಆತ್ಮನಃ ಮಾಹಾತ್ಮ್ಯಂ — their own greatness · ಪ್ರಕಾಶ್ಯಂ ಪ್ರಕಾಶ್ಯ — repeatedly illumining · ಅಭಿವದಂತಿ — declare · ಸ್ಪರ್ಧಮಾನಾಃ — competing · ಅಹಂಶ್ರೇಷ್ಠತಾಯೈ — for the position of "I am the foremost" ।

    ಕಥಂ ವದಂತಿ — how do they declare? · ವಯಂ ಏತತ್ — we this · ಬಾಣಂ — body · ಕಾರ್ಯಕರಣಸಂಘಾತಂ — aggregate of effects and instruments · ಅವಷ್ಟಭ್ಯ — having firmly held · ಪ್ರಾಸಾದಂ ಇವ — like a palace · ಸ್ತಂಭಾದಯಃ — pillars and the like · ಅವಿಶಿಥಿಲೀಕೃತ್ಯ — without loosening · ವಿಧಾರಯಾಮಃ — we sustain ।

    ಮಯಾ ಏವ ಏಕೇನ — by me alone · ಅಯಂ ಸಂಘಾತಃ — this aggregate · ಧ್ರಿಯತೇ — is upheld · ಇತಿ — thus · ಏಕೈಕಸ್ಯ ಅಭಿಪ್ರಾಯಃ — is the intent of each one ॥

    ಟೀಕಾ (Ṭīkā)

    ಏಷ ದೇವ ಇತಿ ।

    ವಕ್ಷ್ಯಮಾಣ-ಅಭಿವದನ-ಆದಿ-ವ್ಯವಹಾರ-ಸಿದ್ಧ್ಯರ್ಥಂ ಚೇತನತ್ವಂ ಸಂಭಾವಯಿತುಂ ದೇವ ಇತಿ ವಿಶೇಷಣಂ — "ಅಭಿಮಾನಿ-ವ್ಯಪದೇಶಸ್ತು ವಿಶೇಷಾನುಗತಿಭ್ಯಾಂ" [ಬ್ರ.ಸೂ. ೨।೧।೫] ಇತಿ ನ್ಯಾಯೇನ ಆಕಾಶ-ಆದಿ-ಅಭಿಮಾನಿ-ದೇವತಾ-ಗ್ರಹಣಾರ್ಥಂ ।

    ತತ್ ಚ ವಿಶೇಷಣಂ ವಾಯ್ವಾದಿಷು ಅಪಿ ಸರ್ವತ್ರ ಸಂಬಧ್ಯತೇ ।

    ವಾಗ್ಗ್ರಹಣಂ ಕರ್ಮೇಂದ್ರಿಯ-ಉಪಲಕ್ಷಣಾರ್ಥಂ ಚಕ್ಷುರ್-ಆದಿ-ಗ್ರಹಣಂ ಜ್ಞಾನೇಂದ್ರಿಯ-ಉಪಲಕ್ಷಣಾರ್ಥಂ ಇತಿ ಮತ್ವಾ ಆಹ – ಕರ್ಮೇಂದ್ರಿಯ ಇತಿ ।

    ಕಾರ್ಯಲಕ್ಷಣಾಃ — ಶರೀರಾಕಾರೇಣ ಪರಿಣತಾ ಆಕಾಶಾದಯಃ ।

    ಕರಣಲಕ್ಷಣಾನಿ — ಇಂದ್ರಿಯಾಣಿ ।

    ಮಾಹಾತ್ಮ್ಯಂ ಇತಿ ।

    ಆಕಾಶಾದೀನಾಂ ಅವಕಾಶದಾನ-ಆದಿ-ರೂಪಂ ಶರೀರಧಾರಣ-ಏಕದೇಶ-ಆತ್ಮಕಂ ಪ್ರಕಾಶ್ಯಂ ಸ್ವಕಾರ್ಯಂ ಪ್ರಕಾಶ್ಯ ಸರ್ವಲೋಕ-ಪ್ರಕಟಂ ಯಥಾ ತಥಾ ಕೃತ್ವಾ ಅಭಿವದಂತಿ — ಅಭಿತಃ ಸರ್ವತಃ ಕೃತ್ಸ್ನಂ ಶರೀರಧಾರಣಂ ಪ್ರತ್ಯೇಕಂ ವಯಂ ಏವ ಕುರ್ಮಃ ಇತಿ ವದಂತಿ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ।

    ಬಾಣಂ ಇತಿ ।

    ಬ-ವಯೋಃ ಅಭೇದಾತ್ ವಾತಿ ಕುತ್ಸಿತಂ ಗಂಧಂ ವಹತಿ ಇತಿ ವಾ, ವಿನಾಶಂ ಗಚ್ಛತಿ ಇತಿ ವಾ, ದೇಶಾತ್ ದೇಶಾಂತರಂ ಗಚ್ಛತಿ ಇತಿ ವಾ ಬಣಂ ಬಣಂ ಏವ ಬಾಣಂ ಕಾರ್ಯಕರಣಸಂಘಾತಂ ಶರೀರಂ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ॥ ೨ ॥

    Overall Translation

    On "eṣa deva" (this god): Ānandagiri explains that the qualifier "deva" is used in order to accommodate the conscious entity that makes possible the activities of declaring etc. to be described. By the principle of Brahma Sūtra 2.1.5 — "the designation as presiding deities is on account of particular qualities and their following" — the term indicates the presiding deity of ākāśa and the like. This qualifier applies to vāyu and all the others as well.

    The mention of "speech" (vāk) is an upalakaṣaṇa for all the action faculties; the mention of "eye" and the rest is an upalakaṣaṇa for all the cognitive faculties — Śaṅkara states "karmendriyas" with this understanding.

    Kāryalakṣaṇāḥ — the five elements transformed into the form of the body. Karaṇalakṣaṇāni — the sense faculties (instruments).

    On "māhātmyam": the greatness of ākāśa and the like consists of giving space and the like — one aspect of sustaining the body. Having illumined their own function to all the world, they declare: "We alone, each of us, sustain the entire body from all sides."

    On "bāṇam": three etymologies — (1) carries an unpleasant odour (b/v interchangeable), (2) goes to destruction, (3) moves from place to place. Any way, bāṇa means the aggregate of effects and instruments — the body.

    Word-to-Word

    ಏಷ ದೇವ ಇತಿ — on the words "eṣa deva (this god)" ।

    ವಕ್ಷ್ಯಮಾಣ-ಅಭಿವದನ-ಆದಿ-ವ್ಯವಹಾರ-ಸಿದ್ಧ್ಯರ್ಥಂ — for the purpose of making possible the activity of declaring etc. · ಚೇತನತ್ವಂ ಸಂಭಾವಯಿತುಂ — to accommodate conscious activity · ದೇವ ಇತಿ ವಿಶೇಷಣಂ — the qualifier "deva" · "ಅಭಿಮಾನಿ-ವ್ಯಪದೇಶಃ ತು ವಿಶೇಷಾನುಗತಿಭ್ಯಾಂ" — Brahma Sūtra 2.1.5 · ಇತಿ ನ್ಯಾಯೇನ — by this principle · ಆಕಾಶ-ಆದಿ-ಅಭಿಮಾನಿ-ದೇವತಾ-ಗ್ರಹಣಾರ್ಥಂ — for indicating the presiding deities of ākāśa and the like ।

    ತತ್ ಚ ವಿಶೇಷಣಂ — and that qualifier · ವಾಯ್ವಾದಿಷು ಅಪಿ — in vāyu and the rest · ಸರ್ವತ್ρ ಸಂಬಧ್ಯತೇ — applies throughout ।

    ವಾಗ್ಗ್ರಹಣಂ — mention of speech · ಕರ್ಮೇಂದ್ರಿಯ-ಉಪಲಕ್ಷಣಾರ್ಥಂ — indicative for all action faculties · ಚಕ್ಷುರ್-ಆದಿ-ಗ್ರಹಣಂ — mention of eye and the rest · ಜ್ಞಾನೇಂದ್ರಿಯ-ಉಪಲಕ್ಷಣಾರ್ಥಂ — indicative for all cognitive faculties · ಇತಿ ಮತ್ವಾ ಆಹ — understanding this, states · ಕರ್ಮೇಂದ್ರಿಯ ಇತಿ — with the word "karmendriyas" ।

    ಕಾರ್ಯಲಕ್ಷಣಾಃ — characterised as effects · ಶರೀರಾಕಾರೇಣ ಪರಿಣತಾಃ — transformed into the form of the body · ಆಕಾಶಾದಯಃ — ākāśa and the rest ।

    ಕರಣಲಕ್ಷಣಾನಿ — characterised as instruments · ಇಂದ್ರಿಯಾಣಿ — the faculties ।

    ಮಾಹಾತ್ಮ್ಯಂ ಇತಿ — on "māhātmyam (greatness)" ।

    ಆಕಾಶಾದೀನಾಂ — of ākāśa and the like · ಅವಕಾಶದಾನ-ಆದಿ-ರೂಪಂ — giving space and the like · ಶರೀರಧಾರಣ-ಏಕದೇಶ-ಆತ್ಮಕಂ — one aspect of sustaining the body · ಪ್ರಕಾಶ್ಯಂ ಸ್ವಕಾರ್ಯಂ ಪ್ರಕಾಶ್ಯ — having illumined their own function · ಸರ್ವಲೋಕ-ಪ್ರಕಟಂ — manifest to all the world · ಅಭಿವದಂತಿ — they declare · ಅಭಿತಃ ಸರ್ವತಃ — from all sides · ಕೃತ್ಸ್ನಂ ಶರೀರಧಾರಣಂ — the entire sustaining of the body · ಪ್ರತ್ಯೇಕಂ ವಯಂ ಏವ ಕುರ್ಮಃ — we alone each perform · ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

    ಬಾಣಂ ಇತಿ — on "bāṇam (body)" ।

    ಬ-ವಯೋಃ ಅಭೇದಾತ್ — because b and v are interchangeable · ವಾತಿ ಕುತ್ಸಿತಂ ಗಂಧಂ ವಹತಿ ಇತಿ ವಾ — carries an unpleasant odour · ವಿನಾಶಂ ಗಚ್ಛತಿ ಇತಿ ವಾ — goes to destruction · ದೇಶಾತ್ ದೇಶಾಂತರಂ ಗಚ್ಛತಿ ಇತಿ ವಾ — moves from place to place · ಬಣಂ ಏವ ಬಾಣಂ — bāṇa is simply baṇa · ಕಾರ್ಯಕರಣಸಂಘಾತಂ ಶರೀರಂ — the aggregate of effects and instruments, the body · ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ॥

    Summary of Mūla, Bhāṣya, and Ṭīkā

    Text Author Function
    ಮೂಲ (Mūla) Upaniṣad (Atharva-veda tradition) Lists the gods — five elements and the faculties — and records their claim to be the supreme sustainer of the body
    ಭಾṣya (Bhāṣya) Śaṅkarācārya (8th c. CE) Classifies the gods as kārya (effects = five elements) and karaṇa (instruments = faculties); explains their competition for supremacy; glosses bāṇa as the aggregate body
    ಟೀಕಾ (Ṭīkā) Ānandagiri (13th c. CE) Cites Brahma Sūtra 2.1.5 for the use of "deva"; identifies vāk and cakṣus as upalakaṣaṇas for their respective faculty groups; gives three etymologies of bāṇa
