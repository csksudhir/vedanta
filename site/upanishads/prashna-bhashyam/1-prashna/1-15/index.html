    ಪ್ರಶ್ನೋಪನಿಷತ್ — ಪ್ರಥಮ ಪ್ರಶ್ನ, ಮಂತ್ರ ೧೫

    Praśna Upaniṣad — First Question, Verse 15

    Source of Ṭīkā: Ānandāśrama Sanskrit Series No. 8 (Ānandagiri on Śaṅkara Bhāṣya)

    ಮೂಲ (Mūla)

    ತದ್ಯೇ ಹ ವೈ ತತ್ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿವ್ರತಂ ಚರಂತಿ ತೇ ಮಿಥುನಮುತ್ಪಾದಯಂತೇ ।

    ತೇಷಾಮೇವೈಷ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕೋ ಯೇಷಾಂ ತಪೋ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯಂ ಯೇಷು ಸತ್ಯಂ ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠಿತಮ್ ॥ ೧೫ ॥

    Overall Translation

    Those who observe that vow of Prajāpati — they produce a pair (a son and a daughter).

    This Brahmaloka belongs to those alone in whom is austerity (tapas) and brahmacarya, and in whom truth (satya) is firmly established.

    Word-to-Word

    ತತ್ — therefore / in that case · ಯೇ — those who · ಹ ವೈ — indeed / verily (particles of well-known fact) · ತತ್ — that · ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿವ್ರತಂ — the vow of Prajāpati (approaching one's wife in the proper season) · ಚರಂತಿ — observe / practise · ತೇ — they · ಮಿಥುನಂ — a pair (son and daughter) · ಉತ್ಪಾದಯಂತೇ — produce / beget ।

    ತೇಷಾಂ ಏವ — of those alone · ಏಷಃ — this · ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಃ — the Brahmaloka (the lunar world / the world of Prajāpati) · ಯೇಷಾಂ — in whom · ತಪಃ — austerity · ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯಂ — brahmacarya (celibacy outside the proper season) · ಯೇಷು — in whom · ಸತ್ಯಂ — truth · ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠಿತಂ — firmly established ॥

    ಭಾಷ್ಯ (Bhāṣya)

    ತತ್ ತತ್ರ ಏವಂ ಸತಿ ಯೇ ಗೃಹಸ್ಥಾಃ ।

    ಹ ವೈ ಇತಿ ಪ್ರಸಿದ್ಧಸ್ಮರಣಾರ್ಥೌ ನಿಪಾತೌ ।

    ತತ್ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತೇಃ ವ್ರತಂ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿವ್ರತಂ ಋತೌ ಭಾರ್ಯಾಗಮನಂ ಚರಂತಿ ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ, ತೇಷಾಂ ದೃಷ್ಟಂ ಫಲಮಿದಮ್ ।

    ಕಿಮ್ ? ತೇ ಮಿಥುನಂ ಪುತ್ರಂ ದುಹಿತರಂ ಚ ಉತ್ಪಾದಯಂತೇ ।

    ಅದೃಷ್ಟಂ ಚ ಫಲಂ ಇಷ್ಟಾಪೂರ್ತದತ್ತಕಾರಿಣಾಂ ತೇಷಾಂ ಏವ ಏಷಃ ಯಶ್ಚಾಂದ್ರಮಸೋ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಃ ಪಿತೃಯಾಣಲಕ್ಷಣಃ ।

    ಯೇಷಾಂ ತಪಃ ಸ್ನಾತಕವ್ರತಾದಿ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯಂ ಋತೋರನ್ಯತ್ರ ಮೈಥುನಾಸಮಾಚರಣಂ ಯೇಷು ಚ ಸತ್ಯಂ ಅನೃತವರ್ಜನಂ ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠಿತಂ ಅವ್ಯಭಿಚಾರಿತಯಾ ವರ್ತತೇ ನಿತ್ಯಮೇವ ॥

    Overall Translation

    Therefore, in this being so — those householders who observe that vow of Prajāpati. The particles "ha vai" are for the purpose of recalling what is well-known. The vow of Prajāpati — which is the vow of approaching one's wife in the proper season — they observe, they perform. This is the visible (dṛṣṭa) fruit for them.

    What is it? They produce a pair — a son and a daughter.

    And the invisible (adṛṣṭa) fruit — for those same persons who perform iṣṭa (sacrifice), pūrta (charity), and datta (giving) — is this very Brahmaloka which is cāndramasa (of the Moon), characterised by the Pitṛyāṇa.

    This belongs to those in whom austerity (tapas) is the snataka-vrata (the vow of the bath-graduate) and the like; in whom brahmacarya is the non-performance of intercourse outside the proper season; and in whom truth (satya) — the avoidance of falsehood — is firmly established, operating without deviation, always.

    Word-to-Word

    ತತ್ — therefore · ತತ್ρ — there / in that case · ಏವಂ ಸತಿ — this being so · ಯೇ ಗೃಹಸ್ಥಾಃ — those householders ।

    ಹ ವೈ ಇತಿ — the particles "ha" and "vai" · ಪ್ರಸಿದ್ಧ-ಸ್ಮರಣ-ಅರ್ಥೌ — for the purpose of recalling what is well-known · ನಿಪಾತೌ — are indeclinable particles ।

    ತತ್ — that · ಪ್ರಜಾಪತೇಃ ವ್ರತಂ — the vow of Prajāpati · ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿವ್ರತಂ — the Prajāpati-vow · ಋತೌ — in the proper season · ಭಾರ್ಯಾಗಮನಂ — approaching one's wife · ಚರಂತಿ — they observe · ಕುರ್ವಂತಿ — they perform · ತೇಷಾಂ — for them · ದೃಷ್ಟಂ ಫಲಂ — the visible / this-worldly fruit · ಇದಂ — this ।

    ಕಿಮ್ — what (is it)? · ತೇ — they · ಮಿಥುನಂ — a pair · ಪುತ್ರಂ — a son · ದುಹಿತರಂ ಚ — and a daughter · ಉತ್ಪಾದಯಂತೇ — produce / beget ।

    ಅದೃಷ್ಟಂ ಚ ಫಲಂ — and the invisible / other-worldly fruit · ಇಷ್ಟಾಪೂರ್ತ-ದತ್ತ-ಕಾರಿಣಾಂ — of those who perform sacrifice, charity, and giving · ತೇಷಾಂ ಏವ — of those very persons · ಏಷಃ — this · ಯಃ — which · ಚಾಂದ್ರಮಸಃ — of the Moon / lunar · ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಃ — the Brahmaloka · ಪಿತೃಯಾಣಲಕ್ಷಣಃ — characterised by the Pitṛyāṇa path ।

    ಯೇಷಾಂ — in whom · ತಪಃ — austerity · ಸ್ನಾತಕವ್ರತಾದಿ — the snataka-vrata and the like · ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯಂ — brahmacarya · ಋತೋಃ ಅನ್ಯತ್ರ — outside the proper season · ಮೈಥುನ-ಅಸಮಾಚರಣಂ — non-performance of intercourse · ಯೇಷು ಚ — and in whom · ಸತ್ಯಂ — truth · ಅನೃತ-ವರ್ಜನಂ — avoidance of falsehood · ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠಿತಂ — firmly established · ಅವ್ಯಭಿಚಾರಿತಯಾ — without deviation / unfailingly · ವರ್ತತೇ — operates / abides · ನಿತ್ಯಂ ಏವ — always / perpetually ॥

    Anvaya (Prose Order of the Bhāṣya)

    One sentence in this Bhāṣya requires explicit anvaya treatment — the long relative clause describing who attains the Brahmaloka.

    Sentence — The conditions for attaining the cāndramasa Brahmaloka

    As written:

    ಅದೃಷ್ಟಂ ಚ ಫಲಂ ಇಷ್ಟಾಪೂರ್ತದತ್ತಕಾರಿಣಾಂ ತೇಷಾಂ ಏವ ಏಷಃ ಯಶ್ಚಾಂದ್ರಮಸೋ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಃ ಪಿತೃಯಾಣಲಕ್ಷಣಃ ।

    ಯೇಷಾಂ ತಪಃ ಸ್ನಾತಕವ್ರತಾದಿ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯಂ ಋತೋರನ್ಯತ್ರ ಮೈಥುನಾಸಮಾಚರಣಂ ಯೇಷು ಚ ಸತ್ಯಂ ಅನೃತವರ್ಜನಂ ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠಿತಂ ।

    Anvaya (resolved prose order):

    [ಗೃಹಸ್ಥಾನಾಂ] ಅದೃಷ್ಟಂ ಫಲಂ [ಕಃ ?] — ಏಷಃ ಚಾಂದ್ರಮಸಃ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಃ ಪಿತೃಯಾಣಲಕ್ಷಣಃ [ಅಸ್ತಿ] ।

    [ಕೇಷಾಂ ?] ತೇಷಾಂ ಏವ — ಯೇ ಇಷ್ಟಾಪೂರ್ತದತ್ತ-ಕಾರಿಣಃ [ಸಂತಿ] ಯೇಷಾಂ ತಪಃ [ಅಸ್ತಿ] ಯೇಷಾಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚರ್ಯಂ [ಅಸ್ತಿ] ಯೇಷು ಸತ್ಯಂ ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠಿತಂ [ಅಸ್ತಿ] ।

    English: What is the invisible fruit for householders? It is this very lunar Brahmaloka characterised by the Pitṛyāṇa. And for whom? For those very persons who perform iṣṭa, pūrta, and datta — in whom is austerity, in whom is brahmacarya, and in whom truth is firmly established.

    Note: Śaṅkara adds three qualifications — tapas, brahmacarya, satya — beyond the mere performance of the Prajāpati-vow. This is the key point that Ānandagiri flags in the Ṭīkā: the Brahmaloka is not attained by the Prajāpati-vow alone. It requires iṣṭa-pūrta-datta in addition, plus these three inner qualifications. This prevents the wrong conclusion that any householder who observes the proper-season rule automatically attains the lunar Brahmaloka.

    ಟೀಕಾ (Ṭīkā)

    ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿವ್ರತಾಚರಣಮಾತ್ರೇಣ ನಾದೃಷ್ಟಫಲಂ ಚಂದ್ರಲೋಕಃ ಪ್ರಾಪ್ಯತೇ ಮೂರ್ಖಾಣಾಮಪಿ ಪ್ರಸಂಗಾತ್ ಅತ ಆಹ – ಇಷ್ಟಾಪೂರ್ತ ಇತಿ ।

    ಚಾಂದ್ರಮಸೋ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕ ಇತಿ ।

    ಅಪರಬ್ರಹ್ಮಣಃ ಪ್ರಜಾಪತೇಃ ಅಂಶತ್ವಾತ್ ರಯಿರೂಪಸ್ಯ ಚಂದ್ರಸ್ಯ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕತ್ವಂ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ।

    ಇಷ್ಟಾದಿಕಾರಿಣಾಂ ತಪಆದಿಕಮಪಿ ಚಂದ್ರಲೋಕಪ್ರಾಪ್ತ್ಯರ್ಥಂ ಅಪೇಕ್ಷಿತಂ ಇತ್ಯತ ಆಹ – ಯೇಷಾಂ ತಪ ಇತಿ ॥ ೧೫ ॥

    Overall Translation

    Ānandagiri opens with a crucial clarification: the lunar world (Brahmaloka) as an invisible (adṛṣṭa) fruit is not attained merely by observing the Prajāpati-vow (approaching one's wife in the proper season) alone. If it were, even fools who happen to observe the rule would attain it — an absurd consequence (prasaṅga). Therefore Śaṅkara adds "iṣṭāpūrta" — with the words "iṣṭāpūrta."

    On "cāndramaso brahmaloka" (the lunar Brahmaloka): Ānandagiri explains that the Moon — which is of the nature of Rayi — being a portion of Aparabrahman (the lower Brahman, Prajāpati), qualifies as a Brahmaloka. It is called Brahmaloka because it is a portion of Prajāpati who is Aparabrahman — not because it is the supreme Brahman.

    On "yeṣāṃ tapas" (in whom is austerity): Ānandagiri states that even for those who perform iṣṭa and the like, the austerity and the other qualifications (tapas, brahmacarya, satya) are also required for attaining the lunar world. Therefore Śaṅkara states these separately — with the words "yeṣāṃ tapas."

    Word-to-Word

    ಪ್ರಜಾಪತಿವ್ರತ-ಆಚರಣ-ಮಾತ್ರೇಣ — by the mere observance of the Prajāpati-vow · ನ — not · ಅದೃಷ್ಟ-ಫಲಂ — the invisible / other-worldly fruit · ಚಂದ್ರಲೋಕಃ — the lunar world · ಪ್ರಾಪ್ಯತೇ — is attained · ಮೂರ್ಖಾಣಾಂ ಅಪಿ — even of fools · ಪ್ರಸಂಗಾತ್ — because of the absurd consequence · ಅತಃ — therefore · ಆಹ — (Śaṅkara) states · ಇಷ್ಟಾಪೂರ್ತ ಇತಿ — with the word "iṣṭāpūrta" ।

    ಚಾಂದ್ರಮಸಃ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಃ ಇತಿ — on the phrase "the lunar Brahmaloka" ।

    ಅಪರ-ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಣಃ — of Aparabrahman (the lower Brahman) · ಪ್ರಜಾಪತೇಃ — of Prajāpati · ಅಂಶತ್ವಾತ್ — because of being a portion · ರಯಿರೂಪಸ್ಯ — which is of the nature of Rayi · ಚಂದ್ರಸ್ಯ — of the Moon · ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕತ್ವಂ — the quality of being a Brahmaloka · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

    ಇಷ್ಟ-ಆದಿ-ಕಾರಿಣಾಂ — for those who perform iṣṭa and the like · ತಪ-ಆದಿಕಂ ಅಪಿ — even austerity and the rest · ಚಂದ್ರಲೋಕ-ಪ್ರಾಪ್ತ್ಯರ್ಥಂ — for the purpose of attaining the lunar world · ಅಪೇಕ್ಷಿತಂ — is required / needed · ಇತ್ಯತಃ ಆಹ — therefore (Śaṅkara) states · ಯೇಷಾಂ ತಪಃ ಇತಿ — with the words "yeṣāṃ tapas (in whom is austerity)" ॥

    Summary of Mūla, Bhāṣya, and Ṭīkā

    Text Author Function
    ಮೂಲ (Mūla) Upaniṣad (Atharva-veda tradition) States the visible fruit of the Prajāpati-vow (a son and daughter) and the conditions for the invisible fruit — the Brahmaloka — namely tapas, brahmacarya, and truth
    ಭಾಷ್ಯ (Bhāṣya) Śaṅkarācārya (8th c. CE) Defines the Prajāpati-vow precisely as approaching one's wife in the proper season; distinguishes visible (dṛṣṭa) and invisible (adṛṣṭa) fruits; adds iṣṭa-pūrta-datta as conditions for the Brahmaloka; defines tapas, brahmacarya, and satya specifically for a householder
    ಟೀಕಾ (Ṭīkā) Ānandagiri (13th c. CE) Prevents the wrong conclusion that the Prajāpati-vow alone yields the Brahmaloka; explains the lunar world's title "Brahmaloka" as arising from the Moon being a portion of Prajāpati who is Aparabrahman; clarifies that tapas etc. are additional requirements even for those who perform iṣṭa

    Core Philosophical Thread

    1. Two fruits are distinguished — dṛṣṭa (visible, this-worldly: a son and daughter) and adṛṣṭa (invisible, other-worldly: the lunar Brahmaloka). The Prajāpati-vow alone yields only the visible fruit.
    2. The invisible fruit requires more — iṣṭa (sacrifice), pūrta (charity), and datta (giving), plus the inner qualifications of tapas, brahmacarya, and satya. This is the full condition for the cāndramasa Brahmaloka.
    3. The lunar world is called "Brahmaloka" not because it is the supreme Brahman but because the Moon is a portion (aṃśa) of Prajāpati who is Aparabrahman (the lower Brahman). Ānandagiri's clarification here is philosophically important — it prevents confusion between the lunar Brahmaloka and the final liberation.
    4. Tapas for a householder is defined precisely as the snataka-vrata and similar observances — not the extreme austerities of a renunciant. Brahmacarya is defined as not engaging in intercourse outside the proper season. Satya is the unfailing avoidance of falsehood.
    5. Ānandagiri's prasaṅga argument — that fools too would attain the Brahmaloka if the vow alone were sufficient — is a classic reductio ad absurdum used to establish that additional qualifications are necessary.
