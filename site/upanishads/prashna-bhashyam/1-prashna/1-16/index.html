    ಪ್ರಶ್ನೋಪನಿಷತ್ — ಪ್ರಥಮ ಪ್ರಶ್ನ, ಮಂತ್ರ ೧೬

    Praśna Upaniṣad — First Question, Verse 16

    Source of Ṭīkā: Ānandāśrama Sanskrit Series No. 8 (Ānandagiri on Śaṅkara Bhāṣya)

    ಮೂಲ (Mūla)

    ತೇಷಾಮಸೌ ವಿರಜೋ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕೋ ನ ಯೇಷು ಜಿಹ್ಮಮನೃತಂ ನ ಮಾಯಾ ಚೇತಿ ॥ ೧೬ ॥

    Overall Translation

    That spotless (viraja) Brahmaloka belongs to those in whom there is no crookedness (jihma), no falsehood (anṛta), and no deceit (māyā).

    Word-to-Word

    ತೇಷಾಂ — of those · ಅಸೌ — that · ವಿರಜಃ — spotless / stainless / free from impurity · ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಃ — the Brahmaloka · ನ — not · ಯೇಷು — in whom · ಜಿಹ್ಮಂ — crookedness / deceitfulness · ಅನೃತಂ — falsehood · ನ — not · ಮಾಯಾ — deceit / dissimulation / showing oneself as other than one is · ಚ — and · ಇತಿ — thus ॥

    ಭಾಷ್ಯ (Bhāṣya)

    ಯಸ್ತು ಪುನಃ ಆದಿತ್ಯೋಪಲಕ್ಷಿತ ಉತ್ತರಾಯಣಃ ಪ್ರಾಣಾತ್ಮಭಾವಃ ವಿರಜಃ ಶುದ್ಧಃ ನ ಚಂದ್ರಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕವತ್ ರಜಸ್ವಲಃ ವೃದ್ಧಿಕ್ಷಯಾದಿಯುಕ್ತಃ ಅಸೌ ತೇಷಾಮ್ ।

    ಕೇಷಾಂ ಇತಿ ಉಚ್ಯತೇ — ಯಥಾ ಗೃಹಸ್ಥಾನಾಂ ಅನೇಕವಿರುದ್ಧಸಂವ್ಯವಹಾರಪ್ರಯೋಜನವತ್ತ್ವಾತ್ ಜಿಹ್ಮಂ ಕೌಟಿಲ್ಯಂ ವಕ್ರಭಾವಃ ಅವಶ್ಯಂಭಾವಿ ತಥಾ ನ ಯೇಷು ಜಿಹ್ಮಮ್ ।

    ಯಥಾ ಚ ಗೃಹಸ್ಥಾನಾಂ ಕ್ರೀಡಾದಿನಿಮಿತ್ತಂ ಅನೃತಂ ಅವರ್ಜನೀಯಂ ತಥಾ ನ ಯೇಷು ತತ್ ।

    ತಥಾ ಮಾಯಾ ಗೃಹಸ್ಥಾನಾಮಿವ ನ ಯೇಷು ವಿದ್ಯತೇ ।

    ಮಾಯಾ ನಾಮ ಬಹಿಃ ಅನ್ಯಥಾ ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ ಪ್ರಕಾಶ್ಯ ಅನ್ಯಥಾ ಏವ ಕಾರ್ಯಂ ಕರೋತಿ, ಸಾ ಮಾಯಾ ಮಿಥ್ಯಾಚಾರರೂಪಾ ।

    ಮಾಯಾ ಇತಿ ಏವಂ ಆದಯಃ ದೋಷಾಃ ಯೇಷು ಏಕಾಕಿಷು ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚಾರಿ-ವಾನಪ್ರಸ್ಥ-ಭಿಕ್ಷುಷು ನಿಮಿತ್ತಾಭಾವಾತ್ ನ ವಿದ್ಯಂತೇ, ತತ್ಸಾಧನಾನುರೂಪ್ಯೇಣ ಏವ ತೇಷಾಂ ಅಸೌ ವಿರಜಃ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಃ ।

    ಇತಿ ಏಷಾ ಜ್ಞಾನಯುಕ್ತಕರ್ಮವತಾಂ ಗತಿಃ ।

    ಪೂರ್ವೋಕ್ತಃ ತು ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಃ ಕೇವಲಕರ್ಮಿಣಾಂ ಚಂದ್ರಲಕ್ಷಣಃ ॥

    Overall Translation

    But that (Brahmaloka) which is indicated by the Sun — the Northern solstice — the state of being the Self of Prāṇa: that is spotless (viraja), pure — unlike the lunar Brahmaloka which is tainted (rajasvalā), subject to waxing and waning and the like. That belongs to those — to whom? It is said:

    Just as crookedness (jihma) — deviousness, a bent disposition — is unavoidable for householders because they are engaged in many conflicting worldly transactions — so (this viraja Brahmaloka belongs to) those in whom there is no such crookedness.

    And just as falsehood, arising from occasions like play and entertainment, is unavoidable for householders — so (it belongs to) those in whom there is no such falsehood.

    And just as deceit (māyā) exists in householders — so (it belongs to) those in whom it does not exist.

    Māyā means: outwardly presenting oneself as one thing while inwardly acting in a different way altogether — that māyā which is of the nature of duplicitous conduct.

    These faults — māyā and the like — do not exist in those who live alone: brahmacarins, forest-dwellers (vānaprasthas), and mendicants (bhikṣus) — because the occasions / causes (for these faults) are absent. Precisely because their means (sādhana) are in keeping with this (viraja) world — that spotless Brahmaloka belongs to them.

    This is the path / destination of those who combine knowledge with action (jñānayuktakarmavants). The Brahmaloka mentioned earlier, however — the lunar one — is for those who perform only ritual action (kevalakarmins).

    Word-to-Word

    ಯಃ ತು ಪುನಃ — but that which · ಆದಿತ್ಯೋಪಲಕ್ಷಿತಃ — indicated by the Sun · ಉತ್ತರಾಯಣಃ — the Northern solstice · ಪ್ರಾಣಾತ್ಮಭಾವಃ — the state of being the Self of Prāṇa · ವಿರಜಃ — spotless / stainless · ಶುದ್ಧಃ — pure · ನ — not · ಚಂದ್ರಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕವತ್ — like the lunar Brahmaloka · ರಜಸ್ವಲಃ — tainted / impure · ವೃದ್ಧಿಕ್ಷಯಾದಿಯುಕ್ತಃ — subject to waxing and waning and the like · ಅಸೌ — that · ತೇಷಾಂ — belongs to those ।

    ಕೇಷಾಂ — of whom? · ಇತಿ ಉಚ್ಯತೇ — it is said · ಯಥಾ — just as · ಗೃಹಸ್ಥಾನಾಂ — for householders · ಅನೇಕ-ವಿರುದ್ಧ-ಸಂವ್ಯವಹಾರ-ಪ್ರಯೋಜನವತ್ತ್ವಾತ್ — because of being engaged in many conflicting worldly transactions · ಜಿಹ್ಮಂ — crookedness · ಕೌಟಿಲ್ಯಂ — deviousness · ವಕ್ರಭಾವಃ — a bent / crooked disposition · ಅವಶ್ಯಂಭಾವಿ — unavoidable · ತಥಾ — so (it belongs to those) · ನ ಯೇಷು ಜಿಹ್ಮಂ — in whom there is no such crookedness ।

    ಯಥಾ ಚ — and just as · ಗೃಹಸ್ಥಾನಾಂ — for householders · ಕ್ರೀಡಾದಿ-ನಿಮಿತ್ತಂ — arising from occasions like play and entertainment · ಅನೃತಂ — falsehood · ಅವರ್ಜನೀಯಂ — unavoidable / impossible to avoid · ತಥಾ — so · ನ ಯೇಷು ತತ್ — those in whom there is no such falsehood ।

    ತಥಾ — similarly · ಮಾಯಾ — deceit / dissimulation · ಗೃಹಸ್ಥಾನಾಂ ಇವ — as in householders · ನ ಯೇಷು ವಿದ್ಯತೇ — does not exist in whom ।

    ಮಾಯಾ ನಾಮ — māyā is (defined as) · ಬಹಿಃ — outwardly / externally · ಅನ್ಯಥಾ — as one thing / differently · ಆತ್ಮಾನಂ ಪ್ರಕಾಶ್ಯ — presenting oneself · ಅನ್ಯಥಾ ಏವ — in a different way altogether · ಕಾರ್ಯಂ ಕರೋತಿ — acts / does one's work · ಸಾ ಮಾಯಾ — that māyā · ಮಿಥ್ಯಾಚಾರರೂಪಾ — of the nature of duplicitous conduct ।

    ಮಾಯಾ ಇತಿ ಏವಂ ಆದಯಃ — māyā and such like · ದೋಷಾಃ — faults / defects · ಯೇಷು — in whom · ಏಕಾಕಿಷು — those who live alone · ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚಾರಿ — brahmacarins (student celibates) · ವಾನಪ್ರಸ್ಥ — forest-dwellers · ಭಿಕ್ಷುಷು — mendicants · ನಿಮಿತ್ತ-ಅಭಾವಾತ್ — because of the absence of the occasions / causes · ನ ವಿದ್ಯಂತೇ — do not exist · ತತ್ಸಾಧನ-ಅನುರೂಪ್ಯೇಣ ಏವ — precisely because their means (sādhana) are in keeping with it · ತೇಷಾಂ — belongs to them · ಅಸೌ ವಿರಜಃ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಃ — that spotless Brahmaloka ।

    ಇತಿ ಏಷಾ — this is · ಜ್ಞಾನಯುಕ್ತ-ಕರ್ಮವತಾಂ — of those who combine knowledge with action · ಗತಿಃ — the path / destination ।

    ಪೂರ್ವೋಕ್ತಃ ತು — but the one previously mentioned · ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಃ — Brahmaloka · ಕೇವಲಕರ್ಮಿಣಾಂ — of those who perform only ritual action · ಚಂದ್ರಲಕ್ಷಣಃ — characterised by / indicated by the Moon ॥

    Anvaya (Prose Order of the Bhāṣya)

    This Bhāṣya has one complex sentence that requires explicit anvaya — the long relative-clause chain defining the qualifications for the viraja Brahmaloka.

    Sentence — Who attains the viraja Brahmaloka

    As written:

    ಮಾಯಾ ಇತಿ ಏವಂ ಆದಯಃ ದೋಷಾಃ ಯೇಷು ಏಕಾಕಿಷು ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚಾರಿ-ವಾನಪ್ರಸ್ಥ-ಭಿಕ್ಷುಷು ನಿಮಿತ್ತಾಭಾವಾತ್ ನ ವಿದ್ಯಂತೇ, ತತ್ಸಾಧನಾನುರೂಪ್ಯೇಣ ಏವ ತೇಷಾಂ ಅಸೌ ವಿರಜಃ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಃ ।

    Anvaya (resolved prose order):

    ಯೇಷು ಏಕಾಕಿಷು — ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಚಾರಿಷು, ವಾನಪ್ರಸ್ಥೇಷು, ಭಿಕ್ಷುಷು ಚ — ಜಿಹ್ಮ-ಅನೃತ-ಮಾಯಾ-ಆದಯಃ ದೋಷಾಃ ನಿಮಿತ್ತ-ಅಭಾವಾತ್ ನ ವಿದ್ಯಂತೇ — ತೇಷಾಂ ಏವ [ಸಾಧನ-ಅನುರೂಪ್ಯೇಣ] ಅಸೌ ವಿರಜಃ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಃ [ಭವತಿ] ।

    English: In those who live alone — brahmacarins, vānaprasthas, and bhikṣus — the faults of crookedness, falsehood, māyā and the like do not exist, because the occasions / causes for them are absent. Precisely because their means (sādhana) are fitting and in keeping with that (viraja) world — that spotless Brahmaloka belongs to those very persons.

    Note: The causal chain here is: absence of occasions → absence of faults → suitability of sādhana → attainment of viraja Brahmaloka. Ānandagiri flags that the word ಭಿಕ್ಷು here does not include the Paramahaṃsa — the highest renunciant who is beyond even the desire for any Brahmaloka — but refers to the lower orders of mendicancy (kuṭīcaka etc.) who still aspire to worlds.

    ಟೀಕಾ (Ṭīkā)

    ತೇಷಾಮಸೌ ವಿರಜ ಇತ್ಯಾದಿ ವಾಕ್ಯಂ ವ್ಯಾಚಷ್ಟೇ – ಯಸ್ತ್ವಿತಿ ।

    ಉತ್ತರಾಯಣ ಇತಿ ।

    ತೇನ ಪ್ರಾಪ್ಯ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ।

    ಪ್ರಾಣಾತ್ಮಭಾವಃ ಅಪರಬ್ರಹ್ಮತಯಾ ಅವಸ್ಥಾನಂ ಇತ್ಯರ್ಥಃ ।

    ಅಸೌ ಕೇಷಾಂ ತೇಷಾಂ ಇತಿ ।

    ತೇಷಾಮಸೌ ವಿರಜಃ ಇತ್ಯತ್ರ ತೇಷಾಂ ಇತಿ ಅನೇನ ಕೇಷಾಂ ನಿರ್ದೇಶಃ ಇತಿ ಪ್ರಶ್ನಾರ್ಥಃ ।

    ನ ಯೇಷು ಜಿಹ್ಮಂ ಇತ್ಯತ್ರ ಜಿಹ್ಮಾದಿಶಬ್ದಂ ವ್ಯತಿರೇಕಪ್ರದರ್ಶನೇನ ವ್ಯಾಚಷ್ಟೇ – ಯಥ ಇತ್ಯಾದಿನಾ ।

    ಮಾಯಾಗ್ರಹಣಂ ತಾದೃಶಾನಾಂ ದೋಷಾಣಾಂ ಉಪಲಕ್ಷಣಂ ಇತಿ ವದನ್ ವಾಕ್ಯಾರ್ಥಂ ಸಂಗೃಹ್ಯ ದರ್ಶಯತಿ – ಮಾಯಾ ಇತಿ ಏವಮಿತಿ ।

    ಭಿಕ್ಷುಷು ಇತಿ ಪರಮಹಂಸ-ವ್ಯತಿರಿಕ್ತಾನಾಂ ಕುಟೀಚಕಾದೀನಾಂ ಗ್ರಹಣಮ್ ।

    ತೇಷಾಂ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಾದಪಿ ವಿರಕ್ತತ್ವೇನ ತತ್ರ ಅನರ್ಥಿತ್ವಾತ್ ।

    ಇತಿಶಬ್ದಾರ್ಥಂ ಆಹ – ಇತ್ಯೇಷ ಇತಿ ॥ ೧೬ ॥

    Overall Translation

    Ānandagiri opens by saying Śaṅkara explains the sentence "teṣām asau viraja" (that spotless Brahmaloka belongs to those) — with the words "yas tu" (but that which).

    On "uttarāyaṇa" (Northern solstice): Ānandagiri explains this means "attainable through it (the Northern solstice)." And "prāṇātmabhāva" (the state of being the Self of Prāṇa) means abiding as Aparabrahman (the lower Brahman, Prajāpati).

    On "asau keṣāṃ teṣām" (that — of those — to whom): Ānandagiri identifies this as a question being raised — in the sentence "teṣām asau virajaḥ," the word "teṣām" (of those) raises the question: of whom exactly is this a reference?

    On "na yeṣu jihnam" (in whom there is no crookedness): Ānandagiri says Śaṅkara explains the words "jihma" etc. by the method of contrast (vyatireka-pradarśana) — beginning with "yathā" (just as). By showing what these faults are like when present in householders, Śaṅkara makes clear what their absence means.

    On the use of "māyā": Ānandagiri states that the mention of māyā is an upalakaṣaṇa (indicative term) for all such similar faults — and Śaṅkara gathers the full meaning of the sentence and presents it with the words "māyā iti evam ādi" (māyā and the like).

    On "bhikṣuṣu" (mendicants): Ānandagiri clarifies that "bhikṣu" here refers not to the Paramahaṃsa (the highest order of renunciant) but to the lower orders such as the kuṭīcaka and the like. The reason the Paramahaṃsa is excluded is that such persons, being dispassionate (virakta) even towards the Brahmaloka, do not desire it — they are "anarthin" (non-aspirants) for that world.

    Finally, on the meaning of the word "iti" (thus) at the end of the Bhāṣya: this is explained with the words "ity eṣā" — meaning "this is (the path of those who combine knowledge with action)."

    ಉತ್ತರಾಯಣ ಇತಿ — on the word "uttarāyaṇa (Northern solstice)" ।

    ತೇನ — through it / by that (Northern solstice) · ಪ್ರಾಪ್ಯಃ — attainable · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

    ಪ್ರಾಣಾತ್ಮಭಾವಃ — the state of being the Self of Prāṇa · ಅಪರ-ಬ್ರಹ್ಮತಯಾ — as Aparabrahman (the lower Brahman) · ಅವಸ್ಥಾನಂ — abiding / residing · ಇತಿ ಅರ್ಥಃ — this is the meaning ।

    ತೇಷಾಂ — of those (Paramahaṃsas) · ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಲೋಕಾತ್ ಅಪಿ — even towards the Brahmaloka · ವಿರಕ್ತತ್ವೇನ — because of dispassion · ತತ್ρ — there (in the Brahmaloka) · ಅನರ್ಥಿತ್ವಾತ್ — because of being non-aspirants / not desirous of it ।

    ಇತಿಶಬ್ದ-ಅರ್ಥಂ — the meaning of the word "iti (thus)" · ಆಹ — states · ಇತ್ಯೇಷಾ ಇತಿ — with the words "ity eṣā (this is)" ॥

    Summary of Mūla, Bhāṣya, and Ṭīkā

    Text Author Function
    ಮೂಲ (Mūla) Upaniṣad (Atharva-veda tradition) Final verse of the first Praśna — the viraja (spotless) Brahmaloka belongs to those free from crookedness, falsehood, and deceit
    ಭಾಷ್ಯ (Bhāṣya) Śaṅkarācārya (8th c. CE) Identifies the viraja Brahmaloka as the Solar / Northern path; contrasts it with the tainted lunar Brahmaloka; defines jihma, anṛta, and māyā with examples from householder life; identifies its attainers as brahmacarins, vānaprasthas, and bhikṣus; closes with the summary distinction between the two paths
    ಟೀಕಾ (Ṭīkā) Ānandagiri (13th c. CE) Clarifies "prāṇātmabhāva" as abiding as Aparabrahman; explains the vyatireka method of Śaṅkara's definitions; identifies "māyā" as upalakaṣaṇa for all such faults; critically distinguishes the bhikṣu from the Paramahaṃsa — the latter being beyond even the desire for the Brahmaloka

    Core Philosophical Thread

    1. Two Brahmalokasare distinguished — the cāndramasa (lunar, tainted, subject to waxing and waning, for kevalakarmins) stated in verse 15, and the viraja (spotless, pure, Solar, for jñānayuktakarmavants) stated in verse 16. This is the closing structural distinction of the entire first Praśna.
    2. The viraja Brahmaloka is identified with the Solar / Northern path — the state of being the Self of Prāṇa, abiding as Aparabrahman. This is higher than the lunar world but still within the realm of Aparabrahman — not the final liberation.
    3. The three faults — jihma (crookedness), anṛta (falsehood), and māyā (dissimulation) — are defined through contrast with householder life. They are unavoidable for householders because of the many conflicting demands of worldly transactions. They are absent in those who live alone because the occasions for them are absent.
    4. The four āśramas are implied — brahmacarins, vānaprasthas, bhikṣus are named; the gṛhastha (householder) is the implied contrast throughout.
    5. Ānandagiri's exclusion of the Paramahaṃsa is the most philosophically subtle point in this Ṭīkā: the highest renunciant is beyond even the desire for the viraja Brahmaloka — they are anarthin (non-aspirants) for any world. This preserves the hierarchy: kevalakarmins → lunar world; jñānayuktakarmavants → viraja Solar world; jñānins / Paramahaṃsas → liberation beyond worlds.
